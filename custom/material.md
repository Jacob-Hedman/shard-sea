Player-authored custom Unique Materials. Like the other custom content these live outside
the PHB, are re-imported every version, and are tagged Custom (outside the book). Format
matches the PHB: the material's effects follow the Weapons and Armor chapters' *Known
Unique Materials* lists, and its Value follows the Resources chapter's Unique Resources.
