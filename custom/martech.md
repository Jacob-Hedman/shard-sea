Player-authored custom Martech — cybernetic Moduli ("Mods"), surgically implanted and
fuelled by Residue held in Circulation by the User's Marian Core. These live outside the
PHB and are tagged Custom. Format matches the PHB item chapters.

**Installing Martech** — every Mod requires a Marian Core (the only Mod installable with
no pre-requisite), a Trained Surgeon, and about a Week of implantation and recovery. Mods
are fuelled by Residue burned from the User's Core.

	`Activate for 0_AP to gain a Buff to your next Spot Test in the same Round, up to 3 for each 10 Residue spent.`

Mod: **Brights**
	*Tier*: 1| *Cost*: 10,000

	*Tier*: 2| *Cost*: 30,000

Mod: **Slice**

	`Activate for 1_AP to pierce obstructions with the affected eye. This breaches anything with less than 10 Durability and burns 100 Residue per Minute (at least 10 per Activation).`

#### Peripheral Moduli
****
Modifications of no particular system — install any combination.

Mod: **Marian Core**
	*Tier*: 1| *Cost*: 1,000
	`The pre-requisite for all further Moduli. The Core lets the User ingest Residue into their Circulation, where it can be burned by other Mods.`
	*Consumption*
	`Residue is ingested through a port (usually in the chest), able to load very concentrated amounts — potentially millions at a time. Ingesting takes time in Seconds equal to the amount of Residue.`
	*Preservation*
	`Circulating Residue cannot be extracted and is untouched even by Aether Vacuums, remaining until expended.`
	*Dissolution*
	`On the User's death, while Circulating more than 100,000 Residue, they detonate — dealing Explosive Damage in a Radius equal to 0.0001 times the Circulating amount.`

Mod: **Eta Cera**
	*Tier*: 1| *Cost*: 3,000
	`Grants an effective extra (or replacement) limb of any kind, functioning as the original body-part for almost all purposes. Extra arms give more hands, but do not allow installing more Manual Moduli than otherwise possible.`

Mod: **Surge**
	*Tier*: 2| *Cost*: 60,000
	`Burn Residue to shed Strain (no Action required): 30 Residue per Standard, 600 Residue per Persistent.`

Mod: **Physo**
	*Tier*: 3| *Cost*: 300,000
	`Activate as a 1_AP Interaction to instantly end your Bleeding. You may also burn 100 Residue (no Action) to reduce the Healing Time of one of your Injuries by one Day. Fully Healing an Injury with a Physo inflicts one Corruption.`

Mod: **Resera**
	*Tier*: 3| *Cost*: 1,000,000
	`When you would be killed, burn 1,000 Residue to be Incapacitated instead. For 10,000 Residue, ignore an instance of Damage completely.`
