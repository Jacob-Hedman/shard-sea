Origin: Gaea

Player-authored custom Magic Items — Gaea. These are NOT products of Artifice; usually the
work of Arcane Magic or some other means. They live outside the PHB and are tagged Gaea
(their origin) and Custom (outside the book). Format matches the PHB item chapters.

Magic Items can be Repaired like any other, but require expertise (Immanence, or other
familiarity with Magical craft) and Aether up to thrice their Tier (Aetheric Residue may
substitute at 100 to 1).
