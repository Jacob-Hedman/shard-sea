Spirit Binding is a Discipline of the Anya Schools, distinct from Demon Binding. Its Binders
make Pacts with the Spirits of the world — the Mitha and Akthe of the five Courts — gaining
Powers fuelled by Light, a Resource held in Gems and drawn from Wellsprings in the Wilds.

(Homebrew Discipline maintained by the group's GM. The Spirits are catalogued as their own
kind; the Binder's Feats and Rites hang off the Spirit Binding Discipline.)

#### Binding Spirits
****
Spirit Binders make Pacts with the Spirits of the World, giving them Light and Honor in trade
for power. Lesser Spirits (Mitha) want only Light and are limited to twice your Training.
Greater Spirits (Akthe) are Familiars, each with a Bond you must keep or lose their Powers.
Powers are Active (used once), Passive (persist until released; up to your Training), or Innate
(granted by Familiars, free and uncounted). Invoking a Power is 1_AP and costs Light equal to
the Tier chosen, up to thrice your Training. Light is carried in Ampules — Sard (3), Onyx (6),
Sapphire (10), Emerald (30), Ruby (60), Diamond (100) — and costs 10 Gilt per Measure.

Feat 1: *Night Eyes*
	The Sages' Initiation — one of the only ways, other than the Gift, to perceive Magic.
	`You can perceive Magic as if you had the Gift, and can perceive Spirits not entirely Outside.`

Feat 2: *Call Beyond*
	*AP*: 0!| *Traits*: Primary
	The eldest technique of the Discipline — Manifesting a Familiar while retaining its Powers.
	`For the Duration, one of your Familiars is Manifested in corporeal form, keeping its stats and Initiative (if its Initiative has passed, it acts after you). Duration by Light spent: 20 for 1 Minute, 1,200 for 1 Hour.`

Feat 4: *Greater Binding*
	Greater Knights draw the gaze of further Names.
	`You gain the facility to Bind a second Familiar.`

Feat 6: *Trinity*
	The mark of the Knights and Sages Triumvirate — the aid of three Spirits Major.
	`You may Bind three Familiars at once.`

Exclusive Activity: *The Rite of Binding*
	`You seek the aid of a Spirit willing to dwell with you. Mitha need only be Named to Bind; Akthe must agree and set a Bond. Each Mitha bound or released takes 1 Minute; an Akthe Familiar takes 1 Watch.`

Exclusive Activity: *The Rite of Enchantment*
	`The means of Creating Magic Items. This uses the Artifice and Magic Items rules, but Spirit Binding subs for Artifice, Light subs for Plugs, and Spirit Names sub for Runes. Only your Bound Spirits may be called on when crafting.`

Exclusive Activity: *The Rite of Rites*
	`Composing Rites (Rituals) using Names instead of Runes, at a Tier equal to your Training. Rites may only call on Names you have Bound and cost at least 3T Light. Trained Binders may contribute their Spirits, raising the maximum Tier by one.`
