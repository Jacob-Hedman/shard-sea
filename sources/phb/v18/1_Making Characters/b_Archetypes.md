#### Archetypical Components
****
The positive aspect of your Character's psyche, an Archetype represents some like a motivation, but more primitive. The Archetype reflects both capacity, and how one response to extreme situations. 

**What Makes an Archetype**
Archetypes are not directly chosen, instead, they are determined by your fundamental drives, i.e., your Principle Skills. Your two Principle Skills make one of six possible combinations, which is your Archetype.
*Archetype Feats*
Your Archetype confers a Feat, which you may always access.
*Panic Response*
Finally, your Archetype also determines what happens when you Panic. Panic Responses are measured by Scale, corresponding to your Corruption. ([[6.0 SHARDS/DOC/Shards of Life/NU PHB no_DMG/2_Survival/c_Panic, Corruption, and Fixations]]) When you Panic, everything at your current and lower Scale occurs. So, if your Corruption was Dark (12-17), you would suffer everything but the Morbid Response.

#### The Archetypes
****

Archetype: **Warrior**
> *"Until it is Done"*
> *Skills*: Action, Resolve.
> *Feat*: Persistence
> `At any time, you may exchange three Standard Strain for a single Persistent Strain, or Persistant Strain for Corruption.`
> *Panic Response*: Rage
> *"The World is red and white, burning, the machine grinds, refusing to cease"*
> - *Clear*: `You become Frenzied for 1d6 Rounds.`
> - *Dark*:` While Frenzied, your Damage is more Vicious, but suffer one Strain for each Round spent Frenzied from Panic.`
> - *Morbid*: `You are Vulnerable_T, but your Damage is more Vicious (for a total of two more).`

Archetype: **Hero**
> *"Fight the Power"*
> *Skills*: Action, Vitality.
> *Feat*: Grit
> `Your Critical Success Range increases for every Injury, and you have Resistence-1_All while Injured.`
> *Panic Response*: Shatter Points
> *"Everything sharpens, between crowns and graves, a knife"*
> - *Clear*: `Your range for Critical Failure is expanded by 1 until you Rest, up to a natural 6.`
> - *Dark*: `You shed one Strain, but your Panic Risk Pools are bigger by one until you Rest.`
> - *Morbid*: `Your next Test is a Coinflip, confering Critical Success or Failure.`

Archetype: **Guardian**
> *"And Justice"*
> *Skills*: Vitality and Potence
> *Feat*: Rally
> `Allies within T Meters gain a Buff of 1 to all Tests.`
> *Panic Response*: Numbness
> *"The ice moves, soothing cold with a white shudder wake"*
> - *Clear*: `All Tests are Harder for you by one until you Rest, up to a maximum of 4.`
> - *Dark*: `You shed one Persistent Strain, but all Allies within T Meters suffer one Strain.`
> - *Morbid*: `You Provoke an Attack from Engaged Enemies.`

Archetype: **Immutable**
> *"I remain"*
> *Skills*: Vitality and Resolve
> *Feat*: Unflinching
> `When you are Damaged, you can voluntarily suffer one Persistent Strain to negate any Injury Risk.`
> *Panic Response*: Slip
> *"It falls away, a distance and a slope down"*
> - *Clear*: `You become one more Vulnerable until you Rest, up to 6.`
> - *Dark*: `You fall Prone, but shed 1d6 Standard Strain.`
> - *Morbid*: `Your drop anything you were holding in your Hands, but shed 1d6 more Standard Strain.`

Archetype: **Opportunist**
> *"When the time is right"*
> *Skills*: Potence and Resolve
> *Feat*: Right Place
> `You may Coin Flip to gain a Boon before making any Test, failure inflicts a Bane instead. You may choose a different Action if you fail, so long as it is not the same type. E.g., may elect to take a General Action after failing the Coinflip before an Attack.`
> *Panic Response*: Fear
> *"Darker, the worse for being harder to see"*
> - *Clear*: `For the next Minute, when you Move towards an Enemy or anything dangerous, you must Coin-Flip to do so.`
> - *Dark*: `When Moving away from an Enemy or anything Dangerous, you may move T more Meters.`
> - *Morbid*: `For the next Minute, every Creature is treated as your Enemy.`

Archetype: **Champion**
> *"Never lose"*
> *Skills*: Action, Potence.
> *Feat*: Glorious
> `Your Critical range is 1 greater and, when you Critically Succeed any Test, you shed one Strain. If you Critically succeed two or more in a row, you may shed one Persistent Strain instead.`
> *Panic Response*: Deadweight
> *"Let them down, not for lack of trying, worse, because of it"*
> - *Clear*: `For 1d6 Rounds, Allies suffer one Strain while within T Meters of you.`
> - *Dark*: `For the next Minute, Allies are one more Vulnerable while within Range.`
> - *Morbid*: `Both effects also apply to Enemies.`
