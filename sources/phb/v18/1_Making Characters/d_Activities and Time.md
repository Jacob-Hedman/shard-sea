****
Time in-game is measured in bulky units of Opportunity, which represent blocks of the day, usually several Hours.

**Opportunity in the Day**
Each Day (24 Hours) is broken into six Watches (4 Hours). As time usually does, Watches pass, decaying to the last, Twilight, before the Dawn of the next day. Activities and other procedures usually indicate medium Durations in terms of Watches.
- *6*: Dawn
- *5*: Noon
- *4*: Eve
- *3*: Dusk
- *2*: Midnight
- *1*: Twilight
*Tracking Watches*
> When playing the game, Watches allow the GM to track the time with a D6, with the current Shift facing up. They may also track Watches with a clock, a pie chart, or any other six-aspected countable.
*Using and Spending Watches*
> Each Day of active gameplay usually involves doing things, spending time. Watches are spent in the doing, usually specified in Activities, granted by a Character's Skills or Disciplines.
*Opportunity Costs*
> Watches are counted down because they correspond to the Durations of Activities and other procedures. So, if a Character elects to spend one Shift, at Noon, they will be involved with that Duration until the Eve of the same Day. *Unless otherwise indicated, only one Activity or other time-consuming procedure can be undertaken at once.*
*Auspicious Moments*
> Lastly, some effects and events require a specific time, say, a Ritual at Dawn or a Prayer uttered at Midnight. These times are tracked with the corresponding Shift.

**Player Currency**
Narrative time in Watches is a global tracker and a resource. Just like real time, there is a dual aspect of the passage of time as an objective process, and the use of time as a resource. 
*Thinking About Time*
> At any given moment in-game, Players can talk about the current and remaining Watches in or out of Character. It being Noon means one further Shift in the normal daytime remains.
*Conflicts*
> As time passes, Players can elect to spend it in differently sized chunks. One might spend one Shift on an Activity, another might spend two. Because time is narratively external to the Players, the first is "free" before the second, while the latter is busy with their chosen Activity until its Duration passes. *So, while Watches are spend as a currency to perform in-game, the passage of time is uniform and outside of their direct influence.*
*GM Fiat*
> Expanding on the last point, the GM tracks and controls the passage of time. They may indicate movement in time without Players spending any, or advise players in advance of a skip or jump in time. Say, when the Players elect to wait for the arrival of an important NPC, and the GM indicates it will take them at least three Watches to appear. In the case of a jump like this, Players can spend those Watches actively on Opportunities or other options, or wait passively.

#### Activities
****
Actions are highly specific procedures usually limited to Round-Round chunks of time. Activities represent a wider means of applying Skills and Disciplines. An Activity is a procedure, one that leverage a Skill towards some tangible effect. From social-interactions to Sneaking, the Activity both enables and governs the means of interacting using your Skills.

**Specificity**
Activities give concrete results to applying your Skills and Disciplines. That also means both Players and GM should be generally aware of what Activities exist. Simple Skill Tests can resolve things quickly, but can leave things like a DC or exact consequences ambiguous. So, one should avoid those resolutions when and where an Activity would give a more satisfying conclusion.
*Social Mechanical*
> This is especially true for social interactions. Simple Skill Tests offer basically no real framework for these, and relying on roleplay and common sense can be unfair. My character being physically strong doesn't demand that I do deadlift in real life. Likewise, playing a charismatic or intimidating character shouldn't require those real skills, necessarily. Activities here are intended to reward good roleplay, but also provide a mechanical backbone to structure those interactions.

**General and Exclusive Activities**
Activities are divided into two broad types. General Activities can be performed by anyone, even if they lack the Skill governing it. Exclusive Activities, conversely, prevent anyone from using them unless they have the governing Skill or Discipline.

## Skill Activities
****
The following is a list of Activities that can be performed in general, grouped by their governing Skills. Other Activities can be found in Disciplines, which are Exclusive to their governing Disciplines.

#### Immanence
****

Exclusive Activity: **Decipher**
> *"Each letter an interface"*
> `When presented with either a cipher or a foreign language, and you have at least some reference regarding its solution, for example, a single translated word of the cipher, a hint from the maker, a similar language in common, etc. you may attempt to decode its meaning.`
> *Time*
> `Deciphering a single page-worth requires a number of Watches equal to the sum of two Chaos Dice, minus your Immanence.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators roll a Chaos Die, and allow you to take the lower Die.

Exclusive Activity: **Conceptual Intuition**
> *"Everything connected"*
> `You raise a Topic, which can be a place, a thing, a concept, especially something important to your current goals, aims, or circumstances. The GM will then roll Chaos Dice, equal to your Immanence. If any come up 6, you gain a Link, which is a vague association between the Topic and another, one which the GM knows is interesting or important.`
> *Structure*
> `Links are just associations, a sense that one Topic is related to another, not so much a Clue but a promise of one. At most, a Link between Topics has a kind of vague shape to it, a feeling, emotional value, or gravity of import.`
> *Time*
> `Doing this takes one Watch, and is limited to once Daily. During the Watches of Twilight and Dawn you gain an effective Buff of 1 to your Immanence.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators may contribute a Chaos Die, of all Collaborators' Dice, only the highest result it counted.

General Activity: **Recall**
> *"What the eye had seen"*
> `You choose something you would have been able to witness, overhear, or may have incidentally noted in your past.`
> *Procedure*
> `The GM rolls Chaos Dice, up to twice your Immanence. If what you wish to remember occured within a number of Days equal to or less than the sum, you recall it.`
> *Time*
> `This requires a number of Minutes, up to the number of Days past minus your Immanence. You cannot retry this for the same thing within the same Day.`
> *Limits*
> `Recalled information is not exact, enough for a few words, an image, a face, etc. Each Chaos Die that rolls a 6 also distorts the recollection in some way.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators can contribute a Chaos Die to the roll.
#### Action
****

Exclusive Activity: **Practice Forms**
> *"one motion, a thousand times over"*
> `You obtain Practice equal to the Watches Spent, up to your Action. Practice can be spent increase any of your Melee Attacks by 1 (before or after rolling), or to instantly Maneuver 1 Meter as a 0 AP Reaction to anything at all.`
> *Collaboration*: Unskilled Collaborators can gain a single Practice for three Opportunity. 

Exclusive Activity: **Sport**
> *"Work it out"*
> `After participating in Sport, you gain a Buff to your Body or Reflex for the next Week, this is equal to the Watches spent, up to your Tier.`
> *Relief*
> `You can Coin-Flip to remove 1 Corruption after Sport, but must Rest before gaining this benefit again.`
> *Collaboration*: Unskilled Collaborators gain the same benefits but for half as long.
#### Potence
****
Exclusive Activity: **Hone Aim**
> *"Just to the left"*
> `You gain Precision equal to the Watches spent, up to your Potence. Precision can be spent to increase a Ranged Attack by 1 (before or after rolling) or, conversely, to raise the Difficulty of any Attack against you or make it less Vicious. Precision can also be used when Striking with a Finesse Weapon to it more Vicious by one.`
> *Collaboration*: Unskilled Collaborators can gain a single Precision for three Opportunity. 

Exclusive Activity: **Reckon the Odds**
> *"Next time's different"*
> `Name a Foe you have witnessed previously and spend one Watch. For the next Day, you gain an Edge against them, which makes them Vulnerable to you equal to your Potence. At any time, you can Consume this Edge to force them to re-roll anything and take the result you choose.`
> *Collaboration*: Unskilled Collaborators who have witnessed a Foe can provide the necessary experience second-hand.
#### Vitality
****

Exclusive Activity: **Conditioning**
> *"Stronger"*
> `You gain Strength for each Watch Spent, up to your Vitality. Strength can be spent at any time to shed a single point of Persistant or Standard Strain, or to temporary double your vertical lift for a single Full-Turn.`
> *Liberation*
> `If you spend three or more Opportunity at one time, you may Coin-Flip to shed one Corruption.`
> *Collaboration*: Unskilled Collaborators may gain 1 Strength for 3 Opportunity.

General Activity: **First-Aid**
> *"Care"*
> `Interacting with an Injured Creature, you make a Vitality Test with a DC of 10+(their number of Injuries). This can be yourself, but this increases the DC by your Tier.`
> *Time*
> `This is usually a 2_AP General Action, but can be extended for better odds. If you spend at least 6 AP you always end the Bleeding Condition, and one Minute or more grants a Boon to your Test.`
> *Result*
> `If you roll at least 8, you remove the Bleeding Condition, and Success removes a single Minor Injury.`
> *Recovery Time*
> `This can also hasten the Recovery Time of a Moderate or Treated Major Injury. Success against a DC of 10+(Injury Strain) you allow the Target to treat every Day that Week as two.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators grant a Boon.

#### Resolve
****

Exclusive Activity: **Reinforce**
> *"Get a grip"*
> `You obtain Willpower, one for each Opportunity spent, up to your Resolve. Willpower can be spent to reduce the amount of Chaos Dice rolled for Panic Risk.`
> *Collaboration*: Unskilled Collaborators that participate may gain one Willpower for three Opportunity.

General Activity: **Interrogate**
> *"Pull it out"*
> `You attempt to extract some information from an unwilling Character, provided that they can Communicate with you and are in your Custody for the Duration.`
> *Procedure*
> `The GM secretly rolls Chaos Dice, two plus the Target's Resolve. The Duration required to obtain your answer is equal to the sum in Hours; for each 6, the Target will attempt to lie at some point in this process.`
> *Custody and Time*
> `You do not need to present for the entire Duration, but the Target must be held somewhere, prevented from leaving, to count as being in your Custody.`
> *Forcing the Issue*
> `For each Opportunity you are present, you reduce the Duration by Hours up to your Resolve. Likewise, you can double this efficacy by making things hard for the Target; the GM rolls another Chaos Die and subtracts it from the Duration, but a 6 yields another lie.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators increase your Resolve by one.
#### Creation
****

Exclusive Activity: **Craft**
> *"Make it first"*
> `You attempt to create a non-Magical, non-Consumable, mundance Item, whether this is possible is adjudicated as follows:`
> *Nature*
> `You must know how to make it and a plausible explanation why.`
> *Resources*
> `You must have the components, these usually have a Price around half that of the Object. When an Item is explicitely made of a given Resource, you must have have enough.`
> *Time*
> `Crafting an Item requires a number of Watches, 10 times its Tier, minus your Creation. So if your Tier was 3, a Tier_1 Item would take 7 Watches to Craft.`
> *Limits*
> `You cannot Craft an Item whose Tier exceeds your Creation.`
> *Collaborators*: Skilled Collaborators reduce the time needed by half, to a minimum of One Watch.

General Activity: **Patch**
> *"Make it better"*
> `You attempt to remove one Notch from an Item.`
> *Procedure*
> `Nature and Resources are evaluated as Crafting, and you must make a Creation Test depending on the time spent.`
> - 1 Hour: `14`
> - 1 Watch: `12`
> - 1 Day: `10`
> `If you fail, you can spend more time to lower the DC. Success removes one Notch.`
> *Critical Effect*
> `You halve the time spent.`
> *Collaborators*: Skilled Collaborators increase your Creation by one.

General Activity: **Cook**
> *"Comfort from some direction or other"*
> `You create a Meal for yourself and fellows, which can have Ingredient Traits with a sum Tier up to your 1+(your Creation), as follows:`
> - 1| Salty: `You are immune to Extreme Cold of 1+T or below.`
> - 1| Sweet: `When Traveling on Foot, your Speed is greater by 5T`
> - 1| Spiced: `You are immune to Extreme Heat of 1+T or below.`
> - 2| Hardy: `You may ignore up to T Days of missed Sleep.`
> - 2| Calming: `When rolling Terrain or Navigation Difficulty, you may reduce the amount of Chaos Dice by T.`
> `All Traits can be added multiple times and may stack.`
> *Duration*
> `A Meal for 6 or so Creatures takes 1 Hour to create, and the effects of a Meal last for the next 24 Hours.`
> *Price*
> `If you lack access to the Ingredients per say, they can be purchased with an approximate cost of T(1+T).`
> *Unique Ingredients*
> `You might gain access to an Ingredient which confers a special effect while at Sea. These can be included with a general Tier of 1, or more at GM discretion, as some may be simply harder to work with.`
> *Collaborators*: Each Collaborator cuts the time per Meal by 5 Minutes, to a minimum of 10.

#### Traversal
****

Exclusive Activity: **Scrounge**
> *"Making do"*
> `You scrape the environs for anything of use, valuables, resources, and tools. If any whole Items exist, you find them unless concealed.`
> *Scrap-Made*
> `Further, when you Scrounge, you can assemble make-shift mundane Items. These scrap-made Items can be anything not Magical and must represent a base Item with a hypothetical Price no greater than (25 times Watches spent) in total. These Items must be intuitively plausible given the environment and account for Resources. For example, you cannot make a Canteen full of water in a desert, unless you find a spring, or fresh air Cannisters from nothing.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators may add their Opportunity to yours, and the Unskilled may do so at half the Rate half their spent Points to the total.

General Activity: **Sea Travel**
> *"Setting sail"*
> `You move from one place in the Shard Sea to another by Alembic Shard Ship. What remains uncertain is how long this may take, and what you may be required to spend.`
> *Units*
> `Expeditioners have a language of Sea-travel, ascending categories of greater and greater size. The Verdun nautical unit is the Maru, roughly 10 Sekta, 30 Koi, or 300 Kilometers in any Direction. On the Map of the Shard Sea, each Hex represents a space 30 Maru in Diameter.`
> *Speed And Time*
> `For each Day, a Ship moves up to 3(its Speed) Maru. This time is spent on the Ship, and doesn't require constant attention (especially if you have functioning crew). Expeditioners on a Ship may spend these Watches on other Activities for the Duration, unless circumstances demand their attention.`
> *Depth*
> `Traveling deeper is much faster, increasing Ship Speed by 2 for each Layer of the Sea descended below the Surface. However, the dangers below are rarely worth the risk. Ascending to the boarder of the Upper Aether reduces Speed by half, but is drastically safer.`
> *Terrain and Navigation Difficulty*
> `These concepts also apply to Sea Travel, though a little differently. Both are rolled in secret for each Stretch of the journey and, while Terrain Difficulty and Hazards apply in roughly the same fashion, Navigation Difficulty adds to the total Distance by 3 per 6 rolled.`

General Activity: **Plot a Course**
> *"Map in hand"*
> `Spend one Watch taking in your surroundings. You gain Guidance, up to one plus your Traversal. Guidance can be spent in the next 24 Hours to counter Navigation and Terrain Difficulty, each in their own ways.`
> *Terrain*
> `When you would roll Chaos Dice for Terrain Difficulty, you may spend Guidance to remove the same number of Dice.`
> *Navigation*
> `You do not necessarily know about Navigation Difficulty in advance, or when it effects your Travel. However, before moving one Hex, Guidance can be spent to counter one potential deflection from Navigation Difficulty.`
> *Courses in the Sea*
> `Guidance can also be used during Sea Travel, 3 can be spent to find a previously unknown Path, adding it to the array of choices for that Stretch of the Course.`
> *Components*
> `With instruments like Compass and Spyglass, you may gain one additional Guidance.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators can increase your Traversal by 1.

General Activity: **Overland Travel**
> *"On the road"*
> `You choose one of the six Directions, corresponding to the faces of the current Hex, and Move by 1 Hex, marking the change on the Recorded Map.`
> *Terrain Difficulty*
> `When Traveling, you roll Chaos Dice equal to the Terrain Difficulty of the current Hex. If any roll 6, a Travel Complication occurs, with a Severity given by the number of Dice. Terrain Complications are rolled by the GM and narrated to the Players, whose goal is to either resolve them or manage their consequences.`
> *Navigation Difficulty*
> `At the same time Players roll for Terrain Difficulty, the GM secretly rolls Chaos Dice equal to the Navigation Difficulty of those Hexes. You may roll Traversal to learn this, as well as the Terrain Difficulty of the Hex you would Travel to.`
> *Speed and Time for Travel*
> `Moving by foot, you may Travel one Hex (10 Kilimeters) per Watch. Other modes of transport can be had, and can impact the Distance moved, inferred from the new rate. For example, if one had mode of Travel that gave a Speed of 20km, they would move two Hexes within a Watch. Likewise, two Points would yield a Distance of three Hexes given a Speed of 15km.`
> *Moving Multiple Hexes*
> `Even when Traveling multiple Hexes at once, as outlined above, each Hex departed is evaluated individually, both for Direction and Terrain/Navigation Difficulty. For example, one could, with a Speed of 20 km, spend one Watch to move one Hex Northeast and then another Northwest.`
> *Limits*
> `A Shard's Aether Wake makes Overland Travel by Ship impossible, without a Pylon to stabilize the Aether within each Hex Travelled.`
> *Collaborators*: Skilled Collaborators can confer a Boon to your Traversal Tests.

General Activity: **Harvest**
> *"A bit of this"*
> `You gather materials from your immediate vicinity, depending on the type.`
> *Dead Creatures*
> `From a freshly dead Creature, you may collect any natural poisons it may have had, its bones, organs, meat, etc. In general, you can obtain edibles equal to a Ration for every (10 minus your Traversal) Bulk. (Of course, this assumes the Creature is edible at all.) Each Creature may be Harvested for 30 Minutes per carcass. (1 Watch for 8.)`
> *Consumable Flora*
> `You obtain Doses up to your Traversal per Watch spent. A DC_10 Traversal Test can double this, triple for a Critical Success.`
> *Resources*
> `You obtain Bulk up to ten times your Traversal per Opportunity. A DC_10 Traversal Test can double this, triple for a Critical Success.`
> *Limits*
> `Of course, limited supplies are a factor, and the GM is free to limit Harvested resources to an amount logical for the Terrain.`
> *Unique Harvests*
> `At GM discretion, you might find something special, particularly if the Creature had a bounty, or was made of a naturally valuable material. This can often take more time to Harvest properly.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators can add their Watches to yours, and the Unskilled can do so at half the rate.

General Activity: **Animal Ken**
> *"Woah there"*
> `You influence the demeaner and behavior of a non-verbal Creature like a Beast.`
> *Disposition*: Friendly: 6-5| Neutral: 4-3| Hostile: 2-1
> `This measures the Target's animosity towards you. The GM knows this value.`
> *Procedure*
> `For every 2_AP spent, you can roll Chaos Dice up to one plus your Traversal. Each 1-3 raises its Disposition, while 4-6s reduce it, to a minimum of 1 (when it might begin to attack if it wasn't already). If its Disposition is more than 6, it ceases hostility. Targets trained to follow directions like mounts or companions are controlled by you in Initiative when their Disposition is greater than 6. (Training a wild animal for this usually takes a month or so of daily work, at least a Watch or so for each.)`
> *Limits*
> `Harming the Target or taking Actions of overt hostility towards it reduces its Disposition, as do sudden and frightening events, which cause temporary reductions even for trained companion Creatures. In those cases, you'll need to use Animal Ken to regain control of them.`
> *Collaboration*: Each Skilled Collaborator allows you to ignore a 6 over the whole course of the Activity.

#### Darkness
****

Exclusive Activity: **Disguise**
> *"Who"*
> `You create a Disguise, using clothing and perhaps specialized tools to pass its wearer off as someone else.`
> *Plausibility*
> `A Disguise needs to represent the Target (even if their is no real Target) in a believable way. Passing yourself off as an officer requires a uniform. The wearer must also reasonably resemble the Target, as even prosthetics may only do so much. When you create a Disguise, it has a Durability equal to the Hours spent, up to one plus your Darkness.`
> *Using a Disguise*
> `Whenever you pass yourself off as someone else, to one or more Creatures at a time, this counts as a use. When you use the Diguise, you roll a Chaos Die. A 6 Notches it; if this ever exceeds its Durability, the Disguise is seen through.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators can increase the resulting Quality by 1.

Exclusive Activity: **Safecrack**
> *"Listen and spin"*
> `You Interact with a Lock, and roll a Chaos Die for every 3 AP. Each 1 Notches it, unlocking once you exceed its Durability. Rolled 6s remove Notches.`
> *Lock Tiers*
> `Locks have Durability along this general curve`
> - 1: `Simple keylocks`
> - 2: `Houses`
> - 3: `A personal Safe`
> - 6: `Bank vaults`
> *Skill*
> `You may roll more Chaos Dice when Safecracking, up to your Darkness.`
> *Collaborators*: Skilled Collaborators allow you to roll an additional Chaos Die.

General Activity: **Cover Tracks**
> *"Traceless"*
> `The GM rolls a secret Test using your Darkness. On a success, you erase forensic evidence of your presence from a location, along a path, etc.`
> *Difficulty*
> `This depends on how much evidence you need to remove, corresponding with your relation to the place:`
> - Your own home: `14`
> - Residence of at least a Day: `12`
> - Present for only one Scene: `10` 
> *Degrees of Failure*
> `If you fail by less than your Darkness, the GM will tell you. Otherwise, your success is ambiguous until consequences ensue.`
> *Critical effect*
> `The GM will tell you that you succeeded.`
> *Multiple Traces*
> `You can erase the evidence of several people, but each additional Creature raises the DC by 1.`
> *Time*
> `This can be performed on the move, but you can spend Opportunity in a single place to gain Boons to this roll at a rate of one for one.`
> *Collaborators*: Skilled Collaborators reduce the Difficulty by 1, Unskilled by half that amount.

General Activity **Sneak**
> *"Quiet"*
> `You attempt to take an Action or Actions without being Detected. All Actions while Sneaking are declared before rolling, and occur after the pool is rolled.`
> *Visibility*
> `Sneaking automatically fails if you do not start in Cover from your Witnesses. Witnesses mean Creatures that would matter were they to see you. Your Allies, a hapless onlooker with some Scopes, or a passing bird are not usually relevant Witnesses.`
> *Risk*
> `The GM will secretly roll a Pool of Chaos Dice, and if any come up Face 6, you are Detected. The size of this Pool is determined by the following conditions:`
> - Exposure| Clear: `4`| Murky: `2`| Dark: `1`
> > - `This identifies how easily the Witness could perceive you, loud, badly lit, or close environs are usually Dark and just about any fight is usually Murky. Clear conditions are just the worst possible situation to be Sneaking.`
> - Witnesses| `The Pool size is increased by the Luminance of the most Skilled single Witness to your Actions.`
> - Skill| `The Pool size is decreased by your own Darkness, to a minimum of one.`
> - Multiplicity: `Each Action after the first increases the Pool by one.`
> *Results*
> `If none of the Dice come up 6, your Actions resolve without Detection. These may cross through the Senses of any Creature with no consequence, but they would instantly Detect you if you were to end your Sneaking there. So, you might Sneak to Move into Engagement from Total Cover and Attack an unsuspecting Foe, denying them the chance to Resist. After the Attack Resolved, you would be Detected; instead, if you Snuck to Move, Attack, and Moved again, you might leave Engagement and return to Cover, starting and ending Undetected (at the cost of a higher potential Risk of being Detected in the first.)`

General Activity: **Deceive**
> *"Spin a Web"*
> `You attempt to lead a Target astray, to lie and mislead.`
> *Disposition*: Friendly: 6-5| Neutral: 4-3| Hostile: 2-1
> `This measures how the Target feels about you. NPC Dispotions are only known to the GM.`
> *Attitude*: Probable: 6-5| Neutral: 4-3| Absurd: 2-1
> `This measures how subjectively plausible the Target regards the lie, and is known by the GM.`
> *Procedure*
> `First, roll a Chaos Die as you make your case to the NPC in-Character. You can roll more for each Minute you spend. Rolling a 1-3 raises their Attitude, and a 4-6 decreases it. If their Attitude would be more than 6, they are Deceived. If their Attitude would fall below 1, they break off the conversation and flatly deny you.`
> *Limits*
> `You can only roll Chaos Dice up to the Disposition of the NPC plus your own Darkness, and automatically fail if you attempt to exceed it. The Target can also pretend to believe you.`
> *Extra Factors*
> `If you make an especially good point, or merit it by circumstance, the GM will notify you that the Disposition of the NPC was increased by 1. These changes can be lasting at their discretion, carrying into further interactions as the Target's opinion of you increases. `
> *Collaborators*: Each Skilled Collaborator raises the maximum Dice you can roll by one.

General Activity: **Intimidation**
> *"Get out of my way"*
> `You communicate, for 1_AP, the danger of crossing you, forcefully.`
> *Disposition*: Concerned: 6-5| Neutral: 4-3| Aloof: 2-1
> `This measures how threatened the Target is by you. The GM knows this, but if your Tiers are equal, this is usually 3; circumstances and reputation are big factors.`
> *Procedure*
> `Make a Darkness Test with a DC=16-(Their Disposition). If you succeed, they refrain from taking any hostile Actions (or anything you explicitely forbid), so long as you don't attempt to harm them. If you Critically Succeed, they attempt to flee the Scene; or they might even fight for you, so long as their close loyalties aren't crossed.`
> *Contagion*
> `If multiple Foes perceive this, and their Disposition would be higher than the Target's, they are also Intimidated on a success. You can ask your Gm to point out group-leaders with a DC_10 Luminance to game this interaction.`
> *Context and Limits*
> - `If you've killed or Injured one the Target's Allies in the same Scene, you roll with a Boon. If you've attemped and failed to Intimidate them, you suffer a Bane. Targets are immune to a third attempt after two failures.`
> - `Mindless Creatures and those incapable of language like Beasts are immune to Intimidation as such.`
> *Collaboration*: Skilled Collaborators grant you one Boon, and can participate as a 1_AP Reaction.
#### Luminance
****
Exclusive Activity: **Study**
> *"What's what"*
> `You attempt to ascertain some information from available Sources, be it a single book, a library, or an entire college. To evaluate this, clearly specify a Question to the GM. They will then roll a secret Test using your Luminance to arrive at the result.`
> *Results*
> `With a roll of at least 10, you gain a loose picture of the body of information available in the Source, as well as a general answer to the Question. At 12 or more, you gain a fairly exact answer to the Question. On a roll of 14, you gain a precise answer.`
> *Time*
> `Study takes at least one Watch, and each additional adds a Boon to your Test.`
> *Failure*
> `If you fail, you do not learn whether the answer exists in the Source, and the GM will provide a generic, but wrong answer to the Question. If the roll is a natural 2, this misinformation will be extremely specific, and in the complete wrong direction.`
> *Critical effect*
> `The GM will reveal your roll.`
> *Collaboration*: Trained Collaborators can pool their Watches and share the benefits of Study.

General Activity: **Wayfinding**
> *"Ear to the ground"*
> `You make a Luminance Test against the Navigation Difficulty of the current Terrain (a value which remains undisclosed.) If you succeed, the GM indicates your current location using the best information available to your Character. If you have a Map which represents the location, for example, you could learn your position on it. If not, you might learn at least relative Distances from important or visited landmarks.`
> *Land and Sea*
> `This works about the same when in the Sea or exploring a Shard. However, you must have a functioning Aetheric Wavecaster to use this in the Sea.`
> *Duration and Difficulty*
> `When Wayfinding, the GM rolls Chaos Dice equal to the Navigation Difficulty. The sum, in Minutes, is the time taken to complete this Activity, and each 6 raises the Difficulty by 1.`
> *Critical effect*

General Activity: **Convince**
> *"Get your way"*
> `You attempt to convince a Target to do or believe something.`
> *Disposition*: Friendly: 6-5| Neutral: 4-3| Hostile: 2-1
> `This measures how the Target feels about you. NPC Dispotions are only known to the GM.`
> *Attitude*: Eager: 6-5| Neutral: 4-3| Hostile: 2-1
> `This measures how they feel the subject you'd like to Convince them of. Like Dispositions, this is only known by the GM.`
> *Procedure*
> `First, roll a Chaos Die as you make your case to the NPC in-Character. You can roll more for each Minute you spend. Rolling 1-3 or less raises their Attitude, and a 4-6 decreases it. If their Attitude would be more than 6, they are Convinced. If their Attitude would fall below 1, they break off the conversation and flatly refuse.`
> *Limits*
> `You can only roll Chaos Dice up to the Disposition of the NPC plus your own Unity, and automatically fail if you attempt to exceed it. Because this is a hidden value, Convincing is a bit of a gamble. Likewise, the Target can always lie, entertaining you for as long as they wish, or pretending to be Convinced.`
> *Extra Factors*
> `If you make an especially good point, or merit it by circumstance, the GM will notify you that the Disposition of the NPC was increased by 1. These changes can be lasting at their discretion, carrying into further interactions as the Target's opinion of you increases.`
