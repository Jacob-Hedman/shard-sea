## What's in a Background?
****
Character Creation is an extremely important element of a system, as it tends to be the thing most frequently latched onto, so to speak, by its community. Classes are a popular example, providing a space for player identification and eventual expression. 

a Background is a kind of person, an abstraction which distills an essence of their role in the Ark. Each provides 3 elements, Initial Conditions, associating your Original Social Class with a Starting Wealth during Character Creation (as well as Traits in some cases), your Contact Points, and an Environment. Environments help you explain your personal history, and can be Invoked during gameplay. 

**Invocation**
When a situation involving any Test (including Flat Tests) relates to your Background, especially its Environment, you can gain a +1, once per Scene. Likewise, your Contacts can be involved and appealed to in the same way any NPC can. Depending on their Type, they might even directly assist you.

#### Backgrounds
****
**Land Barons**
> *Starting Wealth*: 100(2d6)+100
> *Contact Points*: 2
Property-owning agricultural families responsible for food. Because Magic is unable to create it, the highest status is afforded to the patriarch and matriarch, who confer a descending status by association to their children and to eventually even their menial workers. 

In the Hectad, the Barons forward members by mutual negotiation, often exchanging land or favors. The Small Council, by contrast, seats the eldest capable member of the Barony.

**the Priesthood**
> *Starting Wealth*: 100(2d6)+50
> *Contact Points*: 2
Individuals blessed by the Verdun Deities to function as authorities of their domains and conduits of their powers. Priests have their own internal hierarchy, with Gaian Harvest Priests at the top. "She Who Adds" is crucial in the function of the Farms, and this puts her Priests near the status of Land Barons. Hospitallers of Bella provide medicine, Lumenaries are responsible for leadership and for Sunlight, Luna's Shade Walkers glimpse the future, Paladins judge, Forge priests provide the Ark's architecture, weaponry, and raw materials, Friends of Death tend to the Effigies of those gone, and Nous' Orrators recount the history of the world.

6 ranking members of each Priesthood fill the Hectad, excluding Lumenaries, who have the high Priest as representative in the Small Council.

**The Noble Houses**
> *Starting Wealth*: 100(2d6)+200
> *Contact Points*: 3
Families whose firstborn children are Priests. A Priest is a Noble by blood, but the Nobility is a secondary position merited by relation to the higher class Priests. Unlike their cloistered relatives, the Nobles tend to be the drivers of secular business. Connection to the divine, no matter how tangential, is almost always followed by material success.

The Nobles decide representation almost exclusively in terms of wealth. Holding an ample vault of Residue is often key to sitting in the Hectad, while an empty one is a frequent result of paying the necessary bribes to preside on the Small Council.

**Makers**
> *Starting Wealth*: 100(2d6)+100
> *Contact Points*: 2
Workers arranged in guilds, responsible for manufacture from raw materials. Forge Priests can Soulcast nearly anything into workable metals and timber, and so skillful craft is more valued than the luster of most substances. Artists, Bakers, Chefs, and Artificers stand above the rest, followed by tailors and cobblers. Architecture and smithing is accomplished by Forge Priests, and so neither Vocation is seen as the place of a Maker.

Makers delegate their members of the Hectad and head on the Small Council by vote, leading to the relatively wide reputation for populism in their ranks.

**Mages**
> *Starting Wealth*: 100(2d6)+50
> *Contact Points*: 1
The Guild of Mages is set apart and literally below the other classes. Residing in the undercity, Mages can come from any other class, conscripted when found bearing the Gift. They provide vital service in the form of clean air and the protective Wards around each Ark, as well as the basic components of Artifice (as well as Magecraft, Artifice entirely constructed by Mages, usually for their own use.) But the deadly nature of the Initiation and mandatory conscription means that the Guild is held in I'll repute by most, despite their necessity. 

The Archmage of the Guild is decided by merit alone. To the one with the vastest array of Arcane knowledge and ability, goes the chair of the Small Council. Their subordinates, also chosen by merit, seat the Hectad. This procedure earns Mages very little against their reputation as cold competitors.

**Expeditioners**
> *Starting Wealth*: 100(2d6)+50
> *Contact Points*: 2
Those willing to venture beyond the Ark, into the Sea of Shards. This is the most dangerous of all vocations, and open to all classes. Mages, Priests, Nobles, even Makers or lower-ranking members of a Barony find themselves joining the Expeditioner's Guild if they feel the calling. These have similar status to Mages, insofar as they perform a highly necessary task, but the danger and skillsets involved often provoke an equal amount of fear as admiration. An Expedition is an adventure to some, a death sentence to others.

Expeditioners serving in the Hectad or Small Council must forswear the allegiance to their Class of origin (unless their parents were Expeditioners already.) This leaves a fairly small population of Careerists who decide their membership in both bodies by vote. For Careerists, the most important elements of a prospective politician is experience and shrewd judgement, and the members they present tend to be as weathered and crass as one might expect.
## Contacts
****
Part of a Background are people you know. Connections, friends, family, rivals, etc. For this purpose, each Background has Contact Points which you can invest in Contacts, people you know that have the ability to provide you with Info or aid. 
#### Types of Contact
****
When investing in a Contact, you must set a Type, which specifies their relationship to you and what they can do or might expect from you. This also determines the cost in Contact Points.

*You can choose multiple, but cannot have more than 1 of the same Type of Contact.*
****
- **Patron**      : *4*| 
> - Patrons are accomplished people with their own faction who, having seen something in you, are willing to help for a price. They will demand periodic service to them but can leverage their Faction in your favor. Patrons also tend to grow with you.
- **Mentor**      : *3*| 
> - Mentors can help you Train in Disciplines, of which you can pick 3.
- **Friend**      : *2*| 
> - Friends are the people you drink with, talk to, and spend time around. They can help like any other NPC, but will be much more willing to hear you out.
- **Acquaintance**: *1*| 
> - Acquaintances are little better than strangers, maybe able to help, maybe harm. They could be promising future Friends or become Rivals, but most stay out of it. 
- **Rival**       :*-1*| 
> - Rivals oppose you, attempting to trip you up at every step of the way. They grow as you Advance and use whatever is at their disposal to end you.
- **Bond**        :*-2*| 
> - Bonds can be more than one person, a family, parents, or even a place or concept. The importance of Bonds is their hold on you. *Every time you jeopardize or harm a Bond, you suffer 1 Corruption.*
#### Nature of a Contact
****
Once you pick a Type, you should give them some specifics. 

*The Tier and other aspects of the NPC will be decided by the GM. Note: Mentors generally need to be Elites for their Disciplines.*
****
- **Who**  : are they to you? Sex? 
- **What** : do they do? Occupation? Skills?
- **Where**: are they? What part of the Ark?
- **Why**  : do they do it? Interests? Goals?
#### Financial Benefits of a Background
****
Your Background can also allow you to earn money, though finances should primary be handled by your work with the Player Company.

Activity: **Work**
> *Opportunity Cost*:
> As much as desired, minimum 1.
> `Your Background also represents your professional abilities, something you can be paid for. When you Work, you gain Plugs equal to 1/10th the Starting income of your Background, plus your Tier.`
