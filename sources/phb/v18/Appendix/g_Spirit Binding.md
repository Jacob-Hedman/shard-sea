#### Spirit Binding
****
The world that is known is Inside, bounded and ordered by causes and effects. The unknown world is Outside, subtle and invisible, infinite and beyond the structure of this, then, and that. Spirits are of that world, and the in-between.

**What is a Spirit**
*"A Spirit is a Name, or rather one of its thousand-thousand faces. Names are born from their bearers, and bind them. A Name circumscribes it, but they gave us the Word."*

**Meerh**
The skin between the Inside and Out, both invisible and within the reach of mortals is Meerh. While Spirits exist in some sense in all three realms, Meerh demarcates the principle division of their kinds. 
*Arkhe*
	Greater beings, like the Nikeen and others exist first in the Outside, in the world of Gods and Demons. Arkhe have almost no tangible presence Inside; their shadows are only visible in the depths of Meerh.
*Akthe*
	Equally present within Meerh and the Inside, the Twilight Spirits walk the worlds visible and invisible, passing between the two at will.
*Mitha*
	Lesser Spirits, the Mitha are present almost wholly Inside, almost animal in their nature.

**The Courts of Spirit**
Arkhe are their own realm entirely, but the Akthe and Mitha are divided into further distinctions, those of Courts. This also points the role of Spirits in the world, as both Akthe and Mitha seem related to aspects thereof. Two opposing schools of thought predominate in the Anya philosophy, being that Spirits either reflect or facilitate important principles. Common to both, is the notion that Spirits are connected to concepts. 
- *Elementals*: The Spirits of substance, of metals and gems and earth, magma, and air.
- *Forces*: Those of energies, lightning, fire, gravity, light, and cold.
- *Memories*: Of both past and future, rarely seen and esoteric, even as lessers.
- *Caerns*: Spirits of places, inhabiting cities and temples as often as caves and rivers.
	- Also Items aka Excalibur
- *Nymphs*: Living spirits, stepping through trees and over grasses.
- *Faeyer*: The most like People, and the least. Faeyer are abstractions, sometimes baring no clear resemblance to anything they might connect with, or personifying something as invisible as a dream.

**The Disaster**
*"Long ago, when the Serpent Kind still walked the earth, the Spirits began to war. Names turned against their bearers, unleashing power terrific and unbearable across the lands of Dorn, and even reaching the further Wastes of Kaln. All Kingdoms fell, crowns dishonored and destroyed. It was only the Caerns who showed mercy to the People, giving us home and shelter.*

*In his youth, our Great King, known then by another name, ventured from his home, to the Wilds which had long been broken and ravaged before his birth. It was there he learned to forge the Rings, where he made the seven great pacts, binding the seven greater Names, and bringing us the knowledge of the Sages. This knowledge he gave to the other People too, so that we could once again build Kingdoms worthy of crowns.*

*The Sages and Knights now defend us, walking the Wilds and bringing back riches. Most of all luxuries, greatest of all gifts, they bring Light."*

#### Binding Spirits
****
*"Spirit Binders make Pacts with the Spirits of the World, giving them Light and Honor in trade for power. The secrets of making these Pacts are guarded by the Sages, Night for Astarkis and Ramis, the Whites of Imhalis, the School of Red in Prospero, and the Moon Sages of Kaln, these Schools and their Scions protect and keep the King's wisdom, and instruct their members and sworn Knights to use their abilities for the good of the People."*

**Who Can Bind**
The Discipline of Spirit Binding is arduously learned, given at one of the Schools, home to the Sages. Gaining this Discipline implies a kind of education, though not necessarily the disposition of a Scholar. Sages are only one class that practice this Discipline. Knights of greater and lesser repute also learn Binding but as a practice of means rather than art.

**The Practice of Spirit Binding**
Gaining the Discipline grants the Rite of Binding, the Activity one uses to initiate and exchange the Pacts that one currently has with any Spirits.
*Spirits Bound*
	Spirits dwell within their Binder, granting them Powers in exchange for Light. What Spirits you might Bind are limited only by your knowledge and means.
*Light*
	Light is a Resource found in the Wilds, a tangible essence of the life-giving and spiritual power of the world. Light is held in Gems, and expended to Invoke the Powers of Spirits.
*Lesser Spirits*
	Mitha are dim things, manifestations of their Name and want for little more than Light. Spirit Binders are only limited in the number they may Bind at once. 
****
	*Limit of Lesser Spirits*: (Twice your Training in Spirit Binding)
****
*Greater Spirits*
	Akthe have wills, some equal or even greater than that of the People. Binding an akthe demands a form of conduct in kind and is more taxing upon the Binder.
- *Limits*: Each Akthe Bound is a Familiar, and Binders may only have a single one at first.
- *Bonds*: Every Akthe has a Bond, a demand for their Binder. Violating these Frustrates the Bond you have with your Familiar. You lose access to your Powers from that Familiar while Frustrated, and must appease them to regain that capacity.
*Kinds of Spirit*
	Binding Spirits has a notable exception, Caerns are in a sense already Bound to a place. Consequently, you cannot Bind a Caern (though many Caerns will grant Powers to Spirit Binders in their service, just not within the form of Rite and Bond.)
*Powers*
	All Spirits grant the Binder Powers, Magical Effects they may use by Invoking them. Powers consume Light from your stores (worn in Infused Gems on your person.) 
- *Active Powers*: Invoked once, Active Powers take effect and evaporate soon after use.
- *Passive Powers*: After Invoking a Passive Power, you gain its effects Permanently, until you release it (a 0_AP Reaction to anything at all.) However, Passive Powers cannot be multiplied above a certain amount.
****
	*Limit of Passive Powers*: (Up to your Training.)
****
- *Innate Powers*: Granted by your Familiars, Innate Powers are unique because you never have to Invoke them, needn't spend Light to access them, and do not count against your Passives.
*Invoking Powers and Tiers*
	Invoking a Power is an 1_AP Action (unless the Power says it isn't). When Invoking a Power, you choose the Tier you'd like to Invoke it at. When Invoking a Power, you must spend Light equal to the Tier.
****
	*Limit of Power Tier*: (Twice your Training)
****

#### The Practicality of Binding
****

**Light**
This is a Resource of peerless import for Knights; the soul means of fueling their Powers and making Pacts with Spirits. 
*Light in the World*
	Naturally occuring Wellsprings are where almost all Light in circulation originates. Unfortunately, these are easily exhausted and unreliable as a rule. Thus, one of the major goals of any Caravan is locating and gaining Light from a Wellspring.
*The Cost of Light*
	Per Measure, the Resource Cost of Light is *10 Gilt.*
*Containment*
	Light is an effervescent, nearly weightless liquid in the natural condition; making its transportation and collection a dubious task by means of a box or sack, able to sleep through nearly any hole. Light has a natural affinity for Gems, soaking into their structures and lighting fires within their facets. Particularly well-cut and shaped Gems are able to hold more. Syphons use this principle to draw Light from Wellspring as if by suction.

**Ampules**
Devised to carry Light in confined and stable conditions, Ampules are rated by their basic materials, their ability to hold Light and price. Knights often spend quite a bit of their income on these, especially at the start of their vocation.
- *Sard*
	- Bulk: 2| Capacity: 3| Price: 100
- *Onyx*
	- Bulk: 2| Capacity: 6| Price: 600
- *Sapphire*
	- Bulk: 3| Capacity: 10| Price: 3,000
- *Emerald*
	- Bulk: 3| Capacity: 30| Price: 10,000
- *Ruby* 
	- Bulk: 3| Capacity: 60| Price:  60,000
- *Diamond*
	- Bulk: 6| Capacity: 100| Price: 1,000,000
*Attunement and Drawing Light from an Ampule*
	One of them significant features of an Ampule is its confinement, as compared to a raw Infused Gem. Only the one holding the Attuned Key (usually a small gem-chip on an amulet, bracer, etc.) can draw Light from an Ampule. Raw Infused Gems, on the other hand, can be drawn from at a range equal to thrice the Creature's Tier.
*The Knight's Tithe*
	In the case that one lacks an Ampule, or any Light, the custom dictates a stipend of 21 Light (distributed across seven Sard Ampules) be issued at the setting off of a Caravan. (If the Knight wishes, and has Ampules to spare, they can receive the Light with 700 Gilt.) Likewise, Knights credited in the acquisition of a Wellspring receive a Tithe of one to one Hundred Light gained.
