Verdun scripture is fractured, shards of ancient history, carefully bound together in "The Book of Wheels". Maintained by Nous' Orators, this document represents the collective efforts of the Arks in the last half-millennia to reconstruct their past. 

The Book is organized by subject, and frequently split into smaller sections for use in practical efforts or instruction. For example, the Wheel of Law is often printed as an individual document, held close by Paladins, while the Wheel of Ages and Wheel of Wheels tend to be kept together and used in instructing children.

One of the striking features of these works is their anonymity, the canon of the Book of Wheels requires that the included text be stripped of an author. The opening passage of the Wheel of Wheels, the putative first authored section states:
> *"As Al'Haori stepped beyond the World, enclosing his way and leaving the surface of it clean, so too are pages clean, its letters free from all but meaning. To speak into the Wheel, one must step behind it, to vanish from the sight of its words. Only in silence can the word retain its perfect intention"*
#### The Wheel of the Ages
****
Usually a preface, this explains the history of the Verdun, as they know it, mostly concerning the Doom and the ensuing construction of the Arks. Six hundred and forty four years have passed, and the first hundred or so are empty, insofar as the records of them have been lost.

**Concerning the Age of Providence**
*"In the Age before, when the sky was endless azure, the Kings built great palaces, surrounding them with cities, immense and adorned with ten thousand hues. We spoke many tongues in these days, clad as to speak of our enmities"*

*"In these days, the Gift was named in truth, for those the Aether blessed could call its name, could see their will unfold as if speaking Al'Haori's own tongue; this was before air itself turned bitter"*

*"The Gods were pleased in our workings, we worshipped the Seven and the lost brother, their Mother, Gaia, and did honor to the silent Father Sky. It was said their servants walked among us then, both those of spirit and those of sparkling substance"*

*"This Age did not lack for strife, for the great Enemy below was awake, its Demons schemed and unleashed fearful troubles whom they could. The plagues and monsters of the Fly, the Empress' murderous plots, all of these were our ancient foe, that against which we mounted our greatest effort, our grand folly"*

**Concerning the Company of Fools**
*"In the Age before, there was a great nation, whose Prince commanded a Company of Thousand warriors, surrounded by thirty three Gifted magician Scholars, whose words could unleash Creation, and guarded by thirty three holy men and women, whose Virtue was the will of the Gods"*

*"The Emperor came into the twilight of life, finding himself with two children, the Prince and his firstborn, Istera, who was a great Scholar, and whose mother he had loved, surpassing that of the Prince, who would succeed him by customary right. So he summoned the Prince and spoke, saying 'Son of mine, you have made a great name for yourself, terrible to our enemies, but in your inmost thoughts, can you find even one who should not bow his head?' and the Prince rejoined 'Father mine, I and my Company are without equal, in Sorcery and Virtue we have no peer, and in heroism, no head may rise to the level of the Thousand Sons.'"*

*"Hearing this, for the Emperor was a man of capacity, both love and terrible cunning, spoke again. 'Fruit of my own victories, do the demons themselves bow to you? I see your eyes glitter and think what pride might undo when I am gone. Address me, my own son, and admit what faults you must, or show me the Lord of Abisa, Abbadon, at your feet. Should I see neither, Istera will rule.' and the Prince left in silence, eyes shining cold, stars in the emptiness beyond"*

**Concerning Abbadon and its Minions**
*"In the depths of the earth, lay the place of the enemy, called Abisa. Its king was named Abbadon, the ruler of the depths and lord of the void. Other than its name, none could be spoken of it, but its servants were many and known, in infamy they reigned across the realms between those of sky and fire."*

*"The Demonic Hosts were led by greater beings, stooping monsters greater than a single body, these lords of Hell fashioned the Infernal beings in their image, as well as the foul Orcs and all monsters who would terrorize the children of Gaia."*

**Concerning the Doom**
*"When the Prince returned, his eyes were dark, as were those of his counselors and remaining host. The Company had plumbed Hell to its deepest, the pit of the dread master. Though he did not bring the body of Abbadon with him, nor would he speak a reason"*

*"At that time, the sky was written with ten thousand portents, signs of woe known only to the keepers of the ways of stars. They hid these signs from all but those who would build the Arks"*

*"And as Gaia was broken, a great darkness enclosed the sky, a Shield in Heaven, through which nothing can be seen, neither moon or star. At the six cardinal points of this lie the Beacons, from which comes the echo of the sun"*
#### Khaoi'ikhon - The Wheel of Wheels
****
The first scripture describes Creation, the origin of the cosmos from Al'Haori, the Thinker. The primary concepts to be grasped, per the Scholarship of the Arks, are the underlying metaphysical reality, the six Aspects of Creation, which are the Six Great Wheels, the Celestial Bodies, which are the Solar Wheels, and the Unseen places, which are the Wheels Invisible.

**Concerning the Six Great Wheels**
*"And at the inception of Creation, Al'Haori divided his Thought, making each of its parts into a Great Wheel"*

*"Time, the conception of sequence, and of durations great and finite, these formed the span of Wheel of Ages, Tempus"*

*"Space, the geometry of where, here, and there, of touch and emptiness, these became the Wheel of Structures, Cel"*

*"Power, to cause and to be caused, becoming and doing, these are the Wheel of Forces, Rakvis"*

*"Life, the principle of animation and self construction, the greatest of the six, this is the Wheel of Souls, Daimos"*

*"Aether, that which is before everything, the intermediary between Al'Haori's Thought and Being, the Wheel of Emptiness Istari"*

*"Change, the last of the Great Wheels, which is the cycle and nature of act and potentiality, this is the Wheel of Forms Dynamis"*

****

**Concerning the Solar Wheels**
*"After the division of his Thought, did Al'haori fashion others from the wonder of each Great Wheel, the grand spoke and axis of this was the Sun, the pure light of Power, Space and Time and Change orchestrated their motions, Aether divided the boundaries of them, and upon a few, he invested Life. Each Solar Wheel is as a Branch to the point of the Sun"*

*"In the great depth, Al'Haori placed an insignificant body, a Wheel of ice and darkness to circumscribe the Sun's Splendor, this was called Ek'Zin"*

*"There was a curtain thrown about the span of Gaia, whose glittering shield cloaked it from fallen stars, this was called Rama"*

*"Within the Axis of the Sun, the World was made, blessed and fruitful, each turn of the Six Great was in the honor of this, the crown jewel of all Creation, Gaia"*

*"Beyond the Curtain, he made a grander Wheel, whose face was in eternal turmoil, from thence came the Outer God, Ventus"*

*"About the apex of Gaia, was there Li, the Wheel of the Star, unmoving, about its span, Mana, the Wheel of Moon, upon which the Gods would build their kingdom"*

*"Within the Span of the Sun, a Wheel of fire, an ever moving light of terrible swiftness"*

*"When Al'Haori had stepped beyond, it was said by the Gods, out of all his fashionings, 'what is this stranger here?' for they had seen Hao'Kan, the Wheel from another place"*

****
The following passages describes a more abstract notion, that of other planes of existence, some of which are taken to constitute an afterlife, others with less clear concepts.

**Concerning the Wheels Invisible**
*"Al'Haori's Thought far exceeded mortal grasp, and more were fashioned than the Wheels of the realms of life, for there are known six unseen Wheels, and it is said countless are yet unfathomed"*

*"Within the depth of Gaia, the place of the enemy, the dwelling of the great beast, Abbadon, which was also called the Hinge of the World, it was called Abisa which was the point of the great folly"*
> Verdun Scholars and Priests tend to argue that Abisa was a real location, something plundered by the Company of Fools. Likewise, both agree that its remains still exist, Abyssal Shards infested with Demons and other monsters.

*"In his Wisdom, Al'Haori fashioned the World to cast away impurity and all defect. Those spiteful ghosts, those unsouled, they fall to the Wheel of mourning, that realm of dead skies and sepulchers. Herod is the name, unspoken"*
> The lowest realm of the afterlife, Herod tends to be conceptualized as a place punishment by Priests, something inflicted on impure souls. Perhaps owing to the very popular epithet that all Mages are impure, Scholars tend to disagree with this, arguing that, if Herod exists at all, the passage is a metaphor for the dissolution of the Soul into nothingness.

*"The paradise of Blessed Souls, Caello, those above the Sky who won joy, it was from this Golden Wheel that Al'Haori fashioned the Gods' servants"*
> Famously a realm of heroes and legends, less is known about the exact meaning of this passage, which the only mention known of Caello. The rumors of contact with Celestials and Priests give contradictory pictures, the former describing a vast world of supernatural mechanisms, and the latter a palatial expanse of sunbeamed halls, wherein the only sounds are music and silence.

*"The quiet world of ever night, walked by those whose lives were not sung, neither in praise or sorrow, Gadal was given to Luna, who gave the judgement of the dead to Mora, receiving those who the Lady of Death would neither castigate nor acclaim"*
> This is the best understood realm, as the Priests of Luna have the ability to bodily enter the Evernight. Investigation by Scholar-Mages often speak of a vastness there, as if it were much larger than it appeared from within. The Ways of the Evernight are thought to divide the realm, like the tales of the moon and its twin sides. The living walk the closer of the two to the waking daylight.

*"Before he stepped beyond the threshold, Al'Haori fashioned Tamrin, a realm of Gods, a timeless remove at the center of Creation from which all that could is seen"*
> The location, embodiment, and exact nature of the Gods is extremely contentious, so much that the exact interpretation of Tamrin is left as a issue of personal faith, if not an almost willful ignorance. Those less faithful are equally split, as the evidence provided in experience and the scant passages about it is sparse.

*"When Creation was finished, the Thinker stepped behind a door of shining dark, the span of this door is Hao, who shuts the way"*
#### The Branches of the Wheel
****

**Concerning the Origins of the Gods**
*"Upon the making of the world, and the creation of the seven-fold thousand Servants, each of their Choir and purpose, Al'Haori spoke his final words, two names from which would come everything thereafter, ancient Sky, Khaoi, and Allmother Gaia"*

*"From the two words, were born Lumen and Luna, whose powers were Sun and Moon, from them came Telmar, Ventus, Maran, Sylvan, Mora, and Manus, from the last two came first Nous, keeper of the Book of Wheels, and Bella"*

**Concerning the Authorship of the Book**
*"We are the Nameless, servants of the Storyteller whose glory is to be written into the Book, we are the few, we remember"*

**Concerning the Wheel of the Sun**
*"The Sons of Lumen would lead, would guide the children of Gaia and care for their council. Kings and rulers are bound by them, those who would judge the writers of the law"*

**Concerning the Wheel of the Moon**
*"In the Shadow, the Mirror's Chosen are called to know, to disclose the hidden things to those that are owed it, and to conceal from the unrighteous all the greater things"*

**Concerning the Wheel of Law**
*"As the law is authored by kings in Virtue, the law is read by the Paladin, his hammer both justifies and adjudicates it, for the judgement of all save the law's author is his"*

**Concerning the Wheel of Death**
*"When body unleashes its hold on the soul, men ask, whence; it is Mora who is the harvester and guide of the dead, and her Friends of Death are for the living, both to council and to protect against the violations of the mortal order"*

**Concerning the Wheel of Creation**
*"The Forge Priest is thrice blessed, his hands wield the Light of Creation, from this he makes cities, implements, and clean water, with these can he alone forge a people"*

**Concerning the Wheel of Fire**
*"Lastborn, Bella, whose Hospitallers are given the greatest gift, the Flame of Life, her two faces reflect man's origin and end; the man that kills one of her faithful is damned twice"*
