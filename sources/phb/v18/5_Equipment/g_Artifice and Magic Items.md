Fusions of Arcane study and practical handiwork, Magic Items are manufactured by Artificers to serve a host of purposes within the Ark and beyond.

#### Magical Items
****
Artificers create a wide array of Items for daily and special use aboard the Ark and in the Sea of Shards beyond. A Magical Item is a special case of Item that can only be produced by Artifice, and consumes Residue.

**Activating a Magical Item**
All Magical Items must be Activated to function. Unless stated as being part of another Action, or an automatic function, Activating a Magic Item is a 1 AP Interaction.

**Residual Consumption and Storage**
Being that all Magical Items consume Residue, they also are equipped to store it. In general, Magical Items consume a listed Activation cost, and can store up to 10 times that amount.

#### Arcane Automata
****
Imitations of life, patterned from Celestials, these metal bodies move of their own accord. Imprinted with an Intent by a Mage, an Automaton neither thinks nor acts of its own will. Rather, the internal echo of its makers directs its every motion.

**Automata in the World**
An Automaton is technically a Creature, but of a peculiar kind. They lack Mind and, consequently, are immune to Stress, Panic, and Corruption. Automata are unable to Bleed.

**Automata in Action**
An Automaton only acts at the behest oh their maker. A Command for them must be very simple, requiring an Action of a single type with no specific qualifiers but a Target: "Move there." "Attack that." Each Command is a 1 AP Communication, and Automata cannot execute more than a single Command at once. 

Consequently, an Automata can be Commanded to follow a thing, to a approach it, to Attack it, to lift it, etc. But not any of these things at once. *As a general rule, an Automata can spend AP on its Turn performing the Command, say, "Attack that" Striking or Shooting as it may for as many AP as it has. However, if the Target Moved away or out of its line of sight, the Automata could do nothing until Commanded, as a Movement Action is a distinct Command from "Attack that".*

**Fuel**
Automata are machines that require constant energy intake. Each Model has a given Need, which must be met Daily or it will shut down.

**Breakage**
Automata suffer Notches like other Objects. Their Durability is calculated like the Reserve of a Creature.

#### Augmented Equipment
****
Similar to Magic Items, Magical Augments can be applied to otherwise mundane Equipment. Inscribing an Augmentation Rune is undertaken in the same manner as normal Artifice, taking the Item as an input.

When Inscribed with an Augmentation, the Item in Question becomes Magical and cannot receive any more Runes. However, you can perform Artifice on such an Item to replace an existing Augmentation.
