Single-use Items are Consumable, destroyed after being used.

## Breathing
****
The world beyond the Arks retains an atmosphere, sealed under the Shield of Heaven. Like the Aether bound to it, this air is twisted and foul, unwholesome for those not changed in the Doom.

Verdun cannot breath the Miasma of the Shard Sea, and must carry clean Air with them to be able to venture on Expeditions.
#### Flasks
****
> *Price*: 10, 2 for a recharge of clean air.
A lesser product of Artifice manufactured by the Mages' Guild, a Flask holds pressurized clean Air, loaded from larger tanks aboard an Airship. Each Flask holds enough to support a grown Verdun for half a day (3 Opportunity Points worth of Activities, or 12 Hours.)

*At the start of their first Expedition, 3 of these are provided to Expeditioners by the Guild.*
#### The Mask
****
> *Price*: 30
Wrapped around the mouth and nose, the Mask conducts clean air from a Flask.

*Like Flasks, Expeditioners are Provided with a Mask on their first Expedition at the expense of the Guild.*

## Active Consumables and Poisons
****
**The Edible and Venomous**
Alcohols, Tonics, Medicines, and Poisons are compounds that are expended on Ingestion, called a Dose.
- *Active_x*: 
> - Items with this Trait have Effects which apply for the Duration, x.
> - Each smallest effective instance of exposure is a Dose.
> - Multiplication, say by consuming several Active Items, multiplies the Dosage.
- *Addictive_x*: 
> - Over-use of Addictive substances can lead to dependance.
> - Every time you use an Addictive substance, roll x Chaos Dice. If you roll more 6s up than your Mind Bonus, you are Addicted. 
- *Deadly_x*: 
> - These have the potential to harm or kill by exposure.
> - When exposed to a Deadly substance, roll Chaos Dice equal to x-(your Height rounded down.) Each 6 rolled Poisons you for 1 Minute, or inflicts its Overdose Effect for as long. *If you roll more 6s than thrice your Body Bonus, you die.*
> - Each Consecutive Dose of any Deadly substance within 24 Hours is more Deadly by 1 (These do not have to be the same substances.)
- *Contact*: 
> - Some substances are ingested on Contact with the blood, skin, or membranes.
> - Contact substances can be applied to blades or projectiles, but are consumed after Injuring something, or 24 Hours.
- *Edible*:
> - Consumables with this Trait are ingested orally. 
- *Inhalant*:
> - Consumables with this Trait are ingested by inhaling them. 
> - You can avoid ingestion as long as you Hold your Breath.
