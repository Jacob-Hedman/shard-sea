#### On The Kinds of Movement 
---
Creatures have a Movement; a value with expresses how many Yards they can move with their Movement Actions. Beyond this value, Movement can be grouped into subcategories which indicate how the Creature moves and where. Movement Actions and Actions which call for Movement use a Speed. Speeds (walking, swimming, etc.) modify your Movement when used. Which Speed such Actions use is usually up you.  There are Speeds, to which practically everything has access, and unique Speeds, which only some particular creatures can use. 
##### Universal Speeds
****
**Running/Walking**
Striding, stalking, crawling, slithering, etc. on the ground. Running Speed is equal to your Movement, but can only be used to move horizontally or up slopes, stairs, etc. 

**Climbing**
Clambering up a cliff, scaling a ladder, etc. Climbing Speed is equal to half you Movement. 

**Swimming**
Fording a river, crossing a lake, or delving into a water-filled cave. Swimming Speed is equal to one third of your Movement. 
#### Difficult Terrain
---
Beyond the obvious obstacles like walls and doors, there are many kinds of situations in which the environment you move on or through penalizes your Movement.

Difficult Terrain (shifting mud, knee high water, etc.) has a Tier and costs extra Movement to pass through equal to its Tier.
