## The Kinds of "Things"
---
**Things**
Objects, Creatures, and things, all refer to the items in the world of everyday objects. "Thing" is the broadest of these categories. Everything is a "thing" and all things have a weight in Bulk.

**Creatures**
Creatures are animate, alive, or otherwise mobile and active things and can have Ranks in Skills, Styles, Abilities, etc. "Creature" thus, usually means: "has a statblock or Character Sheet." Anything that is not a Creature is an object. 

**Objects**
Objects are inanimate, or otherwise inactive Things. Objects have a Hardness, representing their basic resistance to Damage, and a Durability. If an Object receives Damage, compare that Damage to its Hardness as if it were DT. If the Damage is higher, the Object is Notched. If an Object suffers Notches exceeding its Durability, it is destroyed.
- If the Damage is twice its Hardness or more, it suffers 1d6 Notches, thrice for 2d6, etc.

Determining the Hardness and Durability of any Object is fairly simple, as it is determined by its composition. Brittle materials have low Durability, but may have a very high Hardness, while very durable objects, like a pile of dirt, may have very low Hardness and exceedingly high Durability. You can find some examples below:
****
- *Material*: Paper   |*Durability*: 1 
- *Material*: Dirt    |*Durability*: 5 
- *Material*: Wood    |*Durability*: 3 
- *Material*: Plastic |*Durability*: 4 
- *Material*: Bronze  |*Durability*: 5 
- *Material*: Stone   |*Durability*: 4 
- *Material*: Iron    |*Durability*: 6 
- *Material*: Steel   |*Durability*: 7 
- *Material*: Titanium|*Durability*: 10
- *Material*: Diamond |*Durability*: 1
