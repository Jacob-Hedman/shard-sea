#### What Do you See
****

**The Concept of Targeting**
When you use an Action, Activity, or act in any way that points to, points at, effects in some direct way (not an AOE), or implies your apprehension of something, you are Targeting it. That is, it (Object, Item, or Creature) is the Target of your Action. 
*How do I Target?*
> When something is not in Cover from you, you Detect it, meaning you may freely Target it. If something does have Cover from you, you can attempt to Spot it ([[6.0 SHARDS/DOC/Shards of Life/SHARDS PHB/3_Playing the Game/a_Actions | Actions]]). Otherwise, you may not Target something in Cover.
*What has Cover?*
> If you can See or Touch it, you Detect it. What you Detect has no Cover from you. (Partially true only in the case of Hard Cover, which can still block Damage.)
*The Range of the Senses*
- You can See within Meters up to 10 times your Reflex.
- You can Touch within your Engagement (longer if your Unarmed Attacks have Reach.)

#### Cover and Obstruction
---
When something obstructs your Vision, anything behind it is in Cover from you. A very wide range of things can obstruct, like walls, darkness, bushes, trees, smoke, mist, etc.  

Practically anything that would obscure what you can see can count as Cover, adjudicated by the GM.

**Hard and Soft Cover**
Not all Cover is made equal. Darkness blocks your Vision, but isn't going to stop a blade. 
- Soft Cover obstructs, but provides no protection from harm when Spotted.
- Hard Cover is between you and a Target, and is hit before the Target could be (just like anything else in Line of Sight between you, like a window or Barrier.)
- Hard Cover is effectively Armor with Rating-All and a Durability adjudicated by its composition.
*Hard Cover Durability Quick Reference*
> - Wood: 2
> - Stone: 4
> - Steel: 6
> - Adamant: 10
*Breaking Cover*
> Just like Armor, when Hard Cover is Broken, the Target takes the remaining Damage. For example, a wood Barrier is penetrated by Impaling Damage (Base_3) and deals the remaining Damage to the Target (Impaling_1).

**Cover Quick Reference**
- Can you See or Touch it?
> - *Yes*: No Cover
> - *No*: Yes Cover
- Would the obstacle obstruct a projectile?
> - *Yes*: Hard Cover
> - *No*: Soft Cover
*Example Cases*
> - A shroud of smoke or fog: `Soft Cover`
> - The corner of a building: `Hard cover`
> - A bush: `Soft Cover`
> - Diffuse shrubbery: `Soft Cover`
> - Total darkness: `Soft Cover`
## Heights and Falling
---

Falling, naturally, occurs whenever you are no longer touching the ground and are not propelled or dragged upward. Each round (6 second interval) falling, you will move 180 Meters down. 

**Falling in Combat**
In Initiative, because Initiative is an abstraction of simultaneous events, Falling Creatures don't drop until the Round Ends, or until forced in some way.
*Voluntarily Falling*
> At the option of the falling Creature, they can opt to drop the entire Distance when they begin to fall instead.

**Fall Damage**
When you land from a Fall with a Height greater than your Body and Reflex Bonus, you suffer Crushing Damage.
*Base Damage*
> Crushing Damage has a Base of 1, and every two meters after the first makes this more Vicious. In other words, Falling Damage is equal to half the number of Meters fallen.
## Extreme Environments
---
Extreme environments that would not kill you outright are classified by Extremity, ranging by kind, hot, cold, etc. 

**Cold**
A tier 1 Extreme Cold environment would similar to an arboreal winter day, while a tier 6 Extreme Cold environment would be like the center of an Arctic blizzard. 
- When Exposed to Extreme Cold, you suffer Strain equal to its Tier until you leave or gain a precaution like a fire or warm gear.
- Extreme Cold of Tier 6 and above inflicts Frost Damage each Turn. This is more Vicious at 7 and above.
- If you become Unconscious while in Extreme Cold, you die within Minutes equal to 10-T.

**Heat**
Tier 1 Extreme Heat environment would feel like a desert wind, while a Tier 6 Extreme Heat environment would seem like air above flowing magma.
- When Exposed to Extreme Heat, you suffer Strain equal to its Tier until you leave or gain a precaution, although Heat is harder to alleviate than Cold.
- Extreme Heat of Tier 6 and above inflicts Incendiary Damage each Turn. This is more Vicious at 7 and above.
- If you become Unconscious while in Extreme Heat, you die within Hours equal to 10-T.

*There can be other kinds of Extreme environments than hot and cold, but they will have their own descriptions and effects.* 

#### Precautions Against Heat and Cold
****
The simplest precaution against any Extreme Environment is leaving or Items with the right Extremis Quality, but Heat and Cold have a special relationship as well. Warming or cooling effects can blunt or worsen their respective Tiers and Heat is subtracted from Cold, and vice versa.

#### the Hazard Posed by Fire and Spaces which are Burning
---
Spaces which are Burning pose understandable risks to Creatures and Objects within them. A Space is considered Burning at GM discretion or when otherwise specified. A good rule of thumb is that Spaces containing candles are not Burning, while Spaces containing a campfire, a fireplace, a Burning scenery, etc. almost always are. Starting your Turn or entering a Burning Space inflicts Incendiary Damage.
#### Breathing
---
Breathing is obviously an essential activity but is also something that one can briefly abstain from. Holding your breath is a 0 AP General Action which you can End.

Voluntary or not, lack of breath is always considered Suffocation. While Suffocating, you roll a Chaos Die at the end of each Turn, suffering 1 Persistent Strain on face 6. If you ever become Incapacitated from Strain and cannot begin breathing again (when submerged, in a vacuum, or lacking fresh air) you die within 1 Minute.
## Food and Drink
---
Food and water are requisite for life. The following rules apply when tracking food and water consumption (when exploring the open wilderness, in times of scarcity, etc.)
#### Water
****
Water, the colorless, odorless and tasteless substance that fills rivers, lakes and streams, is, in most cases, readily available. You only need to track it in some select environments like hot deserts, else, assume you manage to find enough and so easily as to avoid note in game. If water is scarce, you risk suffering effects from thirst in the following ways.

If you spend a Day without drinking, you suffer 3 Persistent Strain that can only be removed by drinking. At the end of the next Day, you suffer 6 of the same. At the end of the third, you die.
#### Food
****
Food, usually solid and organic matter masticated with the jaws and swallowed for nutrition, should be tracked more carefully as there are far more opportunities for scarcity. The basic unit of this resource is the Ration, which contains roughly 2,000 calories and sustains you for one day without penalty from hunger. Consuming less than this number, but not zero, contributes half the time towards the penalties from hunger as eating nothing would. 

A Day without food inflicts a point of Persistent Strain which is only removed by eating. You suffer another point at the end of 3 Days without food, and another at the end of a Week, and every Week thereafter. If you ever suffer enough Persistent Strain from hunger to Incapacitate you, you die.
