## Pacts, the Binder's Arsenal
****
Of the many strange abilities derived from the Abyss, the Binding Pact is the most recognizable. A power of the Principality of Greed, Binding Pacts leverage the Souls of the mortals who swear them. During the Conquest, the Arch-Demons used this power, initially, to Bind traitors to their cause. This is the same reason why some few of them survived Abaddon's defeat. 

Arch-Demons were aspects of Abbadon itself, sharing its essence and, by consequence, its end. However, it was discovered that those Demons Bound to mortals survived, if in a bizarre, diminished state.

As the Demons fought the Company of Fools, many Verdun regard the Demons, especially the Arch-Demons with mixed judgements. While they remain Unholy by nature, Binding them is not forbidden for Expeditioners, though it is almost always forbidden within the Arks proper. In the Shard Sea, almost any advantage is something worth considering, even those with high prices. 

#### Binding as Discipline
****
The first step in becoming a Binder is a Demonic parallel of the Mage. Instead of investing the body with Residue, the Binder trades a part of their Soul. The First Pact makes one a Binder, as well as permanently rendering them an Unholy Creature, with all the accompanying interactions. 

The First Pact is made with an Imp of the Principality of greed. These rat-like beings are the prototypical Binders. Literally sat on their shoulder, the Imp is the primary focus of the Binder's art.

**Applications of Skill**
Binding involves learning the Infernal Language, as well as the ability to decode cyphers written therein. Likewise, Binders are made Unholy and deal with Demons, making them natural students of the un-wholesome generally. Binding can be used for Specialized Tests in any of these domains, especially cyphers and Unholy Relics.

**Passive Effects: The Imp**
Mechanically, your Imp is just that. Their Tier is equal to your own. The First Pact inflicts 1 point of Permanent Corruption on you, the currency of Binding. They serve you and act as the partner in your Invocations, both with the Arch-Demons otherwise. Their Pact with you obligates total obedience to your commands. They offer this in return for the link to your Soul, which is something their masters use to survive, and you can use to forge Pacts with them.

Demons have no Souls and, by consequence, lack the free choice of Verdun. Your Imp's nature is immutable, a Creature of schemes and bargains. Your relationship may be as pleasant or fiery as you wish, but the Law remains; they will never repent their ways, and may never disobey you directly.

**Demonic Invokations**
The Infernal parallel of Magic, Invokations are arts of the Soul and fire. Your Imp and link both grant you the ability to engage in these rites. You will learn more on your path as a Binder, but the first comes at initiation:

Invokation: **The Lesser Rule of Blood**
> *"All are equal, one before the bite of the knife"*
> Paying a price of harm and agony, you call another to your aid.
> `Assuming your Imp has been slain, you can spend an entire day and 3 Corruption to Invoke another. This is a new Creature, but the rite grants it all the memories of its predecessor.`

Exclusive Activity - **Invokation of Greater Binding**
> *"Spirit both pale and terrible, awesome and dark, hear me, to the mirror I invoke you, let its silver be your eyes, its crystal glass a mouth open unto Hell itself"*
> `Spend one Watch. You draw in and form an Unholy compact with an Arch-Demon, a spirit of the Abyss. When you do so, you consent to the terms and consequences layed out by the entity, generally the same for all Binders, though some might twist and adjust theirs with the right leverage.`
> *Might*
> `All Pacts have a Strength, up to your Training in Binding, decided when you accept it. Pacts also have a Duration, equal to their Strength in Days.`
> *Penalty*
> `When you Violate the Terms of a Pact, you suffer Corruption equal to its Strength. If this ever exceeds the maximum allowed amount (Your Tier times 10) you Fall, becoming something less than Verdun in the power of the Demons that Bound you.`
> *Trespass*
> `It is technically possible to use this Invokation to dissolve a Pact, a Violation that inflicts twice its Strength in Corruption.`
> *Collaboration*: As a solitary act, there is no solidarity to be found in Invoking Arch-Demons.

## Arch-Demons
****
#### Sargatanas, the Knight Aflame
****
> *"Noble Spirit of honor and revenge, we invoke you from your grand house, we call you from the depths and invite you in. We promise you auspicious hosts and great deeds. Dwell in us and grant us your valor."*

Sargatanas was the Grand Brigadier of the Abyssal armies. In the battle with the Company of Fools, his forces were overmatched and he dispatched articles of surrender signed in blood. It is said that this was the first Contract of Binding, which preserved him from the ravages of the Doom.

To Bind Sargatanas, one must impress him with their courage. In exchange, they are inured to blades and given great skill with all martial implements.

**The Pact**
Those Bound to Sargatanas are obligated to accept all challenges, and to pursue strife.
`Fleeing, stepping down from a challenge, and acts of dishonor are all violations of his Pact. If you Fall to Sargatanas' Pact, you can choose to either die instantly, or transform into a Demon under his control forever.`

In exchange, you gain unmatched skill with all martial implements.
`Sargatanas' Pact can be used to make or resist Attacks with any Weapon, as if using a Skill. (The DC to Attack you becomes 8+Pact Strength, and your Attacks are 2d6+Pact Strength.) These Attacks cannot be Resisted by any Skill. When an Enemy fails a Melee Attack against your Pact Strength, they Provoke an Opportunity Attack from you.`
#### Beelzebub, the Fly Unmade
****
> *"Oh voracious, Spirit of proliferation and hunger, we invoke you into our homes, we entice you from the earth with the scent of a thousand feasts. We promise you warmth and blood. Dwell in us and instruct us the art of consumption."*

The Overseer of the Basins of Rot. Beelzebub raised locusts, plagues, and all kinds of horror from these wells of putrescence. Beelzebub is dead, an exquisite corpse which continually overflows with waves of vilest hunger.

Binders discovered these occult remains soon after the Doom. His undying, flagitious essence can be Bound with a simple promise, subsistence, and in exchange provides protection and inevitable transformations.

**The Pact**
Those Bound to the Fly are compelled to eat, to consume flesh and the remains of their foes.
`Failing to consume at least some of an Enemy's corpse after death or refusing to consume meat when available is a violation of his Pact. If you Fall to his Pact, you instantly transform into a violent, inchoate mass of flies that attempts to consume any and every living thing it can percieve.`

In exchange, the Bound are inured to poisons and earthly harms, if by means most would find disturbing.
`Beelzebub's Pact gradually transforms the body into a roiling mass of insects, beginning with maggots that hatch from their flesh. This grants you benefits at the following thresholds:`
- 1: `You subtract your Pact Strength from your Strain and roll all Injury Risk with a Boon.`
- 3: `You gain an Unarmed Attack with your Jaws: Melee, +1_Accuracy, Cutting, Vicious.` 
> - `When you inflict an Injury or hit a Corpse with this Attack, you shed half your Strain or 1 Persistent Strain.`
- 6: `When Damaged, you can shift into a cloud of flies as a 1 AP Reaction. This makes Cutting, crushing, and Piercing Damage Blunt against you for one Round. While in this form you gain a Flying Speed up to your Movement and can fit through gaps as small as 10 CM. You can also adopt this form with a Minute long incantation for 10 Minutes.`

#### Agaliarept, the Sorcerer
****
> *"Spirit of intellect and subtlety, we invoke you with the name of whispers. We promise our secrets, to break the cyphers and to plunder the hidden place in your name. Dwell in us and lend us the virtue of your eyes."*

Abbadon's armies did not act blindly. In the shadow, traitors and demons alike piled secrets on the altar of the Abyss' lord of whispers. After the Doom, Agaliarept found new and willing servants in the Binders of the Shard Sea.

Agaliarept's Pact is easier than most, owing to the nature of the Sorcerer himself, always eager to find new and more obscure lore. To this end, he provides Binders with several of his Hexes, able to open hidden ways and expand the limits of vision.

**The Pact**
Those Bound to Agaliarept gain power in exchange for secrets, both great and small. There is almost no violation of his Pact, yet there are greater limits on its use.
`When you learn a Secret, you gain a Charge which can be spent on any of Agaliarept's Hexes. You can hold a number of these up to thrice your Pact Strength. You can technically use a Hex without spending Charges, but this is a Violation of his Pact. Falling to agaliarept's Pact is simply lethal, killing you instantly.`

The Hexes of Abbadon's Sorcerer are subtle, useful for finding and keeping hidden things.
- *The Subtle Eye*`| Charges: 1| Effects: You Touch an Object in Range, leaving an invisible Magical Eye. You can relocate your vision to the Magical Eye at any time as a 1 AP General Action. It can see in the dark as if it were day. You can only have Magical Eyes up to your Pact Strength and can disolve any you wish with the same Action.`
- *The Cloak of Shadows*`| Charges: 2| Effects: You can make yourself invisible for the next Minute as a !0 AP Reaction. This makes you impervious to vision.`
- *The Erzatz Tongue*`| Charges: 3| Effects: For the next Minute, you can speak and hear from a remote location as if there yourself. This can be in any place you know, with any Creature you have met or seen, or with any Object you have ever touched.`
- *The Dissolution of Cyphers*`| Charges: 4| Effects: Touch a page or so worth of text as a 1 AP Interaction. You instantly understand its contents unless it is a Magical cypher with a Tier greater than your Pact Strength.`
- *The Silver Key*`| Charges: 5| Effects: Touch a lock as a 1 Ap Interaction. Unless it is Magical with a Tier greater than your Pact Strength, it opens instantly. You can remotely re-seal it once as a 0 AP Reaction at any time or distance.`
- *The Rite of Finding*`| Charges: 6| Effects: Think of an Object, Creature, or place that you know or have a reasonable description of (enough to describe it to another). You instantly know the shortest path to it and any hidden dangers between you and it.`

#### Put Satanachia, the Black Ledger
****
> *"Spirit of desire and dominion, prince under the vault of flames, we invoke you into our circle and under the trigram, within the hex and pentagram. We honor you with ceremony and auspicious signs. Dwell in us and place your honey on our tongues."*

The Grand Courtier of the Abyss, Put Satanachia was the first of the Demons that dealt with the Company of Fools. Forbidden by nature from the war of swords and missiles, his was the war of words and majesty.

The most noble and the most desirous, Put Satanachia obligates Binders to follow his codes and manners, and to honor him with unions of flesh. His reward is a kind of insidious charisma, a sweetness in word and deed that almost none can resist.

**The Pact**
Put Satanachia is a creature of contradictory ends, something which he demands from those who Bind him. The first of these is courtesy, Codes which obligate noble conduct. The second of these is desire, obsessions and needs of the basest kind.
`The Codes of Put Satanachia are as follows, each of which constitutes a violation of his Pact if disobeyed:`
- `Treat others with polite conduct unless they desire otherwise.`
- `Refuse no offer of pleasure and inflict no harm, unless the victim themselves begs for pain.`
- `Take no part in violence, unless under threat yourself.`
`Binders of Satanachia must also honor his desires. Failing to Satisfy him for more than a day and a night constitutes a violation. The following kinds of act are Satisfactory in this way:`
- `Extravigant feasts, expensive and pompous.`
- `Carnal union with a new partner.`
- `Especially lecherous or debauched activity of any kind.`
`Falling to his Pact reduces the Binder to a gibbering predator, driven by depraved compulsions and sick thoughts.`

Those under the sign of Put Satanachia exude an Aura of Domination, able to subjugate and command with invisible strings.
`You gain a Bonus to your Mind equal to your Pact Strength. Those whose Mind is less than yours are Vulnerable to you, and their Disposition towards you is greater by 1. If the difference is more than 6, their Disposition is greater by 2. If the difference is greater by 10, they must Coin-Flip to disobey your verbal commands.`
#### Lucifuge Rofocal, the Golden Idolater
****
> *"Spirit of wealth and conservation, most prosperous and gleaming, we invoke you with gilt offerings. Dwell in us and bring us riches, gold and silver, gems and crystal, carved and precious, crowns and diadems to be heaped before your altar."*

The Treasurer of the palaces in the Abyss, Lucifuge was and is a shrewd creature, willing to treat with any side able to afford his service. The Doom was, for him, no change at all, for avarice is eternal.

Binders who make a Pact with Lucifuge Rofocal do so for a single reason, power. Hell's currency is Debt, the foul twin of obligation. This power is a sword of twin edges, by holding others in your Debt, you honor Lucifuge, but should you be found so impoverished, he will take revenge.

**The Pact**
Hell's Currency comes in denominations, as do all kinds of legal tender. For a Binder of Lucifuge, Debt is a tangible Essence, something they can perceive in and about a person, stemming from the kind of service they owe to another.
`While Bound to Lucifuge, you can percieve Debts of all kinds as a kind of intangible sign, betraying its kind, but not to who it is owed, unless it is to you. You always know if you are the party to whom someone is in Debt, and can always percieve your own Debts. Debts also have a Magnitude, a measure of their import. What you are able to do with Debts depends on this, as the main ability offered by Lucifuge comes in Extracting a Price for a Debt.`
- *The Debt of Conscience*: When one is done a kindness and feels a kindness is owed in kind. These Debts manifest spontaneously, and are usually of little consequence, fading with the emotions that created them.
> - `Debts of Conscience can have no greater Magnitude than 3, and usually only reach 1. A Debt of the Third Magnitude in this case would stem from a feeling as strong as a life-debt or similar grave obligations like parental love.`
> - `Debts of Conscience for you count as a Violation if you allow them to dissuade you from gaining greater wealth or power.`
- *The Debt of Law*: What one owes another by fiat of Legislation. The Law is eternal, and so too are these Debts until resolved, whether or not either party fully comprehends their implication.
> - `Debts of Law have a Magnitude corresponding to the severity of the punishment entailed in failing to honor them. 1 for minor fines, 3 for incarceration, 6 for death.`
> - `When you incur these Debts, you must repay them within the time period of your Pact with Lucifuge or suffer a Violation.`
- *The Debt of Volition*: Words of promise sworn to another. Contrary to Law, this is the greatest Debt in Hell, the giving of the will itself by conscious choice.
> - `This Debt stems from a Binding Pact, one which the Victim is made fully aware of. In exchange, you may set the terms, conditions, and the Magnitude of the Debt incurred.` 
> - `You are also Bound by these Pacts, meaning that failing to honor your end is a Violation in the eyes of the Golden Idolater. Further, if you ever lie to the victim in this proceeding no Pact is formed and you suffer twice the normal Penalty.`

**Extracting Debts**
`You can Extract a Price, up to the Magnitude of the Debt or Pact Strength, whichever is lower. You may do so as often as you like, until the Debt is absolved either by you or by a change in the circumstance. For example, losing the emotional source for Conscience, the warrant of Law, or a breakage of a contract on your part for Volition. Extracting a Price is not an Action proper, and you can even perform this while Unconscious or Helpless.`
- *1*: `You obtain agreement from the Target in some small matter (increasing their Attitude by 2), cause them to forget something within the last Day, or to mispercieve something small for 1 Minute.`
- *2*: `You learn who they owe any of their Debts to, and why.`
- *3*: `The Target falls under your control, operating as a mindless, blank-faced automata for the next Minute. They must obey you in all things. Alternatively, you can transfer an Injury or as many points of Strain (including Persistent) to them as you like.`
- *4*: `You learn the total history of Debt the Target has ever accrued, as well as all those to whom they owed it, when, and why.`
- *5*: `The Target falls Unconscious for 1 Minute. During this period, they suffer any Damage, Stress, Strain, or Corruption instead of you.`
- *6*: `The Target is under your total control and must obey all commands you give until you absolve them of the Debt. Further, you can Extract any of the lesser Prices at will from them.`

#### Lilith, the Queen in Shadow
****
> *"Oh noblest Spirit, empress of treachery and overturner of kings, we invoke you with a wetted crown of sharpened lead. She who cuts the throats of the judge and the regent, dwell in us and guide us in the path of Sedition."*

The only true daughter of Abbadon, Lilith ruled the host of Demons before the Doom. Only bested by the Company of Fools by means of treachery, she abandoned her nobility to sow unrest in the children of her enemy.

Binders seeking Lilith find great and sinister powers, the cost of which is often unspeakably great. Perhaps the most dangerous of the Demons, she regards the remaining Verdun with equal parts amusement and hatred, the children of the Fools' Company in one sense, their greatest failure in another.

**The Pact**
Lilith demands nothing short of murder, a blood offering of a precious vintage to even initiate. Continual service is just as taxing.
`To enter a Pact with Lilith, one must slay a loved one in her name. As you remain so-Bound, she may demand any life she wishes at GM discretion, often taking when and who would hurt the most. Any failure to honor these requests is terminal. You Fall instantly and are transformed into 72 writhing, vicious asps.`

In return, her Binders are offered truly awful strengths, fitting both her price and continual demands.
`You gain the following benefits at the corresponding thresholds of Pact Strength:`
- 1: `Nothing can negate or Block Cutting or Piercing Damage. When your Target rolls Injury Risk, they roll twice and you choose the result.`
- 3: `A single Creature of your choice per Day chosen at dawn cannot perceive you at all unless you choose to let them. Likewise, they cannot Spot you. Neither benefit applies when you stand within a ring of salt.`
- 6: `If you Injure a Creature that does not Detect you, they die.`
