## The Aether
****
Before the Cataclysm, magic was breath. The Aether, an omnipresent background of Arcane potential, allowed the Gifted to freely draw on its power. When the World shattered, so did the Aether. Most of it became estranged, uncontrollable, manifesting as dangerous, Shard-wide Anomalies. The other crystalized into Residue, the fuel that provides the Cities with breathable air, powers Arcane Artifacts, Rituals, and what makes Mages.

Bereft of the subtle Aether, the Gifted must become Mages to access their Arcane abilities. Integrating Residue into their bodies and becoming something between creature and and Artifact.

#### Mages
****
Gifted, Creatures with Arcane talent, become Mages by way of the Initiation, an Arcane Ritual which fuses a seed of Pure Residue with their physical form. This grows as their abilities do, and gradually transforms them. In addition to this, Mages must etch Runes into their bodies to be able to form Aether into Spells.

Consequently, a newly Initiated Mage might appear like anyone else, but the truly powerful are barely recognizable, more Residue-encrusted Rune and crystalline growths than flesh.

## Basic Aspects of a Mage
****
#### What Mages Do
****
Mages have a Core of Primed Residue, a growing crystalline interface throughout their body. This allows them to Channel, to accumulate pure Aether which they can control, rather than the corrupted counterpart in the Shard Sea beyond. 

Doing so requires the use of Arcane Runes, literally etched into their bodies to give the Aether form. These Runes can also be externalized by Mages in Rituals, applications of Arcane Magic that combine multiple Runes.
#### Reach and Limitations
****
A Mage's Core is their source of power as well as their limitation. The Tier of this Item informs two aspects of import when doing Arcane Magic.

**The Arcane Reach**
The amount of power a Mage can withstand, how much Aether you can hold at any one time.
****
> *A Mage's Reach is equal to the twice the Tier of their Core.*
****
Aether gained in excess of your Reach is lost at the Start of your next Turn (in 6 Seconds).

**Influence**
The reach of that power, a Radius surrounding you at all times.
****
> *A Mage's Influence is equal to twice their Reach plus their Mind.*
****
No Mage's Spell can have effects beyond their Influence. Even those with Permanent Durations end when no longer within the Influence of their Caster.

#### Advancing a Core
****
The Arcane Core is not a Skill or Feat, rather an internalized Item. Mage's Advance the Tier of their Core by consuming Primed Residue, which the Core feeds on to grow. Consuming Primed Residue of a given Tier raises a Mage's Core to that Tier.
****
> *The Cost of Primed Residue is equal to 1,000 plus (1,000 times its Tier).*
****
Advancing a Core has some risks, especially if you allow it to grow faster than yourself. *If your Core is greater in Tier than you, you suffer Permanent Corruption equal to the difference.*

#### Runes and Acquisition
****
Acquiring Runes is a lengthy process and often very strenuous for the Mage, which has some important caveats from their resources and Training. 

**Tier**
Firstly, Runes have a Tier, independent of Spells they form. *You cannot Incorporate a Rune whose Tier exceeds your Training in Arcane.*

**Class**
Secondly, Runes have a Class, which groups the abstract principles they represent. As you Advance in Arcane, you will eventually specialize in certain Classes over others. Likewise, some Rituals, Items, and Spells can refer to the Class of a Rune.

Exclusive activity: **Incorporation**
> *"Glass etched in skin, crystal biting flesh, power engraved in blood"*
> `You spend Residue up to 600 times the Tier of a Rune, and Watches up to its Tier. Incorporating a Rune allows you to use it to Cast Spells and in Rituals, but inflicts Persistant Strain equal to its Tier, as well as twice as much standard Corruption.`
> *Collaboration*: Trained Collaborators may pool Opportunity with the Mage under the knife.
> 
#### Channeling and Using Runes
****
Generating and holding Aether is a very active and obvious procedure, causing the inclusions of Residue in their eyes, skin, and the Runes covering their body to glow with cold blue-ish cobalt light.

General Action: **Channel**
> *AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
> *"Cold and hot at once, bittersweet and electric, the ineffable feel of Aether pure"*
> You reach inside yourself and command your Core to begin to burn, summoning Aether, the raw stuff of Magic.
> `For the Duration, you can Generate Aether and Cast Spells from Integrated Runes.`
> `While Channeling, you also gain an awareness of supernatural and Magical phenomena within your Influence. This counts as an Indirect Sense and grants you intuitive understand of the rough shape and nature of the Magic.` 

General Action: **Generate**
> *AP*: ~| *Qualities*: Strain_~|
> *"Burn"*
> Stoke the inner fire and harness the sparks.
> `You gain Aether, up to the number of AP Spent. This cannot exceed your Reach.`
> *Drawing Deeper*
> `Instead of spending AP, you can suffer Corruption to gain (your Tier)d6 Aether. Aether gained this way can exceed your Reach, but is lost after one Round.`

Primary Action: **Cast**
> *AP*: 0| *Qualities*: Primary|
> *"From Aether, power, from the Runes, form. With intent, Being."*
> You release an amount of Aether through a Rune, forming an Arcane Spell, the primary mode of Magic.
> `Choose the Tier at which you would Cast the Spell, and spend that much of your held Aether. Then, make an Arcane Test with the following Difficulty:`
> - Spell DC: `10+(The Tier of the Spell)`
> *Spell Burn*
> `If you fail, you suffer Spell Burn, inflicting 1 Persistant Strain.`
> *Critical effect*
> `The Spell's Tier is increased by your own.`
> *Bolster Patterns*
> `Lastly, when you Cast a Spell, you can spend additional Aether before seeing the result to gain an equal Bonus to the Test.`
#### Spells and their Traits
****
A Rune is a primordial glyph of power, an esoteric form etched into the Mage's body. A Spell is what happens when a Mage Channels Aether through a Rune.

**Tier**
Spells have a Tier, how much Aether they consume, chosen when you Cast them. Effects that refer to T use this number. *Additional Aether spent around a Spell to bolster is chance of success do not add to T.*

**Duration**
Unless otherwise stated, a Spell with persistent effects lasts for 1 Minute. You can extend this by spending Aether equal to its Tier to make it Permanent and last until you choose to release it, its effects leave your Influence, or it is Negated, dispelled, etc.

*Runes that form Spells without lasting effects have a "!" appended to their Class. Ex: "Class: Force!" as opposed to Class: Time"*

**Ending**
You can End any Spell of yours at will, but unless they are within one or more Senses, you cannot tell when one or more is Negated or tampered with. 

**Concentration**
Whenever you Cast a Spell at a Tier equal to or greater than your Core Tier, it gains the Concentration Quality. Spells with the Concentration Quality require a Primary Action to Cast and end when you cast another, unless you make them Permanent.

**Minor**
Whenever you Cast a Spell at a Tier below your Core Tier, it gains the Minor Quality. Casting Minor Spells counts as a General Action.

**Reaction**
Some Runes form Spells which you can Cast as a Reaction, stated in their description.

#### Arcane Rituals
****
Spells express a single Rune and require the presence of the Mage to persist. However, a much deeper extension of the tradition exists in the form of Rituals.

These Arcane works combine multiple Runes together, creating more dramatic and subtle effects that far surpass the abilities of any one Mage. For example, the Ark Shields that preserve its atmosphere and excludes all but those its builder allow is a grand Ritual maintained by the Mage Guild of each Ark. Supplying this with Residue is one of the main causes for Expeditioners in the Shard Sea.

**Performing a Ritual**
To initiate one of these esoteric expressions of the Arcane, you must have the Component Runes Integrated and spend time and any resources dictated by its description. *For Rituals requiring multiple Mages, the assistants only need one of its Component Runes, provided the Initiator has them all.*

**Learning a Ritual**
Rituals also have Formulae, learned from books or other Mages willing to instruct. Books containing Rituals are called Grimoire, and generally have a Value equal to what would be required to gain its Component Runes.

Exclusive Activity: **Ritual Study**
> `Spend Watches equal to the summed Tiers of a Ritual's Runes. You commit the Formula of the given Ritual to memory, allowing you to perform it without a Grimoire.`
> *Collaboration*: Trained Collaborators may pool their spent Opportunity and all learn the Ritual.

**Ritual Tiers**
Rituals have Tiers like Spells do, up to your Arcane. Reaching any higher than this requires more Mages as Collaborators, whose efforts increase the maximum Tier, according to the following progression:
*Collaborators*
- 1: +1
- 2: +2
- 6: +3
- 10: +4
- 20: +5
- 30: +6
- etc...

****
All Mages are provided with the Formulae for the following Rituals at Initiation.

> *Reagents*: At least 1 Bulk of any Residue, at least 1 Air Cannister.

> *Reagents*: Implements for making marks and a floor or surface for receiving a drawn Circle.

## Ritual Potential
****
Rituals are Magical work, powered by Aether and Runes. The Formulae involved are patterns and do no work themselves. So it stands to reason that a Mage could accidentally discover a Ritual without knowing its Formula, or create entirely new Rituals.

Developing a new Ritual or exploring the combinatorial possibilities of Runes beyond simple Spells is something Mages achieve with something called the Probability Trance, a meditative state wherein they constantly Channel, drawing small amounts of Aether and allowing it to flow through and between the Runes Etched into their skin.

#### Exploring Rituals
****
In the metagame, the procedure of the Probability Trance is a discussion between the Player and GM. The Player has a result in mind they want to achieve, a selection of Runes that they know the individual effects and meanings of, and other Rituals as precedent for what kinds of effects can be expected.

In the conversation, the Player should connect these, explaining the desired effects using the Runes they choose. For example:
> *"We need to get warm. I Know Shell and Star make a Magic Circle, if I add Spark, which can create fire, could I make a zone that warmed us up?"*
Given that this is a relatively minor effect, and that other Runes in the Power Class can do this exact thing, the GM can give assent and allow the Player to perform a Ritual with the same rough structure as Magic Circle, with the effects of warming the air within by 1 or 2 steps.

This is entirely subject to GM Fiat, of course. They are free, and encouraged even, to add restrictions on time required, Reagents, and unintended effects to the Ritual. 

If they desired effects is very, very drastic, say something like raising a Creature to full Life, something that Priests with divine authority over life can't even do, the GM should either deny it as outright impossible with the present Runes or add extreme restrictions to the Ritual. For example, a Ritual that can bring the dead to life by syphoning the lives of a thousand others to nothing.

At the conclusion of the Probability Trance, when agreement has been reached about that is possible, this conclusion can be recorded by the Player as a Ritual Formula. These don't need to approximate the structure of one the pre-written Rituals per-se, but it needs to include the following:
> **What can the Ritual do**:
> **How long does it take to perform**:
> **What is needed to do it**:

If the GM elected to include hidden consequences, those can be added to the Formula when discovered.

As a closing bit of advice for the GM, this is an entirely free-form and mostly narrative forward system. If circumstance or other issues preclude positive, healthy discussion, the conversation is happening at the expense of other Players' time and enjoyment or it seems like it will take longer than 10 or so minutes of real-life time at the table, consider ruling the Ritual impossible until further notice. *Also, given that the Probability Trance is an Activity that consumes in-game time, warn Players if the initial idea is absurd or unfeasible, ex. Star, Distance, and Touch to open a portal into the sun.*

For Players, you should approach the Probability Trance and Ritual exploration from your Character's perspective first. Consider what their Runes mean to them, and how those meanings can combine abstractly to form some more complex effect. With that concept in mind, how can that be simply and effectively performed in game?

Exclusive Activity: **The Probability Trance**
> `A number of Integrated Runes you choose as candidates enter Permutation; you spend a number of Watches equal to the Runes permuted, and enter a state of quasi-Unconsciousness for the Duration.  This allows you to explore their possibilities in a Ritual without a Formula and potentially record a Formula for a new Ritual.`
> *Collaboration*: Trained Collaborators may contributes any Runes they know.
