****
Good leaders are a rare treasure, one which Trainees of this Discipline hope to achieve. Another time-honored practice, Leadership is a Noble pursuit be convention, but more often achieved elsewhere.

Leadership is forged in adversity, tempered and hardened by struggle. Leaders place themselves in the greatest danger, and fight alongside their charges. Conflict abounds beyond the Arks, making Expeditioners and Sea-going Paladins the real champions of Leadership.

**The Discipline of Leadership**
`You gain the Inspire Competance Activity`

Exclusive Activity - **Inspire Competence**
> `You spend ten Minutes, give a rousing address to all that will hear it, and make a Leadership Test. If your roll is at least 10, all who attend you shed 1 Strain. If you roll at least 12, they also gain an effective increase in 1 Skill of your choice, depending on the subject of the address. If you roll 16 or more, they benefit from a Partial Rest.`
> *Collaboration*: All who benefit must be Collaborators, and the Trained each confer a Boon to the Test if they participate in the address.

Feat 1: **Command**
> *"Follow me"*
> You issue a direct order, a single instruction to be followed to the letter.
> `You Command an Ally to take an Action with an AP Cost up to the amount you spend. Following a Command from you is a !0 AP Reaction.`
> `This Action gains the Primary Quality if the Action you Command is a Primary Action.`
> *Limits* 
> `You must spend at least one AP for each Command.

Feat 2: **Bannerlord**
> *AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
> *"To me!"*
> Allies fall in line, into formation behind the emblem.
> `You raise a metaphorical (or literal) Banner. This has a Range equal to your Leadership plus 1.`
> `For the Duration, Allies within Range can Move with you, up to their Movement, when you take a Movement Action.`

Feat 3: **Efficacy**
> *"Good leadership is simple"*
> Your words carry force that betrays a depth of meaning.
> `When you Command an Ally, the Action you Command can cost 1 more AP than you spend, so long as you spend at least 1.`

Feat 4: **Warning**
> *"Left!"*
> A noble leader protects those under their command.
> `When an Ally within Range of your Banner is Attacked, you can grant them Resistence_All up to your Leadership as a !0_AP Reaction.`

Feat 5: **Coordination**
> *"Strike!"*
> As Leadership becomes second nature, your commands flow at a break-neck pace.
> `Your Command is no longer a Primary Action under any circumstance.`

Feat 6: **Direct Assistance**
> *"With me!"*
> Your presence inspires competance.
> `Allies within Range of your Banner gain a Bonus to a single Test per Turn of your choice, equal to your Leadership.`
