****
Dynamism and release, Athletics directs and controls bodily force. While not necessarily making one strong, it gives the strong new and powerful ways to use their strength.

Older than history, Athletics is as widely applied, suitable for sport, recreation, combat, and Expeditions. The dynamic environments beyond the Arks make this an appealing skill, especially when gaps and falls should be expected.

**Passive Effects**
`You gain Bonus to your Encumberance equal to your Athletics, can use Athletics to Roll.`
****
Feat 1: **Warmed Up**
> *AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Stance, Movement|
> *"Air like fire"*
> Plant your feet and breath deep, ready to push yourself even further.
> `For the Duration of this Stance, you gain a Bonus to your Movement equal to your Athletics, and can Leap equal to your Movement. When you Leap and make an Action Test or Attack, you gain a Boon.`

Feat 2: **Knee Drop**
> *"Must come down"*
> Every Expeditioner knows how to cushion a fall, you know how to turn it to your advantage.
> `When you land from a Fall in Engagement with one or more Creatures, a succesful Roll applies the Damage to one of them, knocking them Prone if it Injures them.`

Feat 3: **Tackle**
> *"Can't hold back"*
> Athletics' implementation of a timeless principle, it's harder to hit you from the ground.
> `When you pass through a Creature's Engagement while Sprinting, you can make an Opposed Athletics Test against them as a !0 AP Movement Action, knocking them Prone on a success. A success also negates any Attacks of Opporunity from them.`
> *Critical effect*
> `The Target suffers Falling Damage as if from Meters up to their Weight, they may Roll.`

Feat 4: **Falling in Line**
> *"One after another"*
> Like cards stacked in a row, your foes topple one after the next.
> `When you knock a Creature Prone, you can displace horizontally them a number of Meters up to your Athletics. Further, whenever you forcibly move a Creature and they would collide with another, the second is knocked Prone.`

Feat 5: **Sky High**
> *AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Strain, Movement|
> *"Real strength is never effortless"*
> Tense your legs and release an explosive burst of speed, propelling you further, faster, and past the finish line.
> `Leap up to twice your Movement without Provoking Attacks.`

Feat 6: **Relentless Rush**
> *AP*: 2| *Qualities*: Strain, Movement|
> *"Unstoppable force"*
> Blitz ahead, displacing everything that would dare impede.
> `Move thrice without Provoking Attacks. Each Creature whose Engagement you pass through is knocked Prone.`
