****
Once an impractical art, Tumbling is a deadly serious practice for Expeditioners. When terrain, threats, and hazards are totally unknown, speed and avoidance are some of the mightiest shields.

Tumblers are known for their sometimes baffling mobility, a kind of almost supernatural alacrity that recalls the Discipline's ancient birth as sport.

**Passive Effects**
`You have a Buff to your Movement equal to your Tumble.`
****

Feat 1: **Drive**
> *AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Movement|
> *"In the wind"*
> You step, impossibly quickly, covering spans instead of the usual shallow interstice.
> `You Maneuver plus your Tumble in any direction at all. When you Drive though a Creature's Engagement, you can attempt to Trip them.`
> *Limits*
> `This assumes you have some surface to propel from. You may technically Drive without such a surface, but doing so changes the AP cost to !2.`

Feat 2: **Slip**
> *"Left behind"*
> Close brushes with you can be a unnerving experience, a rush of wind and empty air.
> `When you pass through a Creature's Engagement, they become Vulnerable equal to half your Tumble for one Turn.`

Feat 3: **Tumbler's Grace**
> *AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
> *"Passing by"*
> Your steps betray nothing, flowing from here to there like living water.
> `For the Duration, you do not Provoke Attacks at all, gain a Climbing Speed equal to your Walking Speend, and suffer less Damage from Falling, equal to twice your Tumble.`

Feat 4: **Continuity**
> *"Never coming down"*
> Interruptions are for those still bound to the earth.
> `When taking a Movement Action, you can take a different Action during the Movement without stopping.`

Feat 5: **Limitless**
> *"Embrace the freedom"*
> Very little can stop you, geometry itself remains an obstacle.
> `All Difficult Terrain is reduced for you by your Tumble, and you can cling to flat surfaces seemingly without handholds when Climbing.`

Feat 6: **Beyond**
> *"Through the wall"*
> The impossible is defined by the limited.
> `When you Drive, you can pass through anything but total iron-clad obstructions, such as Meter-thick walls of metal. Anything short of it and seemingly absurd gaps are enough for you to pass through.`
