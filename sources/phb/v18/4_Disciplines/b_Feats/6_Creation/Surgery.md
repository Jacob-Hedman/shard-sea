****
Injuries, especially the more grievous, have been with the Verdun likely before there were Verdun. Then and now, Hospitallers are the usual refuge against these. However, secular means exist for preference or of necessity. Barbers are the experts of these means, would surgeons of another time and place filling a distinct role within the Shard Sea.

Barbers are part doctor, part militarist, given the needs of Expeditioners for aid beyond the confines of the Greatwards. Unable to match the extent of a Hospitaller, Barbers ply their craft for breadth.

**The Discipline of Surgery**
As a Barber, one is expected to be able to competently assess wounds, deaths, and even aberrant anatomies in the field, making the Discipline a prime candidate for Research. Further, you gain the Activity of Surgery.

Exclusive Activity - **Surgery**
> *"Sawbones"*
> `Spend at least one Watch and Make a Surgery Test, indicating an Injury of your patient, with a Difficulty according to its Severity, 12 for Minor, 14 for Moderate, 16 for Major. Success Treats the Injury. Undergoing Surgery usually inflicts one Corruption on the patient.`
> *Time and Treatment*
> `Treated Injuries have half their Recovery time (this benefit does not stack.) Minor Injuries are removed entirely, and Major Injuries must be Treated to begin Recovery at all.`
> *Shedding*
> `Treating an Injury reduces its Permanent Strain by one, but only once for the same Injury.`
> *Critical effect*
> `Critical success doubles the effects of Treatment and negates the Corruption.`
> *Collaboration*: Trained Collaborators allow you to Treat more Injuries, one for each.

Feat 1: **Splints and Poultices**
> *"Bite this"*
> Battle medicine and triage are the first callings for the Barber, ever present needs and puzzles.
> `When you use First-Aid, you can treat the Test as a Specialized Surgery Test. If you succeed by 3 or more, the Target sheds 1d6 Strain.`

Feat 2: **Hands of a Surgeon**
> *"Drink that"*
> The best medicine is prevention, less work for you if your patients break even a few fewer bones.
> `As a !0 AP Action you can administer a Consumable to an Ally within your Engagement, or yourself.`

Feat 3: **Killing Time**
> *"Scalpel, please"*
> Surgery is an active process, push here, pull there, hold this quiver.
> `When using First-Aid, you can Maneuver, make an Interaction, Strike, or Shoot as a part of the same Action, as if with 1 less AP as used for the First-Aid.`

Feat 4: **Pick-me-up**
> *"A doctor, sometimes magician"*
> A swift slap across the face and a pat on the back can be a surprisingly effective treatment.
> `Creatures you give First-Aid automatically shed Strain equal to half your Surgery, and you can end any Condition they are suffering from (including Exception Conditions) of your choice.`

Feat 5: **Saintly**
> *"Not yet"*
> No amount of skill is a substitute for Magic or Divine intervention, but you can give it a shot.
> `When a Creature dies, you can attempt to perform First-Aid with a Difficulty of 16. If you succeed, they are only Unconscious. This only works if the Target only "died" within Simple-Turns equal to or less than your Surgery. Likewise, Creatures that die due to a lethal instance of Damage are not effected.`

Feat 6: **Just a Scratch**
> *"Keep off it for a week"*
> No, you can't re-attach a severed leg, but you can help someone forget a puncture to the carotid artery, at least for a handful of seconds.
> `When you give a Creature First-Aid, they can ignore the effects of any but a Major Injury for 1 Minute.`
