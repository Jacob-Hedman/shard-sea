****
Tonics, Medicines, and other less reputable kinds of substance existed long before the Doom. With the advent of the Expeditionary age and of the confinement within the Arks, it would hardly take a prophet to predict their proliferation.

Chemists practice a Discipline of trial and error, ascertaining secular means to the sacred arts of healing and bodily empowerment. Profane as it may seem, many Expeditionary parties and private persons may lack the option of a Priest's Expiation through small or no fault. At least that's what the Chemist would say.

**The Discipline of Alchemy**
Chemists are Makers, responsible for the creation of Consumable Items for a wide range of applications. Some Chemists progress the study as far as brewing a good beer. Others go much further than they should. You gain the Brew Activity and basic knowledge of Consumable goods.

Exclusive Activity - **Brew**
> *"Fire burn, crucible flare"*
> `Spend Watches, equal to the Tiers of the Items you would create, You create one or more Consumable Items, provided you know how and have reasonable access to the materials.`
> *Cost*
> `Like Crafting, half that of the Item.`
> *Expression*
> `Like other Disciplines, you might be able to create a new Consumable entirely at GM discretion. Consult the Mage's Probability Trance for an in-depth discussion of this kind of procedure.`
> *Collaboration*: Trained Collaborators can pool their Opportunity, and the Skilled can contribute half they spend to the effective total.

Feat 1: **Mutagenic**
> *"Invidious"*
> Chemists are well known for their services. On the one hand, everyone appreciates a good drink. On the other, the implications of the wider Discipline tend to turn stomachs.
> `In addition to the normal array of Items, Chemists are known for a select array of special concoctions known as Mutagens. A Mutagen is made from a Creature's own blood, mixed and adulturated for less than natural effects.`
> `A Mutagen only effects the Creature whose blood was used as an ingredient, but Mutagens lack a Cost for Brewing, provided the blood is obtained without a material Cost. Likewise, when you Brew a Mutagen, its Tier is equal to the value of any Numerical Bonuses it provides, up to your Alchemy.`
> *Rudimentary Mutagens*
> - 1 Minute| *Mutandis*: `When Consumed, the Creature suffers a penalty up to your Alchemy to any Ability of your choice, in exchange for a Bonus of equal size to another.`
> - 1 Minute| *Erger*: `When Consumed, the Creature becomes Frenzied for the Duration, but gains a Buff up to half your Alchemy to their Body.`
> - 1 Hour| *Succor*: `When Consumed, the Creature gains a Buff to their Reserve, up to your Alchemy. In exchange, they are as one more Vulnerable and Off_Balance.`

Feat 2: **Stimulating Vapors**
> *"Deep breaths"*
> The active principle of a thing is subject to change, according to its nature. The use of Chemist Weaponry would have been a crime in the years before. Now, perhaps even this has its place.
> `When Brewing, you can package a Consumable as a Bomb. A Bomb is a Light Weapon that deals no Damage. Instead, every Creature within a T-Meter Burst from where it lands when Thrown is effected by the Consumable at half its normal effect in terms of Buffs, Bonuses, or the Addictive and Deadly Qualities. Mutagens function at half their Tier as Bombs.`
> *Compensation*
> `When creating a Chemist Bomb, you can also choose to include twice the concentration, as if making two, to normalize the effects within the area of effect.`

Feat 3: **Sickly Regrowth**
> *"Neither better nor worse"*
> All formulae have alternatives, substitutions subject to experimentation. The same is true for bodies.
> `You can Brew a Vulnerous Decoction as a Mutagen for a Creature with an Injury. When Consumed, they can ignore the effects of Injury (including Permanent effects) for a Day but suffer 1 Corruption.`

Feat 4: **Wakeful Medicine**
> *"This will bring you up, for now"*
> Equality is essential to the science of Alchemy, the give and take of substance and, with this particular formula, of time.
> `You can Brew a Quickening Tonic as a Mutagen. This allows a Creature's to add your Alchemy to their Reflex for Initiative for the next 24 Hours and removes their penalties from lack of sleep. After the duration, they suffer the effects of 2d6 missed nights of sleep.`

Feat 5: **Doctor's Orders**
> *"Everything is everything"*
> For the action of a medicine to be possible, the potential of it must lie in the blood, true also for a poison.
> `When Brewing, you can make any Consumable at all as if it were a Mutagen, but this only effects the Creature whose blood you used.`

Feat 6: **Monstrous Adaptation**
> *"It lives in you"*
> Finally, for the principals of blood to be general, so to must blood itself by universal. All Chemists eventually make this discovery, should their curiosity run deep enough.
> `Your Mutagens can effect anyone, regardless of the blood used. When under the effects of another's Mutagen, the patient suffer 1 Corruption. Further, you can twist any of your Mutagens when Brewed, inverting the benefits into penalties.`
