****
Scribes have two records, the written word, and the physical brain. Memory is the cultivation of mental capacity, expanding ones inner life in a way that allows its practitioners unnatural accuracy of recollection.

Scribal Training is a practical task for many other callings, Mages, Nobles, Priests, and even some Makers benefit from it. A dedicated Scribe is a Maker, a writer and recorder of books.

**Passive Effects**
`Your Mind is increased by half your Memory.`
****

Feat 1: **The Mind Palace**
> *"Safe inside"*
> The practice of Memory is safekeeping, storage of information in a form both private and secure.
> `You have an internal memory model, your Mind Palace, that aids in memorizing things that others simply couldn't. Your Mind Palace allows you to record the exact contents of anything that could be written, drawn, or painted on a Page of average size. Something that would take multiple of these can still be Memorized, but fills multiple Pages in your Mind Palace.`
> `You have a number of Pages in your Mind Palace up to twice your Memory, all of which start Blank. Filling a Page takes approximately 1 Minute of scrutiny of the thing you wish to Memorize. This makes the Page full, even if the entirety isn't consumed.`
> `If all the Pages in your Mind Palace are full, you can over-write any of them you choose when Memorizing.`

Feat 2: **Rushed Approach**
> *"Ravenous"*
> The mind is a flexible thing, able to record at astounding speeds if one is willing to stretch a bit.
> `By suffering Strain, you can hasten the process of Memorization. This reduces the time needed to Memorize to 6 Seconds, or a 3 AP General Action.`

Feat 3: **Hardware**
> *"External theory"*
> Who ever said the mind was only in the head? Your tools, paper and pen, facilitate the movement of information from the internal Palace to a real one.
> `You can externalize your Mind Palace, allowing you to replicate one of its Pages on a real Page in the same amount of time. Physical Pages can be used to add or remove things from your Mind Palace without loss of any information.`

Feat 4: **Brute Force**
> *"Semiotic"*
> Even things not memorized leave signs. With effort, signs can become the real thing.
> `When attempting to remember something not in your Mind Palace, you can make a Difficulty 16 Memory Test. If you succeed, the information is gained as Pages. If you fail, you cannot attempt this again for 24 Hours for the same information.
> *Critical effect*
> `The Information gained no longer occupies any space in your Mind Palace.`

Feat 5: **Continuity**
> *"Neither here nor there"*
> With expert use, the divide between the Mind Palace and the real trove of information begins to blur.
> `If you have a physical Page from your Mind Palace on your person, you can treat it as if it were a mental Page.`

Feat 6: **The Second Mind**
> *"Proliferation of Memory"*
> Rather than a plateau, you reach a cliff in capacity, a sudden burst of insight which widens your horizon.
> `Your Mind Palace's Page limit is doubled.`
