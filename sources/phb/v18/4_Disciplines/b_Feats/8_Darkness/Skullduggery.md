****
The Discipline of the Thief. Pickpockets and burglars alike practice Skullduggery, as well as Expeditioners, when the need arises.

Skullduggery is a tricky Discipline, literally, and because its usefulness tends to incline one towards less than legal uses of their Training.

**The Discipline of Skullduggery**
`You can add your half your Skullduggery to your Darkness for Activities.`
****

Feat 1: **Slight of Hand**
> *"Careful, quiet"*
> Your deft fingers slip and whirl, denying the prying gaze.
> `You can Conceal an Item or Items on your person, with no more Bulk than your Skullduggery. These cannot be perceived by normal means, but edge cases like a cavity search or Magic might.`
> `Objects Concealed by Slight of Hand can be Transfered, as a 1 AP Interaction, and doing so prevents the Targeted Creature from knowing they the Concealed Object now resides on their person.`
> `You can also Conceal an Object in another Creature's possession as an Interaction with an AP cost equal to half its Bulk. If they do not Detect you, and the Object is not in their hands, they do not notice the loss.`
> `All uses of Slight of Hand count as Interactions.`

Feat 2: **Duck**
> *AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Movement|
> *"Slipped away"*
> You veer away, passing under and out from sight for a moment.
> `You slip out of Sight, combining with the next Action taken this Turn for the purpose of Stealth.`

Feat 3: **Dodge**
> *AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Reaction|
> *"Passing by"*
> You follow the direct of a threat, moving with and past it.
> `When Attacked, you can Maneuver away, potentially leaving its Range or blocking line of sight.`

Feat 4: **Continuity**
> *"Deft fingers"*
> Your dexterity allows you to weave your moves into one another.
> `When you make a Stealthy Movement Action, you can include a Stealthy Interaction at any point in the Movement without stopping.`

Feat 5: **Flash**
> *AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Strain_1|
> *"Look over there!"*
> Make a sudden noise, kick over a pile of crates, cause mayhem.
> `You create a diversion which hides you from all Senses for the next Simple-Turn.`

Feat 6: **Blink**
> *AP*: 2| *Qualities*: Movement, Strain_2|
> *"Was that?"*
> Between the motion of two eyelids, you dissapear.
> `You vanish entirely, intangible but unable to Act until the start of your next Turn. At such a time you emerge, you can be anywhere within Meters from the original Space up to your Skullduggery`
