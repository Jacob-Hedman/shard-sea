Kriegsmesser measures your durability and current state in a few ways. Managing these is key to surviving an Expedition.

**Your Reserve**
Like a literal Durability, your Reserve is your resistance to fatigue. It has a value, equal to:
****
> *Reserve*=(Your Tier)+(Your Body Bonus)
****
- This is measured in a line on the upper border of your Sheet.

**Strain**
This is a universal measure of duress, and comes in three types:
****
> - *Standard*: expires in ten Minutes.
> - *Persistent*: shed by Resting.
> - *Permanent*: Inury-related, and requires healing.
****
- This is measured with your Reserve, for clarity of purpose and function.
*What Strain Does*
> When you suffer Strain in excess of your Reserve, all your Abilities and derived statistics fall by the same amount. When any of these falls to zero, you are Incapacitated.
#### The Core Gameplay Loops
****

**Tracking Strain**
When you suffer Strain, you mark it below your Reserve, checking a box with the corresponding symbol:
- *Standard Strain*: { / }
- *Persistent Strain*: { X }
- *Permanent Strain*: { \* }
*Priority*
> More severe Strain is moved to the left of the tracker, facilitated by the marks made. So:
> - If you had three Standard Strain, that would be three slashes on the track.
> - If you suffered one Persistent, you would mark the left-most space with an X, and move the slash to the fourth space.

**Taxing an Ability**
Another way in which your Abilities can be diminished, you may Tax an Ability before making a relevant Skill Test to automatically Succeed (unless Success is impossible for some reason.)
*Cost*
> Taxing an Ability reduces it by one until you Catch your Breath. ([[6.0 SHARDS/DOC/Shards of Life/SHARDS PHB/2_Survival/d_Rest and Catching your Breath]].)
*Relevance*
> Which Ability you may Tax for which Test depends on its relationship to that Skill. ([[7.0 Ophan 2/Shared Workspace/twophan/PHB/1_Making Characters/f-Ability Thresholds and Bonuses|f-Ability Thresholds and Bonuses]])
*Recourse*
> If you are denied the Success for Taxing an Ability for any reason, you do not reduce the Ability.

**Incapacitation**
When Incapacitated, you black-out, and are Unconscious for 1d6 Minutes. While Unconscious, you are Helpless, making all successful Attacks instantly Lethal. At the end of this time, you shed all Standard Strain and all Persistent Strain over your Reserve.
*Overload*
> If you have enough Permanent Strain from Injuries to Incapacitate you, you are Unconscious until this is no longer the case.

**Bleeding**
When you suffer the Bleeding Condition, you begin to rapidly lose blood. After a number of Rounds equal to your Natural Body value (absent the influence of your Strain or Taxation), you are Incapacitated. After twice that amount of time, you die. *Bleeding can be stopped by First-Aid.*

**Damage and Injuries**
When you are Attacked with a Weapon, fall from some height, or are Damaged by any means, you Risk being Injured.
*Damage Types*
> Instances of Damage comes in Types, corresponding to its source. Falling inflicts Crushing Damage, an arrow inflicts Piercing Damage, etc.
> - The Damage Type determines what Injury Risk Table you should roll from.
> - If an Attack inflicts multiple types of Damage, the Attacker chooses one as the primary effect.
*Threat*
> The amount of Damage dealt in any instance depends on the Type, for example, Cutting has a Base Damage of 1.
> - Objects and Armor are Notched up to this value when Damaged.
> - Damage is modified by the Vicious Trait, increasing (or reducing) it by a number of steps.
> - *If an instance of Damage would be less than one, it is Blunted instead. Blunted Damage inflicts a single Notch against Objects, Items, and Armor, and Creatures automatically roll a natural 12 on their Injury Risk for Blunted Damage.
*Injury Risk*
> When you suffer Damage, you roll from an Injury Table corresponding to the triggering Damage Type. ([[6.0 SHARDS/DOC/Shards of Life/SHARDS PHB/2_Survival/b_Attacks, Damage, and Injuries]])
> - When Injured, you suffer Permanent Strain equal the Damage.
> - An Injury has a Severity, which determines how it can be removed.
> - The Permanent Strain from an Injury is only shed when the Injury is removed.

**Armor**
This is a kind of Equipment with the ability to mitigate Damage. Armor has a Durability and a list of Damage Ratings.
*Avoiding Damage*
> When you would be Damaged by something your Armor is Rated for, you are unharmed. However, the Armor suffers Notches equal to the Damage.
*Breaking*
> Like any other Item, your Armor Breaks when Notched beyond its Durability. When your armor Breaks, you suffer the remaining Damage instead. For example, if your Armor had a Durability of 3, two Notches already, and suffered 4 Crushing Damage, the armor would be Notched twice, Break, and you would suffer the remaining 2 Crushing Damage.
