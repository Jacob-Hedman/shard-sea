
General Activity: **Catching your Breath**
> `After 10 Minutes or so of inactivity, you shed all Standard Strain. An Hour of light Activity also counts for Catching your Breath.`
> *Collaboration*: All participants gain the benefit.

General Activity: **Rest**
> `You Spend Watches to gain the following benefits, depending on the amount of time:`
> - One Watch: `You shed all Standard Strain and Persistent up to your Tier.`
> - Two Watches: `You shed all Standard Strain and half your Persistent.`
> - Three Watches: `You shed all Standard and Persistent Strain.`
> - Four Watches: `As three, and all your Injuries also reduce their Recovery time by one.`
> *Degradation*
> `Each Day you spend without Resting at all inflicts 1 Permanent Strain, which can only be removed by Resting for three Watches.`
> *Collaboration*: Resting together, beyond comradery, has little effect.
