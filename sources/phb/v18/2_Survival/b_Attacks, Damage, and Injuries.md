#### Attacks
****
These are Skill Tests made with Weapons to harm a Target, usually by inflicting Damage. Attacks use one of four Principle Skills, and are Resisted by the Same Skill used to make them.

**Damage**
Damage from an Attack depends on its Traits (or those of the Weapon used to make it). The Base Damage is set by a Type, Cutting Damage has a Base of 1 for example. Further, the Vicious Trait increases this amount. So, an Attack with a Vicious_2 Cutting Weapon deals 3 Damage.

**Melee Attacks**
Made against close Targets, Melee Weapons are either made with Weapons or Unarmed, using the skills of Vitality or Action.

**Ranged Attacks**
Made at a distance, Ranged Attacks always involve a Weapon, and use the Skills of Potence or Resolve.

**Gaming Attacks**
Characters can only have two of four Principle Skills, and Attacks are made with non-overlapping sets. In Character Creation, it's important note this and plan accordingly. 

****
>As a final note, most Enemies in Kriegsmesser are specifically designed to have gaps in their coverage, so to speak. Gauging allows you to find these and make smart decisions when picking which foe to Strike, which to Shoot, which to stay in Engagement with, and so on.

#### Using Weaponry
****
So, what's a Weapon then? These are Items used to make Attacks, and have important Traits to consider when evaluating the result of the Skill Test.
- **Accuracy**: This is added to the Attack as a Bonus. Negative Accuracies, consequently, are a penalty to your chance to hit.
- **Bulk**: The amount it weighs, measured in Bulk. This is especially important for Melee Weapons.
- **Misc Traits**: How many Hands one needs to Wield it, what Type of Damage it inflicts, and other properties go here.

**Melee Weapons**
Used against close foes, Melee Weapons require an amount of strength to use effectively.

**Ranged Weapons**
Able to pick out distant Targets, Ranged Weapons offer the advantages of both relative safety and scope, but are often harder than Melee Attacks.
- *The Trait of Range*: If a Target is further away than the Weapon's Range, then the Attack is made with a Bane and is Harder by 1 for every two Meters.

**Unarmed and Improvised Attacks**
When you Attack without a Weapon, or with something no traditionally classified as one, use the following profiles:
*Unarmed Weaponry*
> *Tier*: (Your Own)| *Damage*: `Crushing`| 
> *Accuracy*: -T| *Bulk*: 0
> *Traits*: `Blunt`


*Improvised Weaponry*
> *Tier*: (Your Own)| *Damage*: `Varies`
> *Accuracy*: -T| *Bulk*: `Varies`
> *Traits*: `Clumsy`
> - `The Damage of these Weapons is decided by the GM. Sharp Objects usually inflict Cutting or Piercing, but most inflict Crushing. If the Object's Bulk is greater than the Target's this becomes Traumatic Damage.`
> - `You must be able to vertically lift something to use it this way.`

**Simple Attack Reference**
The following steps outline what the usual procedure is when making an Attack:
- *1*: Make an Attack against a Target.
- *2_a*: If the Target is wearing Armor, it is Notched equal to the Damage.
- *2_b*: Otherwise, the Target rolls from an Injury Table, corresponding to the Damage Type, and suffers Permanent Strain (equal to the Damage) from any Injuries.

**Weakness and Resistance**
Sometimes, a Creature can be Weak or especially Resistant to a kind of Damage.
- *Weakness_*: When a Creature has the Weakness Trait to x Damage Type or Types, that Damage is more Vicious against them by x.
- *Resistance_x*: When a Creature has the Resistance Trait to x Type or Types of Damage, that Damage is less Vicious against them. As usual, Damage is Blunted when reduced below 1. *Blunted Damage gives the Target an automatic 12 on their Risk, and only inflicts a single Notch.*
- *DR:x*: When a Creature has DR related to a Type of Damage, this means they can ignore it completely if the amount is x or less. For example, DR:3_Crushing would allow a Creature to ignore any instance of Crushing Damage that was less than 4.
*Scale*
> Viciousness increases (or decreases) the amount of an instance of Damage. So both of these Traits usually have a value attached (if no value is listed, assume 1). So, against a Creature with Weakeness-3_Crushing, any Crushing Damage would be more Vicious by 3, raising it to 4, from its Base Damage of 1, all else equal. 
*Equipment*
> Weakness and Resistance applies both ways to and from Equipment. So, the Creature in the above scenario would suffer four Notches to any Armor it was wearing from Crushing Damage. Likewise, equipped with something that granted Resistance-3_Crushing, the Creature's innate Weakness would be canceled out.
*Multiplicity*
> If some instance of Damage has more than one Type, the Attacker chooses which is applied.
> - Weaknesses and Resistances may only apply to the single Damage Type.
> - Only one Damage Type can be chosen for this purpose.

#### Injury Risk
****
When Damaged by something, you roll from the corresponding Table, suffering whatever the result stipulates. 
- Several types of Damage have general rules, like Cuts being harder to Bandage, Crushing Injuries Stun you, etc.
- Injuries inflict Permanent Strain equal to the Damage, in addition to anything caused by the result.

**Injury Severity**
- *-*: `Instead of an Injury or Permanent Strain, you only suffer some effect or Condition for a given Duration.`
- *Minor*: `These Injuries are removed by First-Aid`
- *Moderate*: `These Injuries are removed by Recovery.`
- *Major*: `These Injuries are removed by Surgical Treatment.`
- *Lethal*: `You cannot remove a Lethal Injury, as you are simply dead.`

#### Types of Damage
****
Damage Types have a Base Damage value (for Strain and Notching Items) as well as an array of Injuries they can inflict. Likewise, Damage Types can have Traits:
- **Physical Damage**: cuts, impact, explosons, stabs, etc.
- **Energy Damage**: burns from less definite forms of power.
- **Arcane/Magical Damage**: inflicted by Spells and Rituals.

Physical Damage: **Cutting**
> *Base Damage*: 1
Incisions and wounds of separation. Cuts are gruesome work, an even uglier way to die. `Injuries from Cutting are harder to Treat or provide First-Aid, inflicting a Bane on associated Tests.`
> - *8-12*: Minor| *Superficial Cut*: A line of cold crimson, a weeping incision below the surface. `You begin to Bleed.`
> - *4-7*: Moderate| *Gash*: Grimacing, open across and in-through sinew. `You begin to Bleed and Risk Minor Panic.`
> - *2-3*: Major|  *Arterial Vent*: Blood pours from the opening, its vacuum welcoming ice into your core. `You begin to Bleed and Risk Major Panic.`

Physical Damage: **Crushing**
> *Base Damage*: 1
The impressions of fists and hammers. These Injuries are not usually lethal, but generally debilitating. `Crushing Injuries do not inflict Bleeding. When you suffer one, you become Stunned for 1d6 Turns.`
> - *11-12*: -| *Wheezer*: A blow that steals the breath, but little more. `You are Stunned for one Round and suffer 1 Strain.`
> - *7-10*: Minor| *Dazed*: You come to your senses, if just. `You suffer 1d6 Strain and are Off-Balance.`
> - *6*: Minor| *Tenderized*: A blow that leaves more than a mark, a shatter-point that you must guard. `You suffer 1 Strain and are one more Vulnerable.`
> - *4-5*: Minor| *Dislocation*: Something comes loose. `You Risk Moderate Panic, and lose the use of 1 Limb, chosen by the Attacker. If this is a leg, your Movement is halved. Flying Creatures fall if this is a wing.`
> - *2-3*: Major| *Thoracic Fracture*: A crack in your chest, each strenuous move comes with a grinding agony. `You suffer Major Panic from pain, which inflicts Persistant Strain. Each time you are Injured after this, you Risk additional Moderate Panic.`

Physical Damage: **Piercing**
> *Base Damage*: 2
Harm from stabs and arrows. While superior in piercing defenses, this tends to be a very unpredictable form of Risk. `When you suffer a Injury of Minor or greater Severity, you begin to Bleed.`
> - *8-12*: -| *Graze*: Your skin stings and ears whine from a brush much closer than comfortable. `You are Off-Balance for 1 Round and suffer one Strain.`
> - *6-7*: Minor| *Remote Puncture*: A hot line drawn through a foot or hand. `One of your extremities at random is Injured. Tests with Injured hands are penalized by 1, and each foot or mobility-limb penalizes Movement by 1.`
> - *4-5*: Moderate| *Gutshot*: Your breath catches with the sudden loss of pressure within your abdominal cavity. `You Risk Minor Panic, and this Injury is automatically Infected.`
> - *2-3*: Major| *Rent Lung*: A wheeze escapes your mouth, a dry-wet rasp betraying internal compromise. `You Risk Moderate Panic from Pain and suffer a Penalty of 2 to your Body.`

Physical Damage: **Impaling**
> *Base Damage*: 3
The greater form of Piercing Damage, Impaling Damage is much the same, but far more lethal.
> - *7-12*: -| *Graze*: Your ears ring, following a trench cut in the air. `You suffer one Strain, and are Off-Balance and Stunned for 1 Round.`
> - *3-6*: Major| *Socket Piercer*: Grinding friction from a joint rendered useless. `You lose the use of one of your limbs entirely (chosen by the Attacker). If this is a leg, your Movement is cut in half, suffering this to both legs makes your Prone. Flying Creatures begin to fall from this Injury.`
> - *2*: Lethal| *Headshot*: Iron through the skull, taking thought and life along the line of flight. `You die.`

Physical Damage: **Rending**
> *Base Damage*: 3
The greater form of Cutting Damage, hewing chops and sweeps of lethal potential. `Harder to treat, as Cutting Injuries. All results, even a Swipe, cause Bleeding.`
> - *11-12*: -| *Swipe*: A near-blow, the closeness its own kind of harm. `You Risk Minor Panic and are Off-Balance for 1 Round.`
> - *8-10*: Moderate| *Laceration*: `You instantly lose half your remaining time to Bleed-Out.`
> - *3-7*: Major| *Loss*: Nothing left but a bleeding stump. `You instantly lose half your remaining time to Bleed-Out and you lose a non-critical limb or bodypart of the Attacker's choice, with all the consequences following to Actions and Senses. Recovery does not restore the Lost part.`
> - *2*: Lethal| *Decapitation*: `You die.`

Physical Damage: **Traumatic**
> *Base Damage*: 3
The greater form of Crushing Damage, tremendous force, limbs of awful size, falling beams, crushing and crashing structures. `Unlike Crushing Damage, all results of Minor or greater Severity cause Bleeding.`
> - *7-12*: -| *Traumatic Graze*: You roll away, confused but unharmed. `You suffer one Strain and are Off-Balance for 1 Full_Turn.`
> - *3-6*: Major| *Break*: Recoiling, you live, but terribly wounded. `You suffer the combined effects of a rolled Crushing and Cutting Injury.`
> - *2*: Lethal| *Collapse*: The force was simply too great. `You die.`

Physical Damage: **Explosive**
> *Base Damage*: 4
Shattering reports and waves of burning heat, ripping, scorching, tearing asunder.
> - *11-12*: -| *Minor Concussion*: Somehow, you pull away mostly unscathed. `You Risk Moderate Panic and are Stunned_2 and Deaf for the next 2d6 Rounds.`
> - *4-10*: Major| *Shelled*: The ripping air and wind passes through, not dead, not yet. `You begin to Bleed, are more Vulnerable by one, and Risk Moderate Panic.`
> - *2-3*: Lethal| *Obliteration*: Gone. `You die.`

Physical Damage: **Catastrophic**
> *Base Damage*: 6
Might without measure, doom in the air, death passing by or through.
> - *11-12*: -| *Providential Turn*: A brush, a breath of wind that hardly betrays the Doom that just passed you by. `You suffer 1 Strain.`
> - *2-10*: Lethal| *Annihilation*: The obvious conclusion. `You die.`

Energy Damage: **Frost**
> *Base Damage*: 2
Black gnawing at the shafts of your bones, rime in the veins, blue lips and cracked skin. `All Frost Damage inflicts the Stunned and Off-Balance Conditions with a Duration in Turns up to the Strain suffered.`
> - *12*: -| *Shiver*: A mere brush with the arctic. `You Risk Minor Panic.`
> - *4-11*: Moderate| *Bite*: Numb, darkened streaks, almost like a bruise. `You Risk Moderate Panic.`
> - *2-3*: Major| *Black Burn*: Deeper bites, killing at the extremities first. `Roll Major Panic Risk. You suffer a penalty of 1 to all Tests made with you hands. If you do not receive Treatment within 24 Hours, this is Permanent and cannot be removed by normal means.`

Energy Damage: **Voltaic**
> *Base Damage*: 3
Electrical power from bolts and blue arcs of light, streaks of hate that freeze the muscles and freeze the heart. `Voltaic Damage Stuns you for Rounds up to your Tier.`
> - *8-12*: -| *Murmur*: A twitch as the power passes by. `You Risk Minor Panic and are Stunned for one Round for each 6 rolled.`
> - *4-7*: Moderate| *Shudder*: Dendritic lesions etched across the flesh. `You Risk Moderate Panic and are Stunned_2 for one Round for each 6 rolled.`
> - *2-3*: Major| *Tree-Scar*: Black lines of burned skin, clawing deep inside. `You Risk Major Panic and are Stunned_2 for each 6 rolled, and Stunned_1 until Recovery. If this Injury Incapacitates you, you die.`

Energy Damage: **Incendiary**
> *Base Damage*: 3
Burns and caustic lesions, Incendiary Risk is a near promise of lasting harm. `If you suffer sufficient Permanent Strain from Incendiary Damage to be automatically Incapacitated, you die.`
> - *11-12*: -| *Superficial Burn*: A flash of pain quickly subsides as you find yourself almost unscathed. `You Risk Minor Panic.`
> - *6-10*: Moderate| *1st-Degree Burn*: Bright, hot and painful, a red bloom in the senses. `You Risk Minor Panic which Inflicts Permanent Strain.`
> - *4-5*: Major| *2nd-Degree Burn*: A cold, deep bite, inflammation in the skin and your own heart. `You Risk Moderate Panic which inflicts Permanent Strain.`
> - *2-3*: Major| *3rd-Degree Burn*: Something worse, loss of sensation and the dread of permanent disfigurement. `You Risk Major Panic which inflicts permanent Strain and suffer a Penalty of 1 to Reflex and Body.`

Energy Damage: **Chaos**
> *Base Damage*: 3
Strange, violating energies that twist the body and mind.
> - 7-12: -| *Seeing Violet*: `You suffer Strain equal to the Damage and are Frenzied for 1d6 Rounds. If this rolls 6, you cannot resist the Frenzy at all.`
> - 2-6: Major| *Chaos Burn*: `You suffer a penalty of 1 to all Abilities and instantly Panic.`

Energy Damage: **Order**
> *Base Damage*: 3
The energy of perfection, with all the restrictions and inhibition demanded by flawless Order.
> - *11-12*: -| *Release*: `You Risk Minor Panic.`
> - *7-10*: Moderate| *Chained*: `You Risk Moderate Panic and are Castigated for one Round for each 6.`
> - *2-6*: Moderate| *Shackled*: `You Risk Major Panic and are Castigated for one Round for each 6.`
#### Treating Injuries
****
Each Severity of Injury has associated Activities required for treatment, that is, to remove the Injury. Some of these can be performed yourself, while others require help, either for lack of Knowledge, or because you lack the ability.

**Minor Injuries**
These can be removed by Bandaging them, but Recover on their own with Rest.

**Moderate Injuries**
These have a default Recovery Time, equal to ten times the Injury Strain. At the conclusion, their effects are removed.

**Major Injuries**
You need Surgical Treatment before these even begin Recovery. If you have a Major Injury, untreated, for Days in excess of 10 minus the Injury Strain, you die from systemic infection. After Treatment, Major Injuries have a Recovery Time thrice as long as an analogous Moderate Injury.
