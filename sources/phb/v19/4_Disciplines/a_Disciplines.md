Skills represent your capacities, dedication, and cultivated ability. Likewise, Skills have specific applications, decisions made to specialize in one or more Disciplines which express a  particular Skill. For example, two Characters with ranks in Immanence are likely "bookish" in some way, but they may study very different things and have distinct ways of using the same Skill. For example, one might be a Mage, while the other is a Scholar of history.

Disciplines capture differences like this, as well as provide information about the kinds of specialized training one can pursue in the world. Having a Rank in a Skill generally makes you eligible for any of its Disciplines.

**Specialized Tests**
Disciplines can be Tested in place of their governing Skills, which is something called a Specialized Test. Recall Tests are a kind of Specialized Test, and where this Class is usually called for. Your GM can also ask for these to reflect that a Test requires expertise in a niche application of a Skill, as opposed to its wider range. 

When you make a Specialized Test, you add your Training in the Discipline and resolve as normal.

- **Minimum Training** | **Difficulty**:
- *Novice*           | 12
- *Initiate*         | 14
- *Expert*           | 16
- *Master*           | 20

**Feats**
Disciplines also grant your Character more options in gameplay, called Feats (which can be read equally as shorthand for "features" or literally, akin to "Feats of strength"). Each Feat (sometimes called something else like Prayers for Priests or Invokations for Binders) has a Tier. If your Training in a Discipline is equal to its Tier, you gain the Feat.
#### Training Disciplines
****
Because Disciplines are real(fictional) discourses and practices, you need to learn them from someone. This is a Mentor, which can be any Creature (PCs included) with whom you can meaningfully Communicate and has Training greater than your own in the Target Discipline. 

General Activity: **Training**
> *Duration*: Training is a discontinuous Activity with a variable Opportunity Cost. The total Duration in Watches to Advance in a Discipline is equal to twice the next step in Training.
> *Requirements*: A Mentor with Training in the Discipline greater than your own. You must spend EXP to Advance your Training in a Discipline, equal to 50(the next Degree of Training)
> *Description*
> Time, Experience, and study come together, building on the foundations of Skill, upwards towards mastery.
> `At the conclusion, you Advance in the Discipline.`
> *Limits*
> `You can only progress in a given discipline up to twice your Tier. Further, the sum of all your Disciplines cannot exceed six times your Tier.`
> *Collaboration*: No substantial benefits come from Training together, other than mutual acquisition of the benefit of Training itself.

**Finding Mentors**
Mentors are other people, Creatures with Degrees exceeding your own in the desired Discipline. This could be another PC or an NPC met organically. You could solicit Mentorship with a Social Approach, or you could even buy the service.
