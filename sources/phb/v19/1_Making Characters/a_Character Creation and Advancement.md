## Predefinition
---
To play Kriegsmesser, you need a Character. Player Characters are Creatures under your control and whose role you adopt in play.
#### Abilities
****
A Character has Abilities, three values that measure their strengths, distinct from their Skills.
- **Body**: physical power.
- **Mind**: strength of mind.
- **Reflex**: swiftness to act.
#### Secondary Values
****
Abilities then define a number of derived characteristics called Secondary Values. These are:
- **Movement**: equal to your Reflex, Movement is the basic value for all of your Speeds, which measure how fast you can Move.
- **Encumbrance**: equal to your thrice your Body, which measures the ability to carry and haul weight, measured in Bulk. 
> - Bulk is not a literal measure of weight, rather it represents both weight and awkwardness inherent in manipulating this or that object. 
> - You can horizontally push up to twice this value, and vertically lift half of it. 
> - Every point of Bulk over this value counts against your Movement and Initiative by the same amount, until either reaches 0, in which case you must drop something.
#### Tertiary Values
****
A Player Character also has a number of Tertiary Values which are not directly derived from their Abilities. These are:
- **Size**: literally how large you are. This is categorized by your height in Meters:
> - *Small*: 0.5-1.5 Meters
> - *Medium*: 1.5-2.5 Meters
> - *Large*: 2.5-3.5 Meters
> - *Huge and Above*: 4+ Meters
- **Weight**: measured in Bulk, which has a base value set by your Size in Meters times 10. 
> - A Medium Creature usually weighs between 20-30 Bulk, a Large Creature between 35 and 45 etc. 
> - *(Note: your Armor Bulk is added to this Value, while other Equipment does not.*
- **Corruption**: your psychological stability and potential for disorder. This begins at zero and evolves as the game does. 
- **Experience Points**: or EXP for short. These track your Player Character's Advancement and can be Integrated to increase their strengths, Skills, etc.
- **Tier**: which is a rough approximation of how much Experience your Player Character has Integrated. 
## Character Creation
#### A Path
****

**Backgrounds**
Kriegsmesser's Skills are general concepts with both practical and psychological implications. The structure of your initial Skills can explain both how your Character attained their place and why. Mage's uniformly gain Immanence, which represents the concept of ambition, attainment, and looking up. Priests always have Unity, the Skill of fidelity, understanding, and leadership. Etc.

**Archetypes**
The next step is to arrive at your 5 Skills, including 2 of the 4 Principle Skills. This choice will determine your Archetype, which respresents motivation and response to Stress and Panic
#### Abilities
****
In Shards of Life, you roll for your Abilities' initial values in arrays. This is a set of 3 numbers, each determined by a roll of 1d6. Roll two arrays and pick the set you prefer. The numbers from one array can be assigned one to each Ability in any order you like.


*When rolling an Ability Array, you should re-roll 1s.*
#### Advancement
****
Kriegsmesser doesn't discriminate Characters into classes. A Player Character grows by Integrating EXP to gain Feats, Ability Advancements, and Skill Ranks.

*At Character Creation, you gain 500 EXP to spend as you like.*
#### Ability Advancements
****
**INTEGRATION COST**: 100(your Tier)
By Integrating EXP, your Character grows stronger in some way.
- When you Advance an Ability, it increases by 1.
- You can Advance your Abilities no more than 6 times in total before Advancing your Tier.
#### Disciplines and Feats
****
**INTEGRATION COST**: 50(the next Degree of Training)
Integrating EXP with Training from another Character, you increase your Degree of Training in a Discipline and gain a corresponding Feat.
- Your Degree in any Discipline cannot exceed 2(your Tier).
- your total Degree in all Disciplines combined cannot exceed 6(your Tier).
#### Disciplines and Training
****
As these elements of your Character reflect learned applications of their Skills, advancing a Discipline to a given degree of Training requires a Mentor who already has at least that many points in the Discipline. You must spend time with them Training. 

*This does not apply to the Feats gained on Character Creation.*
#### Advancing your Tier
****
**INTEGRATION COST**: *Tier 2*: 300| *Tier 3*: 1,000|
Every so often, your Character will reach plateaus in their development, requiring them to round out their experience, capacities and training to progress further. To Advance your Tier, you must:
- Advance your Abilities 6 times.
- Obtain 6 total Degrees of Training in across your Disciplines.

*Tier Progression*
> Increasing your Tier, subsequently, increases all of your Abilities by 1.
#### The Limits of Mortality
****
The final plateau of mortal capacity is usually the 3rd Tier. Some supernatural circumstances can allow you to transcend this, reaching beyond the limits of your mundane existence. However, most Characters cannot Advance their Tier beyond 3.
