
#### Institutions
****
Structure: **Temple**
> `Tier: T| Durability: T`
> `Cost: 50k×T×(Durability)`
> `Resources-F: -| G: -|
> The heart of Faith, the Temple is established by the placement of an Ordained Keystone, blessed with the authority of the totality of the Priesthood. 
> *Traits*
> - `By communing with the Keystone, one can advance their Training in Faith without the need of a Mentor. Likewise, all Priests have a Buff of one to their Faith within a Temple.`
> - `Unholy Creatures cannot enter a Temple, unless their Tier is greater or they are permitted by a Priest.`

Structure: **Arxiton**
> `Tier: T| Durability: T`
> `Cost: 20k×T×(Durability)`
> `Resources-F: 1| G: -|`
> One of the hinges which makes the Commission possible, the Arxiv is a collection of works that represents the Fundaments of the Arcane, of the Nascene Metaphysics, and the Praxiology Residua. 
> *Traits*
> - `Study of the Arxiv can allow a Mage to advance their Training in Arcane without the need of a Mentor. Likewise, Mages can learn Rituals from the Arxiv, from the collection of Fundaments.`
> - `The Arxiton can also produce the requirements to advance a Mage's Core, supplied the necessary amount of Pure Residue.`

> `Resources-F: -| G: 1|`

Structure: **Refinery**
> `Tier: T| Durability: T`
> `Cost: 20k×T×(Durability)`
> `Resources-F: 1| G: 1|`
> Stocked with a plethora of tools and the collected knowledge of the Verdun sciences, a Refinery provides an Outpost with the means to ascend towards the sophistication of the Arks.
> *Traits*
> - `A Refinery can be used to advance one's Training in Alchemy, and Artifice without a Mentor. The Tier of Items you make within a Refinery is greater by 1, up to a maximum of 4.`

Structure: **Hospital**
> `Tier: T| Durability: T`
> `Cost: 20k×T×(Durability)`
> `Resources-F: -| G: 1|`
> The conditions of the Outpost substantially improved the standing of Barbers, as Hospitalers remain in short supply, Surgery becomes a much more precious need.
> *Traits*
> - `The Hospital can be used to advance one's Training in Surgery without a Mentor. Those Trained in Surgery can treat their Training as one greater within the Hospital.`
> - `One can receive Surgery in a Hospital, rolled with a total Bonus of 4 times the Structure's Tier. Depleted Growth Tokens can also be placed in the Hospital, up to 2T, to restore them within 3 Days.`

Structure: **Academy**
> `Tier: T| Durability: T`
> `Cost: 50k×T×(Durability)`
> `Resources-F: -| G: 1|`
> A place of learning, where one may practice and research. Academies are also the place where the Outpost's new generations will learn, adding to the corpus of Verdun understanding as they become the new vessels of their own heritage.
> *Traits*
> - `The Academy can be used to advance one's Training in Scholarship, Tactics, Elocution, or Leadership without a Mentor. The collection of materials within an Academy is fairly extensive. It automatically contains information in the Grasp of a Scholar of twice its Tier, regarding all known Subjects.`
> - `You can pay a Character to engage in the Study Activity for you at an Academy, they are presumed to have Training in Scholarship equal to twice the Structure's Tier and charge a rate of 30T per Opportunity spent.`
