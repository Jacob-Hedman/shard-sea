#### What is an Item?
****
Items are a subset of Objects, generally requiring you to Equip to benefit from them.

**Hands and Equipment**
Wielding an Item is usually 1 AP Interaction and occupies the Hand used (meaning that it is no longer "free".) How many things you can Wield is a function of available AP and number of Hands, as well as any additional allowances given in an Item's Traits (for example, Worn Items do no need Hands to be Equipped.) Dropping something Wielded in the Hand is a 0 AP Interaction. 

**Durability**
All Objects have a Durability. When Damaged, it becomes Notched. When the number of Notches exceed the Item's Durability, it Breaks. When Broken, the Item must be Repaired to be useful again.

**Repairing Broken Objects**
Notched Items do not behave any differently until they actually Break. Once Broken, the only way to truly repair it is to remove all Notches.

**Traits**
Most if not all Items have Traits, specifying repeated information across Objects. One of the most frequent is a Resource Trait, which states that an Item is made from a Type of Resource. For example, the Craft_Metal Trait means the Item is made from such a substance, and requires it to Craft or Repair.
#### Currency: Plugs, Loops, and Spheres
****
Everything depends on Residue. Without it, the Mages' Guild would be unable to create the Wards that protect the Ark and clean its air or to produce Artifice. In turn, the Land Barons would lack the air and tools needed to produce food, and both Nobles and Makers would lack the necessities of comfort within the Ark. 

So, as it a necessity, scarce, and impossible to forge, Residue itself became the medium of exchange for trade. The form taken is a utilitarian one. A thumb-sized shard of Residue is called a Plug, both the primitive unit of commerce and the standard fuel input for Artifice. Greater denominations exist in orders of 10; the Loop can fit about a wrist and is equal to 10 Plugs, the Sphere fills a fist, and is worth 100 Plugs. Finally, the Globe is the rarest denomination, the size of a head, and is worth 1,000 Plugs.
