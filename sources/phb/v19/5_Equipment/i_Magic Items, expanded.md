These Items are not products of Artifice, usually the product of Arcane Magic or some other means.

**Repair**
Magical Items can be Repaired like any other, but require some amount of expertise (the Skill of Immanence or some other means of familiarity with Magical craft.) 
*Cost*
> In addition to any other materials, Magical Items require Aether, up to thrice their Tier to be repaired. (Aetheric Residue can substitute, at a ratio of 100 to one.)
