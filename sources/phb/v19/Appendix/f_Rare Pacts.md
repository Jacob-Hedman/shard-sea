#### Lucifer, the Son of the Sun
****
> *"Oh Spirit of Glory, we invoke you from the endless sands of Perdition, against those ignorant, against the scorn of fools, in spite we name you King in Truth and promise you justice. Dwell in us put your might in our hands and your foes beneath our feet."*

The Spirit Lucifer is a jealous lord, covetous as he is violent, as magnificent as vicious. Unknown before the Doom, speculations wild as to his origin, from an incarnation of Verdun spite, an ascendant Binder, or even the Prince of Fools arisen as a new king of Hell.

Binding Lucifer is sacrifice, an exequy of majesty and fire; the Flame of Perdition which his Binders wield is a mystery in itself, almost Holy in aspect, burning with the anger of a soul stoke with the wrongs of a thousand thousand years of ignominy and hatred.

**The Pact**
To receive his Power, Binders abandon all other allegiance, at least so long as his dread eye regards their service.
`For every Round that you benefit from Lucifer's Pact, you must give up the benefits of all other Pacts. If you violate this exchange, you suffer 1 Corruption for each Round. Falling to Lucifer's Pact, you erupt in smoke malign flames. This kills you and subjects everying within (Your Pact Strength) times ten Meters to twice the normal Damage from the Flame of Perdition.`

The Flame of Perdition is a cruel implement, a vengeful blade of might and destruction.
`At any time, you can choose to Wield Perdition's Flame. This is not a Reaction or Action at all, and can be used even when Unconscious.`
`While Wielding the Flame, you are shrouded in a violet-yellow corona of incinerating Glory, moving you to the first place in Initiative regardless of rolls; if used during another Creature's Turn, you interrupt them. This also confers a Buff to all of your Abilities equal to your pact Strength, makes you immune to any Conditions or mental domination, and causes all Creatures within your Engagement to suffer Chaos Damage.`
`Each Round spent Wielding the Flame requires you to roll Chaos Dice equal to your Pact Strength, suffering 1 Corruption for each Die that rolls face 6.`
*Conflict*
`If two Binders Wield the Flame at once, their Initiatives are used to break the tie in Turn order.`
#### Buer, Cloven in Pentacles
****
> *"She who nurtures the downtrodden, second mother to the beggar, thief, and first to the Witch, we call your name and invite into our hearth. Meet our hospitality with your own, spirit of transformation and magic, teach us your ways, give us gifts, dwell in us."*

Ancient Buer, the Five-hooved Hag, Ork Mother, these are names from before, from the first Binders of Gaia, then called Witches. Hers is a less overtly sinister bargain, born from a kind of perverse maternity which she and her servants seem to genuinely espouse. 

Binders seek Buer for Hexes, Magical arts of healing and harm. These are both identifiable from the use of a black iron Cauldron, the fetish-object used to focus the powers a Pact with Sacharine Buer.

**The Pact**
Buer styles herself as a patroness of the less fortunate, but her patronage does not discriminate between its beneficiaries. 
`"Tread not the garden underfoot"-Buer regards the impoverished as her fruit, but with a gaze tolerant of tangles and thorns that most would recognize for weeds. Petty criminals, miscreants, thieves, pickpockets, beggars, orphans, urchins, and all rabble must be allowed to prosper in their own way, per the terms of your contract. Unless under threat of your own life or property, you are forbidden from interfering with them. Buer regards most misbehavior like a woodsman might slime and maggots, a necessary part of the lower world. Falling to Buer's Pact, apropos of her inclinations, causes your body to explode in a shower of filth laden with fertile spores and foul, nutritive juices.`

**Buer's Cauldren**
To use her Hexes, you must hold a Black Iron Cauldron in one or both Hands, this Object focuses her presence and can also be used in other ways. Creatures bled to death into the Cauldron (which never overflows with blood) grant you a Whisp, a Magical resource you can spend in the following ways:
- `To remove 1 Corruption at any time from yourself.`
- `To increase your effective Pact Strength with Buer for the next Turn, by 1.`
- `To shed a single point of Strain.`

**Buer's Hexes**
Buer's Hexes are White or Black, White Hexes can generally be Invoked without fear, while Black Hexes have two dangerous effects. First, Black Hexes harm, and mustn't be used against the terms of your Contract. *Second, whenever you Invoke a Black Hex, roll Chaos Dice equal to your Pact Strength, if any roll 6, you suffer 1 Corruption.*

*White Hexes*
> *Crude Balm*
> `Over the course of 10 Minutes, you mix a foul-smelling mixture of animal fat, blood, and bile. Even if these ingredients were poisonous, the resultant concoction dispells Infections and speeds Recovery Time by a number of Days equal to your Pact Strength.`
> *Bone Divination*
> `You cast a handful of knuckle-bones while focusing on a question, the GM will respond with either Weal or Woe, this takes 1 Minute, and you should remain cautious, as the meaning of Weal or Woe is ambiguous at best.`
> *White Sacrament*
> `For 2 AP, you bring both hands your mouth and expel a fragrent cloud of Magic Spores in a Burst with a Radius equal to your Pact Strength, centered on you. Allies in this Burst gain a Buff of 1 to all Tests, and Enemies suffer an equal penalty. The cloud lasts for 1 Round, and gives rise to a ring of white-button mushrooms within the next Day, if possible. These are edible and lightly psycho-active.`

*Black Hexes*
> *The Witch-hammer*
> `For 1 AP, you pronounce a Curse on a Space within 6 Meters. Within 1d6+1 simple-Turns a maul of midnight blackness pounds the area, inflicting Crushing Damage.`
> *Eyebite-Curse*
> `For 3 AP, your eyes begin to shine with a ruby-amber light. Each Round thereafter for 1 Minute, you can Gaze at a Creature for !0 AP, you instantly learn their deepest fear, if any, and make them Vulnerable equal to your Pact Strength for 1 Full-Turn.`
> *Black Smoke Rite*
> `After spending 6 AP, you belch an acrid pall of roiling ebony smoke with a Radius of 6 Meters. Every Creature you choose with Mind less than your Mind and Corruption is Blinded for 6 Minutes. If their Mind is lower by 6, they also become Frenzied for 1 Minute, and you can control their Actions forced by Frenzy. If it is lower by 6 Again, you gain complete control of their Actions for their next Turn, leaving them Blind and Frenzied thereafter.`
