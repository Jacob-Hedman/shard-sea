#### The Shard Sea
****
The current state of that world is important to understanding how one might explore it. Looking from the outside-in, we would see it as follows:
- **The Shield of Heaven**| `100 Kilometers`: A great, golden sphere set with six Cardinal Points, containing the vastness of the Aether within.
> - This is impenetrable from either direction.
> - At each Cardinal Point, the Luminary Temples hang from the inside of the Shield. At their apex, great points of light emit radiance, believed to be drawn from the Sun itself. 
> - Each Luminary is active for 12 or so Hours. 
- **The Upper Aether**| `99-60 Kilometers`: Near the Shield itself, this atmosphere is too thin to support any but the lightest vessels, and is crossed by thin wisps and currents of light.
- **The Surface**| `60-30 Kilometers`: Where the Aether is right for both Alembic enginery and for Shards, great remnants of the once-world of Gaia. 
> - The Shard Sea is also traversed by the great Arks, the vessels created by the ancient Verdun to withstand the Doom. These are usually located just at the height of the Upper Aether, safe from most debris and Storms.
> - The Aether at this height, especially as one goes lower, tends to be more chaotic, prone to disturbance, and even invasion from Storms from the lower layers.
- **The Shallows**| `30-10 Kilometers`: Inferior to the Sea, the Aether here is still traversable, but very dangerous. Debris-fields, Aetheric disturbances, Storms, and other dangers are common.
- **The Depths**| `10-0 Kilometers`: Barely traversable by Alembic engine, this marks the limit of the known world. Whisps of Dark, extremely dense Corrupted Aether swirl and pulse with currents of deadly power. The few that venture here usually forswear it if they survive at all.
- **The Dark**| `0-? Kilometers`: Below this ebon surface, by necessity of known geometry, Scholars have estimated that more than 10,000 Kilometers of unexplored space exists. An immense, spherical expanse of only the Gods know what. None have ever touched this realm and returned.

**The Aether**
Throughout all layers of the Sea, from the Shield to the Dark below (and even within it, though none know for sure), the Aether pervades. Thin, invisible, air-like, and tasteless, Aether is a primal substance, from which Mages conjure Magic and which, in more perverse states, gives rise to all manner of dangers.
*Aether Disturbances*
> Though Aether cannot be seen under normal circumstance, often significant anomalies and events can give rise to visual effects. Mages, for example, emit bright cobalt-blue flares of light while Channeling. Likewise, the surges within the Dark are presumed to be another kind of visible Aetheric phenomena.
*Aether Cannot Satiate*
> Verdun cannot breath Aether, though it is not normally harmful to do so in small quantities. Rather, Aether is no substitute for a wholesome atmosphere. As such, the Verdun carry their breath in Cannisters and other means, produced by the same Alembics that carry them about.

**Shards in the Sea**
In the Surface layer and into the Shallows, one finds Shards, enormous splinters of Gaia' own surface. Shards are all unique, and are the aim of Expeditioners to find and explore. Parties of intrepid Verdun scour these for their riches in Aetheric Residue, crystalized Magical substances that power Artifice. Likewise, many Shards have other kinds of wealth: food, exotic life, and even Nodes where Residue grows as if it lived and Ruins from before the Doom. Shards are also filled with danger, often in the form of that same exotic life and from the terrain of the Shard itself.
*Typology*
> Shards are usually grouped by Type, representing what kind of Terrain one might expect there. The more common Types are:
> - *Veridian*: Swarming with life and greenery of a thousand kinds.
> - *Arctic*: Clad in a thick coat of rime.
> - *Aether-Touched*: Where Residue forms part of the Shard itself.
> - *Volcanic*: Light with an inner flame and constantly belching smoke., 
*Accessibility*
> One of the quirks of a Shard is its Wake in the local Aether, an effect preventing stable travel by Alembic vessel too near it. Aether Wakes can be quelled, but only by extensive installations into the Shard, Pylons, constructed by Outpost Expeditions.
