
#### Panic Risk
****
This represents your Character briefly faltering in a moment of shock, pain, or anxiety, when circumstances briefly overwhelms them. 
- Panic Risk is a rolled Pool of Chaos Dice, with a size according to its Severity.
- If any Chaos Dice in the Pool roll 6, you Panic. The sizes of these Pools are:
****
> *Minor*: 2| *Moderate*: 3| *Major*: 6
****
*Mental Resistance*
> When rolling Panic Risk, you roll fewer Chaos Dice by your Mind Bonus, minimum one.
*The Effects of Panic*
> When you Panic, you suffer Strain for each 6 rolled, and your Panic Response takes effect.
#### Corruption
****
The negative aspect of the psyche, Corruption represents long-term damage and dysfunction, compounding anxiety and eventual collapse.

**The Measure of Corruption**
Starting from zero, Corruption can build over time. Corruption has a Scale, depending on the value, as follows:
- 0-5: *Clear*
- 6-11: *Dark*
- 12-17: *Morbid*
- 18: *Terminal*
*The Effects of Corruption*
> The major effect of Corruption is that, when you Panic, you suffer effects corresponding to its Scale. Likewise, the more Corruption you have, the easier it is to gain more.

**Rolling Corruption**
When you suffer Major Panic, and at GM discretion when your Character is exposed to or, especially, when they enact something genuinely depraved or psychically dangerous, you roll Corruption. This is a Pool of Chaos Dice, with a number corresponding to your current Scale:
- *Clear*: 3
- *Dark*: 5
- *Morbid*: 7
*Gaining Corruption and Falling*
> For each 6 rolled, you suffer a point of Corruption. If this ever raises your Scale to Terminal, you Fall. This is a form of psychotic break, losing control of your Character to the GM. Recovery from Falling is possible, but is something the Fallen Character will always resist, usually to an extreme if not deadly degree.
#### Fixation and Depravity
****
Fall and recovery have other alternatives, when you would Fall, you may suffer a Fixation instead. Fixations are distorted states of mind that both strengthen and parasitize their victims. The Fixated have a unique relationship with the Corruption, namely that they actively feed on it, as their warped state feeds on them.

**Fallen**
When you suffer a Fixation, you shed all Corruption, but lose your Archetype and associated effects. Instead, you gain the effects of the Fixation, which has its own Feat and Panic Response. 

**Depravity**
Instead of a Scale, your Corruption gains a numerical Bonus, like an Ability, with a Value equal to (1 plus every six Corruption.) So, just after Falling, your Bonus would be 1, 2 if you gained 6 Corruption, 3 for 12, etc. This Bonus is called your Depravity, and scales the effects of your Fixation.

**Permanence**
Suffering a Fixation has no natural path to Recovery and is essentially a defining choice, unless your Character receives some extraordinary blessing like genuine divine intervention. 

**Finality**
If you ever suffer 18 or more Corruption after suffering a Fixation, your psyche is totally broken, reducing you to a violent, dangerous husk; an NPC under the control of the GM with no hope of regaining even a facsimile of sanity.
#### The Fixations
****
Fixation: **The Inverse Panopticon**
> *"They hate me"*
> *Feat*: False Armor
> `When you roll Panic Risk, you can reduce the Pool by your Depravity.`
> *Panic Response*: Fracture
> *"Run, run, run run r-"*
> `You are compelled to flee, as fast as you are able.`

Fixation: **Void**
> *"Only I exist"*
> *Feat*: Head Like a Hole
> `You may add your Depravity to any Test to manipulate another.`
> *Panic Response*: Perfection
> *"Give them nothing"*
> `You gain a Boon to all Tests for the next Minute, but every time you fail, you Risk Minor Panic. Critically Failing raises this Severity to Major.`

Fixation: **A Hungry Wound**
> *"They should fear me"*
> *Feat*: Power from the Dark
> `You may add your Depravity to Attacks and can roll Corruption to make the Damage more Vicious by your Tier.`
> *Panic Response*: Burn
> *"Destroy Everything"*
> `You are Frenzied and compelled to kill the closest living thing for Minutes up to your Corruption.`

Fixation: **The Obverse Bend**
> *"Don't tell me what to do"*
> *Feat*: Only Survivor
> `You gain a Bonus to all Abilities up to your Depravity, but each Ally within as many Meters reduces this by one.`
> *Panic Response*: Anti-Synergy
> *"I don't need you"*
> `For the next Minute, your Allies suffer the same penalty as you to their Abilities from your Depravity.`

Fixation: **The Narrow Way**
> *"I need it"*
> *Feat*: Purity
> `You can ignore all other sources of Panic Risk, and gain a repository of Boons up to twice your Depravity to be used for any reason. These replenish when your Rest. However, you have a singular, highly specific Goal. If you fail to pursue or follow it, you suffer Major Panic Risk. Wore, if you ever achieve your Goal, you instantly fall.`
> *Panic Response*: Collapse
> *"No, no, no no no no nononononononononononnono"*
> `You suffer 1d6 Corruption.`
