#### Poisonous and Deadly Substances
****
Some compounds are intrinsically harmful to consume or contact, captured by the Deadly Trait:
> *Deadly_x*: 
> - These have the potential to harm or kill by exposure.
> - When exposed to a Deadly substance, roll Chaos Dice equal to x-(your Height rounded down.) Each 6 rolled Poisons you for 1 Minute. *If you roll more 6s than thrice your Body Bonus, you die.*
> - Each Consecutive Dose of any Deadly substance within 24 Hours is more Deadly by 1 (These do not have to be the same substances.)
> - So, a Height 3 Medium Creature would not need to roll any Chaos Dice upon consuming a deadly_1 substance for the first (or even the third) time, while a Height 1 Small Creature would start rolling after the second dose. Assuming both had 12 Body, either would be killed by six 6s rolled from the Deadly Trait.

#### Disease
****
Diseases represent corruption of the body by microbial, fungal, or viral life and other invasive forces. Each Disease is unique as are the ways of contracting and curing them. 

In general, a Disease has the following elements:
- **Risk**: which gives an initial roll or procedure for contracting it. Contracting a Disease makes you Infected. 
- **Progression**: when Infected, this quantifies how the Disease worsens.
- **Stages**: a Tiered system (beginning at 0, Infection), that gives symptoms and effects as the Disease progresses.
- **Resolution**: The manner by which the Disease can be Recovered from, if at all.
#### Addictions
****
An Addiction is a dependency on a single substance which is always an Item with the Addictive Quality, such that you cannot adequately function without it. When you fail to consume the Item within 48 Hours, you enter Withdrawal and suffer effects depending on the substance. Addictions can be recovered from by suffering withdrawal for a Week. Each use of the substance while in withdrawal resets the remaining time, not exceeding the total.
