
General Activity: **Catching your Breath**
> `If your Strain is not in excess of your Reserve, you shed all Strain. This takes one Minute.`
> *Collaboration*: All participants gain the benefit.

General Activity: **Rest**
> `You Spend Watches to gain the following benefits, depending on the amount of time:`
> - One Watch: `You shed Strain equal to your Tier.`
> - Two Watches: `You shed Strain equal to twice your Tier and Reserve.`
> - Three Watches: `You shed all Strain.`
> - Four Watches: `As three, and all your Injuries also reduce their Recovery time by one.`
> *Degradation*
> `Each Day you spend without Resting at all reduces your Mind by one, which can only be recovered by Resting for three Watches.`
> *Collaboration*: Resting together, beyond comradery, has little effect.
