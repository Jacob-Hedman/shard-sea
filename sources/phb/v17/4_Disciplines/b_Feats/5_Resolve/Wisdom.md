
****
Rarest of Disciplines, issuing from an unknown origin, lost in the Doom. Wisdom is not a spiritual practice, is not an art, and is not even properly a Discipline, in truth. Wisdom is a mentality, one that, when cultivated, can be formed into a weapon against the unreal.

Few practitioners exist, but any can practice, provided they are willing to heed the strange teachings of an adept of Wisdom.

**The Discipline of Wisdom**
You gain the Activity of Meditation.

Exclusive Activity - **Meditation**
	*"I see through it"*
	`For each Watch spent, you shed 1 Corruption.`
	*Collaboration*: You may lead even Unskilled Collaborators in Meditation, who shed Corruption at 1/3rd the rate. The Skilled can also be led, and shed Corruption half as fast as you do.

****
Feat 1: **Emptiness**
	*"Gone, Gone, Gone to the other shore"*
	Through Wisdom, one gains liberation, both from worldly concern and illusion alike.
	`When under a Magical Effect of any Kind, if its Tier is less than or equal to your own, it does nothing. For effects without apparent Tiers, like some Rituals or Magic Items, use that of its maker.`

Feat 2: **Shroud**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"An enfolding curtain of absence"*
	Wisdom can be applied, freeing the mind and body from external circumstance.
	`For the Duration, Your effective Tier for Emptiness is increased by half your Wisdom, and you can spend AP to directly remove any Condition, 1 AP for Minor, 2 for Moderate, 3 for Major, etc. You can also do this as a Reaction to receiving the Condition, and enter this Stance even when Unconscious.`
	

Feat 3: **Negation**
	*"Non-independence of origination"*
	All phenomena are equally empty, Wisdom allows one to trade one for another.
	`When you would be Injured or Panic, you can suffer 1 Persistant Strain to ignore the cause.`

Feat 4: **Release**
	*"Arrival"*
	Being empty, all deceits and confoundments can be dispelled, if not destroyed.
	`While under the effects of Magic of any kind, you can suffer 1 Strain to suspend the effect for 1 Full-Turn.`

Feat 5: **Joy**
	*"At the other shore"*
	The Wise are untroubled by the world, not from ignorance, but because they understand its intrinsic impermanence.
	`When you Rest, you automatically shed 1 Corruption.`

Feat 6: **Nothingness**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"There is nothing without cause, and so in truth there is nothing"*
	Your Wisdom unfolds, a flower of pure white. Against this, no distortion or untruth can prevail.
	`For the Duration, your resistance to Magic applies to everything with an Aura centered on you, with Radius equal to your Wisdom.`
