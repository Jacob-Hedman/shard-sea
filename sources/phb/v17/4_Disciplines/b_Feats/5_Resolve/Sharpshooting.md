****
Archers wield lethality, a tools useful only for reaping. Noble in some respects, many Verdun still regard Sharpshooting with some mixed feelings. Killing from a distance can appear cowardly, and the sheer efficiency of a Trained Sharpshooter can appear almost malicious.

Still, when one's foes are the Corrupted Beasts, Pirates, and Demons of the Shard Sea, some ill intent is almost necessary for survival.

**Passive Effects**
`You can add your Sharpshooting to Ranged Attacks with a Bow.`
****
Feat 1: **Steady Breathing**
	*"Draw with the breath and release"*
	You implant yourself, a living pylon of stability. This allows your shots to gain nearly supernatural purchase, and for your hands to move like the wind.
	`For the Duration, your Movement is 0, but you can Reload Bows for less AP, up to your Sharpshooting and your Ranged Attacks are more Vicious by one.`

Feat 2: **Sighting**
	*"Hold"*
	You retain absolute stillness for a moment, further amplifying your awareness.
	`You can Gauge a Target to make them Vulnerable, up to half your Sharpshooting, to you for one Turn.`
	*Critical effect*
	`Your next Attack against the Target is easier by your Tier.`

Feat 3: **Lethal Insight**
	*"In-between the ribs"*
	Sharpshooting is a cruel practice, in some respect. Its primary use is to kill.
	`When you hit a Vulnerable Target with a Ranged Attack, your Weapon is one more Vicious.`

Feat 4: **One Follows Another**
	*"Toppling a pile"*
	With each success, your momentum mounts.
	`If you Injure a Target with a Ranged Attack, you can make another for !0 AP. Its Damage is Blunted.`

Feat 5: **Rain in Winter**
	*AP*: 3| *Qualities*: Primary
	*"Hail, dagger-sharp"*
	Unleash a storm of lethal points, falling like a rain of stars.
	`You knock several Arrows at once, up to twice your Sharpshooting, firing a Volley within Range. This Creates a Burst, with Radius up to your Sharpshooting. Each Creature within it must roll a Chaos Die. On faces 4-6 they are struck by your Bow.`

Feat 6: **Ice Storm**
	*"Kill them all"*
	Shot after shot, lance after lance of deadly cold, every bit of yourself spent is another field of death.
	`You can fire multiple Volleys at once when you use Rain in Winter, suffering 1 Strain for each. Volleys do not overlap for Damage, but Targets within multiple Bursts roll as many Chaos Dice and take the highest result.`
