****
An entirely mental Discipline, but hardly academic. Iron Will is the practice of strength, control, and resistance. Adepts of the Iron Will can become more than fearless, as a lack of fear is folly, masters of their own mind.

Expeditions often expose themselves to desperate situations, and to effects that distort and warp the mind itself. Iron Will is a path to surviving those dangers.

**Passive Effects**
`When you roll Panic Risk, you can reduce the Pool by one.`
****
Feat 1: **Tempering**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"I must not fear"*
	Through focus, you erect a wall around your mind, a void beyond which the worlds of emotion and external influence slip away.
	`For the Duration, your Reserve is greater by one and you may ignore effects that target your Mind unless their Tier exceeds the same.`

Feat 2: **Feedback Loop**
	*"The branch bent without breaking is made stronger"*
	By resisting, you preserve yourself, and through preservation, you regain strength.
	`Whenever you suffer Panic Risk and do not Panic, you can shed 1 Strain.`

Feat 3: **Necessary Evil**
	*"My failures will not define me"*
	Even when you fall, you can stand up. Perhaps the standing will be slower than before, but you will stand.
	`When suffering from any Condition, you can negate it, no Action required, at the cost of 1 Persistent Strain.`

Feat 4: **Steel Mind**
	*"A standing chill"*
	Rather than fade, you sharpen yourself, again and again you refuse to fall.
	`When you would become Incapacitated or Unconscious, roll a Chaos Die. On faces 1-5, you ignore the cause. Each time you do this the range of success decreases by 1 for the rest of the Day.`

Feat 5: **Quenching**
	*AP*: 1| *Qualities*: -|
	*"Hot and cold"*
	Your mastery of your own mind allows you not just to resist fear, but to feed on it.
	`You can voluntarily suffer Panic Risk of any Severity. If you do not Panic, you shed Strain and your Allies gain any benefits against Stress and Panic that you have for 1 Full-Turn`

Feat 6: **Diamond Mind**
	*AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Strain_~|
	*"Nothing, nowhere"*
	The void is complete, total insulation from even the threat of faltering. However, no one can withstand the void itself for long.
	`For the next Minute, your Reserve is greater by 6, are immune to Panic, and invulnerable to any attempts on your mind, but you suffer 2d6 Persistent Strain at the conclusion of this Action. `
