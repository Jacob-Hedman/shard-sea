****
Span instructs its adepts to wait and strike when the moment demands. Primarily a dedication to the Polearm, it still has lessons for almost any warrior. 

While an older Discipline, Span lacks some of the nobler connotation. Then and now, the Polearm is a practical weapon useful before beauty. This is its own kind of virtue.

**Passive Effects**:
`If you have not taken a Movement Action in the same Round, Tests against you suffer a penalty equal to half your Degree of Span (Min 1).`
****

Feat 1: **Mountain Guard**
	*AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Steady, pensive, like a rock at the edge of a cliff."*
	Hold your Weapon high, ready to punish anything that moves too close or loses focus.
	`For the Duration, you are unable to make Attacks except Attacks of Opportunity. However, Enemies Provoke Attacks from you when they enter your Weapon's Range (including the conditional, extended Range of Polearms.) or Attack an Ally.`

Feat 2: **Warding**
	*"Stay right there"*
	Your reach is a shield and a cage, walls of intent and the point of a spear.
	`When you Attack a Creature with a Polearm, their Movement is cut in half and they are Off_Balance for 1 Full-Turn.`

Feat 3: **Deft**
	*"It's about the grip in the hand"*
	Your Training affords you capacity, expansion at a lesser cost.
	`The penalty from extending the Range of a Polearm is only 1 for you.`

Feat 4: **Cutting the Rigging**
	*"The ship unsailed sinks"*
	Seek the support of your foe, taking their legs from under them.
	`You can use the Range of your Weapon to Trip (Including the extended Range of Polearms), and can substitute the Skill Test with a Melee Attack.`

Feat 5: **The Falling Stone**
	*"Passivity is never weakness"*
	When the moment is right, your revenge is swift, striking the ground and cracking bones.
	`When you make an Attack of Opportunity, you can treat the Strike as if you had spent 3 AP.`

Feat 6: **Steel Across the Sky**
	*AP*: 3| *Qualities*: Primary, Strain_3|
	*"Over and over"*
	You strike at the limits of reason, again and again your Weapon flashes out, only stopping when you falter.
	`Make a 1 AP Strike with the maximum Range of your Polearm, ignoring the accuracy penalty. If you succeed, you can make another with an Accuracy penalty of 1. If this succeeds, you can make another with a penalty of 2, and so on until you miss.`
