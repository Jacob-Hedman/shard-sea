****
The Cleaver is a merciless Weapon designed to cut indiscriminately. Whirl is the Discipline of weaving those cuts into a spinning circle of death.

Learned mostly from Orcish warriors in the Conquest, Whirl adapts Verdun weaponry to a more frenzied style of combat than traditionally applied to bladed Weapons.

**Passive Effects**
`Feats in Whirl require the use of a Cleaver or Axe unless otherwise stated.`
****

Feat 1: **Cleave**
	*"Pivot"*
	Step into your swing and carve.
	`Whenever you make a Melee Attack, you can select additional Targets, up to your Training in Whirl, within Range. All Targets must be unique.`

Feat 2: **Sidewinder**
	*AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Keep loose, stay low"*
	Sword held to the side, you adopt a grim posture, feeding your momentum into the blade.
	`For the Duration, you can move an additional Meter when you Maneuver. Further, if you Attack directly after taking a Movement Action, your Cleaver counts as Heavier, up to the Meters moved, for the purpose of dealing Rending Damage.`

Feat 3: **Cutoff**
	*"Before it falls"*
	At the vital moment, swing and step away, placing yourself outside of an inferior reach.
	`When subject to a Melee Attack, you can make one of your own against them as a 1 AP Reaction. Instead of dealing Damage, this lets you Maneuver before their Attack resolves, potentially putting you out of their Range.`

Feat 4: **Full Moon**
	*AP*: ~| *Qualities*: Strain_1|
	*"Spare none"*
	Abandon caution and embrace the wind.
	`Move and Strike. Everything within Range is subject to this Attack. Injury Risk provoked by this Damage is rolled with a Bane.`
	*Critical effect*
	`This automatically deals Rending Damage, and the Targets instantly begin to Bleed, whether or not you Injure them.`

Feat 5: **Backswing**
	*"Never fail"*
	Every frustration is an opportunity.
	`When you miss with a Melee Attack, you can Target something else within Range as a !0 AP Reaction. This cannot be the same Target or Targets.`

Feat 6: **Harvest Gleam**
	*"Fall"*
	Full embrace the turn, the violent wheel of your own momentum.
	`When you execute a Full Moon, you can increase your Weapon's Range by T and suffer an additional point of Strain.`
