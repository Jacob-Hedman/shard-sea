****
Not all magic was disrupted by the Doom. The Priests never depended on the Aether for their abilities. Instead, a priest is Elected to serve a Deity. Within Verdun Noble Families, the first-born children inherit a Priestly role on their Name Day. Thereafter, they must choose a Deity to serve.

Priesthood is a responsibility, and one that comes with power, both cultural and literal in the form of Prayers, supernatural abilities and effects that received from their Deity. Likewise, Priests serve the crucially important role of communicating with the Celestials outside the Shield of Heaven. Doing so is one of the primary goals of the Church, and cause for many Expeditions to brave the dangers of the Sunspots, the only place where it is possible to receive a Celestial message.

**Responsibility and Virtue**
One becomes a Priest in Character Creation by choosing the Archetype. All Priests have the Tertiary Value of Virtue, which represents your affinity with your Deity. Virtue effects a great deal. Namely, how powerful your Prayers are, and if you can use them at all.

Virtue begins at 1, and is gained from performing an action in line with your Deity's Benediction, the acts and conduct that reflect their nature. Virtue can also be lost by falling short of or intentionally promoting your Deity's Anathemas, the contradiction of their nature.

**The Limits of Virtue**
Increasing your Virtue starts with an accomplishment (or at least a noble effort) towards your Deity's Benediction. This is followed by a Flat Test with a Difficulty of 6 which grants 1 Virtue on a successful roll.

Some limits apply to gaining Virtue; you cannot gain more than 1 Virtue in a Day. Gaining Virtue increases the Difficulty of this Test by 1 for each, up to 12. At the end of the Month, this Resets. *This does not occur during the Holy Month of your Deity.*

Likewise, you cannot gain more than double your Faith in Virtue and, if your Virtue exceeds your Faith, roll 1d6 at the end of the Month. You lose that much Virtue.

*Finally, and importantly, you cannot benefit from a Prayer if your Virtue is less than its Degree.*

**Applications of Skill**
Faith is a study of lore and Divinity. This allows you to read and decode Celestial language, and to study Holy Items and Relics. Likewise, Priests are called to make a study of practices fitting the domain of their Deity. For this reason, a Priest can use their Faith for Specialized Tests pertaining to the following subjects:
- *Lumen*: exploration, government, leadership
- *Luna*: music, cyphers, schemes
- *Telmar*: warfare, battles, law
- *Mora*: the Undead, tradition, funerals
- *Manus*: craftwork, repairs, art
- *Bella*: medicine, herblore, defense
- *Nous*: performance, oration, history

**Passive Effects**
Priests gain Prayers when their Training in Faith is equal to its Degree. Both universal Prayers (Bless, Purgation, and Expiation) and specific prayers from their God.

Forge Priests are called to be humble servants. While their powers are great, no bid for aid should be demeaned. 
**Anathema**
Pride is antithetical to true Creation. Pretention, scorn, and cruelty are deadly sins.
****

Prayer 1: **Forge Light**
	*Manus, lord of making and fashioning, lend me your Light. Invest my hands with your great skill, and make the stones stuff of the earth obey in your name*
	The Priests of Manus bear a fragment of the Forge's Light, causing their forearms to glow cherry red, filled with a heat that softens but never burns. This power is known for its role in the building of almost everything.
	`Your arms constantly radiate soft light, you have a Bonus to your capacity to lift and drag equal to twice your Virtue, and under your Touch, stone and metal have the consistancy of soft clay, bending and warping with no excess heat or damage to their temper. You can adjust this effect at will, gripping a whole sword in one hand, and pressing the other through a stone as if it were butter.`
	`Likewise, this makes your arms and hands nearly indestructible. They cannot be subject to Injury and, further, you can Block using a free hand. This functions as Shield with Hardness equal to your Brawn and thrice your Virtue. You are subject to the Damage if it exceeds this.`
	`Finally, this augmentation to your arms grants your Unarmed Attacks a Bonus to Damage equal to your Virtue.`

Prayer 2: **Soulcasting**
	*"King of the Forge whose Light dwells in my hand, bathe these materials with your power and attend them to me. I call the Elements within and bid them obey, to change and become something new."*
	Under your outstretched hands, the Forge's Light effects change, instant and pure. This ability is largely responsible for the economy of the Arks. Instead of depending on precious metals, which a Forge Priest can make from almost anything, food and Residue dominate, a compound and substance respectively that they are unable to form.
	`You can exchange the materials of Objects you Touch, changing one to another. The mechanics of the change follow the Primal Ring, which are the structure of Elements which Manus' Priests espouse to be the root of all things. The ring is made from the following five Elements:`
	- *Water*: the Element of flux.
	- *Wood*: the Element of growth.
	- *Stone*: the Element of eternity.
	- *Metal*: the Element of strength.
	- *Crystal*: the Element of form.
	`These stand in a loop, with Water adjacent to Crystal. At the center of the Ring lies the sixth Element:`
	- *Life*: the Element of the Soul.
	`When you change something, first identify the strongest consistuent Element; Stone for rocks, Wood for a plank, Metal for a blade, etc. The result is an identically shaped re-imagining of the Target made from a pure form of an adjacent Element in the Ring. Life is an immutable Element, situated appart from the rest. For this reason, you cannot transmute a Creature, living, Undead, Demon, Celestial, etc.`
	`When changing a thing, you can also decide the Phase of the Element, provided that Extreme Heat or Cold generated would not exceed your Virtue.`
	`Plants present a unique exception to the immutability of Life, given that their principle Elements are Wood and Water in equal measure, with Life as a tertiary compound. This means that you can miraculously exchange the substance of a plant while leaving its Life unchanged, producing a Magical plant made from a different Element. Flowers of gold, trees made from granite, and grass formed from ice are all possible applications of this Prayer.`
	`Finally, their are obstructions to Soulcasting. Materials that are Unholy, Magical, and otherwise unnatural, Arcane, Demonic, or otherwise estranged from the natural world cannot be effected. Likewise, while drinkable water can be made, edible food is impossible, because food's nutritive power stems from a complex blend of all Elements, and the results of Soulcasting are pure.`

Prayer 3: **Inner Light**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Master of forms and of strength, ignite the flames in me, let the bellows blow and air sing with the heat of your power."*

	`Your Unarmed Attacks inflict Fire Damage, and when you Block with an arm, you can suffer 1 Strain to negate all Damage (You can see the Damage roll before doing so).`

Prayer 4: **Forge Blessing**

	You have a supernatural knack for making, repair, and construction. In your hands, tools and artifice seem to dance.

Prayer 5: **Supernova**
	*"King of Creation and of making, protect me, temper my body as steel, and preserve me from all ills."*
	Force seems to slide from your arms, transforming from peril to relentless flame.
	`When you negate Damage with your Inner Light, you can emit the same amount, as Fire Damage, in a Radius equal to your Virtue, from an Aura centered on yourself.`
	`Further, you do not suffer any Strain if the negated Damage was already Fire.`

Prayer 6: **The Beginning**

#### Nous, the Storyteller
****
First Daughter of Manus and Mora, Nous is a peculiar Divinity. She has no clear symbol or sign, rather she embodies the Archive, the history of the world, and the Orator, she who tells.

Orators travel the world, what remains of it, gathering tales and stories of what was, what is, and what might be. They are a peculiar Priesthood, as their deity is a strange goddess. Orators are more often Expeditioners than proper members of the Priestly Class.

**Benediction**
Orators are called to inspire, to learn, and to excel in bravery. Uncovering secrets and telling great Truths is their highest directive.
**Anathema**
In the service of their mission, cowardice is a sin, as is sloth, disinterest and disregard for what is noble and Good.
****
Prayer 1: **Nous' Inspiration**
	*"Nous, she that sees our works and tells our histories, let your Inspiration move through me. Let this quicken our hearts, and bring us to great deeds."*
	Orators are not scholars. Their purpose in learning and keeping is more than memory. Rather, their calling is to transform the past into the vibrance of the present.
	`You have a pool of Inspiration, equal to your Virtue. This can be spent in any of the following ways:`
	- `When you or an Ally make a Test, you can spend Inspiration to grant a Boon, before or after you see the result.`
	- `Before making a Test, you can Spend Inspiration to grant yourself a Bonus to the roll.`
	- `You can spend 2 or more Inspiration to negate Panic Risk.`
	- `You may increase the Disposition of a Target by 1.`
	`All Inspiration is regained when you Catch your Breath, when you or an Ally critically succeeds a Test or exceeds its Difficulty by 3 or more.`

Prayer 2: **World's Stage**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Keeper of stories, heavenly Witness, see me now. Look on our efforts and shine your favor on our bravery."*
	You strike up a heroic posture, speaking litanies of victory and hope.
	`For the Duration, within an Aura centered on you with a Radius up to your Virtue, all Tests made by Allies and yourself gain a Bonus of 1, and when subject to a Melee Attack, you can spend 2 Inspiration to reduce the roll by your Virtue. If this would make it a critical failure, you regain Inspiration.`

Prayer 3: **Investiture of Hope**
	*"Nous, daughter of both Start and Conclusion, enliven us with your grace, guide our footsteps and strengthen our arms."*
	The grace of the Witness begins to truly live in you, a quickening, shining thing with silver edges.
	`While you have Inspiration, you gain an equal Bonus to your Agility and Movement Speed. If you spend all remaining Inspiration on a single Test (for yourself or another Creature) you can make it a Flourish. This confers the effects of a critical success if it succeeds, but does not replenish your Inspiration.`

Prayer 4: **The Promise of Victory**

Prayer 5: **Against all Odds**

#### Bella, the Hearth Flame
****
The youngest of the Verdun pantheon, Bella gives warmth to homes, comfort to the weary, and hospitality to the good. Often represented as two figures, a young girl wrapped in the arms of a grandmother, this pair signifies the spirit of her being. Bella is the persona of divine mercy, given form in fire.

Hospitallers, her Priests, are healers, able to tend to the Injured and Sick. Their creed and method reflects the nature of their Deity: Joyful, as the young Hearthmaid. Austere, as the elder. Alive, as the flame.

**Benediction**
Hospitallers heal the sick, help the weary, and protect the weak. The essence of their calling is the sacrifice of the self, sometimes even literally.
**Anathema**
Servants of the Hearth Flame and wielders of the Breath of Life, Hospitallers are forbidden from causing harm, unless at the direction of a sacred authority and for good purpose.
****
Prayer 1: **Purifying Fire**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Bella, unleash your fire in me, awaken the Breath and ignite your will!"*
	A pair of flames, green and crimson, dance in your palm before racing out to circumscribe a holy ground for you. The latter, the Hearth Flame, warms while the former, a Spark of the Breath of Life, can be bidden to heal.
	`For the Duration, all Spaces within an Aura centered on you equal to your Virtue are as many steps Warmer for counter-acting Extreme Cold, and all Allies within can subtract your Virtue from Fire Damage or Stress they suffer (including and especially Stress from Panic).`
	`You can Heal any Creature within your Purifying Fire, as if using a Bandage as a Specialized Faith Test. This does not require materials and, if you use more than 1 AP, sheds 1 Strain from the Target.`
	`When you fail to Heal a Target, they suffer Minor Panic Risk.`

Prayer 2: **Hearth Emmenation**
	*"Merciful Daughter, Hearthmaiden of the above, I seek a spark from the greater flame."*
	A finger of cheerie flame erupts from the earth under your fingertips. Basking in its warm, one might glimpse either of Bella's figures in silluette within it.
	`By spending 10 Minutes in incantation, you can invoke a Hearth Emmenation, a harmless cherry-red mote of fire, within Engagement. Hearth Emmenations radiate the Aura from your Purifying Flame and, within that area confer the following effects:`
	- `Unholy Creatures suffer Extreme Heat at the Tier of your Virtue unless you invite them in.`
	- `All Creatures benefit from a Rest as if they had spent an additional point of Opportunity, and shed 1 point of Corruption per Rest.`
	- `You can dispel and forbid any Magical effects of a Tier less than your Virtue.`
	`Hearth Emmenations persist until you dismiss them.`

Prayer 3: **Purgation's Flame**
	*"Elder, Maiden's watcher, I seek the austerity of your will, with which I may dispel rot and banish the wicked."*
	Your grasp of the Hearth Fire deepens. This fire can be made to heal, but the penance it extracts can be great.
	`When you Heal an Ally, you can Amplify the power of your flames, both restoring and stripping away rot. This removes 1d6 Strain, but provokes Minor Panic Risk from the pain.`
	`Likewise, if you Heal an Unholy Creature, you can deny it any benefits, inflicting only the Panic of failure. If it is a Mindless Undead, this inflicts 3d6+(your Virtue)d6 Fire Damage.`

Prayer 4: **Lesser Aspect of the Breath of Life**
	*"Bella, she who waits at the end and who is the start of new journeys, instruct me in the intimacies of the fire inside."*
	Combining both Flame and Breath, your understanding verges on the meaning of Mercy, first apprehending its harsher dimension. A Hospitaler's touch is able to reknit flesh and bones, be it at the cost of the pain of correction.
	`When you Heal an Ally, you can penalize your Test by 1 to remove a Minor Injury, 2 to remove a Moderate Injury, and 3 to remove a Major Injury. Targets suffer Panic Risk of corresponding Severity from the pain.`
	`Failing to remove an Injury always inflicts Major Panic Risk.`

Prayer 5: **Mercy**
	*"Watcher at the door and guardian of the home, tell me of that greater wisdom, of the natures of Flame and Breath, of the sythesis of Mercy."*
	Hospitallers teach that the real essence of Mercy is love, that true sort which is in itself the highest kind of sacrifice. At the penultimate step of your sanctification, you begin to truly understand this.
	`During your Purifying Fire, you can suffer Panic Risk in the stead of anyone you choose, suffering 1 Strain from the pain instead of needing to roll.`

Prayer 6: **The Hearth Flame**
	*AP*: 3| *Qualities*: Primary, Strain_~
