****
Conversation isn't usually considered a formal Discipline, but a Rhetorician would disagree. Further, that disagreement might just win you over.

Nobles tend to be the frequent students of Elocution, as are Nous' Priests.

**Passive Effects**
`You can add your Elocution to the roll when you Preempt.`
****
Feat 1: **Humourism**
	*"Checking the stove"*
	Everyone has emotional currents, convection waves of hot and cold perception. Reading these Vital energies from tone and behavior is essential to proper conduct.
	`When speaking with a Creature, you can pick a Topic (a subject or specific conversation) and learn their subjective "Temperature" about it. Temperature tells you the strength of their emotions about the Topic. The hotter, the stronger. This doesn't tell you if the emotions are necessarily positive or negative, but you do not have to mention to Topic at all to gain this.`
	*Limits*
	`You can gain this information for the same Creature about a number of Topics up to your Elocution.`

Feat 2: **Whistling in the Dark**
	*"You should have seen the other guy"*
	Rhetoricians study both feeling and speech, with this Training they can blunt even the sharpest emotional sting.
	`When an Ally suffers Panic Risk, you Communicate with them, granting them Resilience to Stress from it, up to your Elocution.`

Feat 3: **Daring**
	*"Easy now"*
	Hot running blood can be a tool of persuasion if treated with care.
	`The Disposition of a Target is increased by 1 regarding Topics with a high Temperature, and their Attitude is better by 1 about those with low Temperature.`

Feat 4: **Good Humour**
	*"Laughter is the best medicine"*
	You are hardly a Chemist, but your Training allows you to provide similar relief.
	`When an Ally benefits from your Whistling in the Dark and does not Panic, they shed 1 Strain.`

Feat 5: **Nudging**
	*"Feather light"*
	With care application of pressure, the internal landscape of flame and ice can be molded to purpose.
	`When speaking with a Creature for longer than 1 Minute, you can temporary raise or lower their Temperature related to a Topic, consequently blunting or heightening their emotional responses related to it.`

Feat : **Ride the Lightning**
	*"Stoke the flame"*
	You can channel emotions like others might Magic.
	`When engaging in any Activity about a Topic with especially high Temperature (implying it was already high and then you increased it), their Disposition cannot fall below 1, allowing you to keep them talking for as long as you'd like.`
