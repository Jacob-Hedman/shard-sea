****
Ancient as life, the Discipline of seeking life to take and gather. Hunters would have been a kind of Maker before the Doom, walkers of the wilds and a valuable source of food and goods. Now, the Shard Sea is the only hunting ground which remains, a grander and more dangerous place than any field or wood of lost Gaia.

Hunters are Expeditioners, seeking bounty, discovering new Creatures, and, most of all, surviving.

**Passive Effects**
`When Harvesting, you can substitute the value of your Hunting for Traversal for all purposes. When Surveying, you also learn more about the local Fauna than others. For example, at least one Species you might encounter, regardless of your roll.`
****

Feat 1: **Pitfall**
	*"Careful where you step"*
	Hunting is never about a fair fight.
	`By spending 1 Minute, you can leave a Snare in a Space or Spaces. This has one of the following effects when a Creature first enters it:`
	- `The victim is knocked Prone and Restrained until they untangle themselves for 3 AP.`
	- `The victim suffers Cutting, Piercing, or Crushing Damage.`
	- `The Snare makes a loud noise.`
	- `The Snare produces the effect of an Interaction in the same Space or an Adjacent Space.`
	`Creatures do not automatically see your Snares. Finding one requires a Test against your Hunting.`

Feat 2: **Hunter's Awareness**
	*"Breath deep, savor the wind"*
	You are keenly aware of your surroundings, simple smells, tracks, and currents of air are enough to warn you what you might face.
	`You are always passively aware of the kind of Creatures within a Kilometer of you, as well as the relative number (Eg. tens, hundreds, thousands, etc.) Likewise, when you Gauge a Creature, you can treat them as having a greater Weakness according to their kind:`
	- *Living*: Piercing Damage
	- *Undead*: Incendiary Damage
	- *Demons*: Order Damage
	- *Celestials*: Chaos Damage
	`Only you benefit from this Weakness, which is equal to the Creature's Tier.`

Feat 3: **Instant Ambush**
	*"Not so fast"*
	You push yourself further, move faster, reactions running through your nerves, fire on dry twine.
	`When Initiative begins, you can put yourself at the top of the Turn Order, but you suffer 1 Strain at the end of that Turn.`

Feat 4: **Killing Move**
	*"Time to collect"*
	Hunters are the cousins to the Assassin, both Train themselves to dispatch foes as swiftly as they are able and more.
	`When you Attack a Creature before they act in the Turn order, you can treat all of their Vulnerabilities as twice the normal value, and they roll their Injury Risk with a Bane.`

Feat 5: **Fast Setup**
	*"Careful now"*
	You move in a blur, pulling ropes tight and cover over.
	`You can lay a Snare instantly, but suffer 1 Persistant Strain.`

Feat 6: **Harvest Moon**
	*"Grim and glorious"*
	Like the asymmetry of height, more deadly is the asymmetry of time.
	`When you are the first Creature to act in a Round, your Critical Success range for all Tests is greater by half your Hunting.`
