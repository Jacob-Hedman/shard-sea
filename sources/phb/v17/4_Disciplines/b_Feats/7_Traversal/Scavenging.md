****
The Shard Sea is a waste, full of detritus and cast offs and lost things. Scavengers are the experts in finding, adepts of making much of very little. As such, many Expeditioners pick up at least a little Training here.

Dedicated Scavengers have little role in the Ark other than replenishing their Resources for the next Expedition. This earns them something of a reputation, not wholy negative, but perhaps a little demeaning.

**Passive Effects**
`Your Encumberance is increased by your Scavenging, as is the value you can create when Scrounging.`
****
Feat 1: **Deep Pockets**
	*"I wish we'd thought of that"*
	Scavenging is a highly practical Discipline, teaching one to be resourceful and efer-ready to produce the right thing at the right time.
	`You have an amount of Resources equal to twice your Scavenging. Under the circumstance where you need an Item or would benefit from it, could have plausibly obtained it and transported it to your current location, you can produce it, spending as many Resources.`
	*Backwards Causation*
	`Producing an Item retro-actively applies all the conditions under which it would have been obtained, including incumbrance and cost. If you could not have acquired it for these or other reasons, you cannot produce it.`
	`Your Resources replenish whenever you visit an Ark or when you Scrounge, which restores Resources up to the Opportunity Cost.`

Feat 2: **One Eye Open**
	*"I'm a light sleeper"*
	No one can really tell when you're out of it, some times it seems like you never really sleep.
	`You gain 1 free AP when rolling Initiative, which you may spend on any Action or save for Reactions, in addition to any Agility.`
	*Springboard*
	`If you are Unconcious or otherwise insensible in a Scene, you can Coin-flip to retro-actively be considered Awake, but pretending to be asleep. This does not apply to Unconsciousness induced by a Consumables or being knocked out by Damage, Stress, etc.`

Feat 3: **Emergency Use**
	*"Hold on a second"*
	You always keep a little bit in reserve.
	`You can over-draw from your Resources. This inflicts Persistent Strain equal to the difference and depletes them until you reach an Ark or Outpost.`

Feat 4: **Helpful Reminders**
	*"Are you sure?"*
	You prod a comrade, prompting them to remember what you said they should bring from the Ark.
	`When you use your Deep Pockets, you can use an Ally's circumstances instead of your own, at the cost of 3 additional Resources.`

Feat 5: **Playing Possum**
	*"Maybe"*
	You'd never admit to being shifty, but that doesn't change the fact that getting a read on you is basically impossible.
	`You can Coin-Flip to apply the benefits of One Eye Open to any kind of Unconsciousness, Incapacitation, or other limitation on your mental awareness other than death.`

Feat 6: **All the Time in the World**
	*"Are you approaching me?"*
	It's not accurate to say you can literally pull extra time from a hat, but it looks like that sometimes.
	`You can spend Resources to gain extra AP.`
