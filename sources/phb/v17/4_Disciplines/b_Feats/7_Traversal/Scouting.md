****
Search and discovery are incredibly valuable talents. Scouts are iconic Expeditioners, able to move quickly over long distances and keep an eye on a multitude of threats.

A Scout can be lifesaver out there, making this one of the most commonly taken Disciplines. Better to know what's coming and where from.

**Passive Effects**
`Your Reflex is increased by half your Scouting, and you can Survey as a Scouting Test.`
****

Feat 1: **Ear to the Ground**
	*"Quiet"*
	You have a knack for knowing where things are and where they might be soon.
	`When you Gauge a Creature, you can leave a Trace on them. A Trace allows you to generally intuit their current location at any time. You can also Indirectly Gauge Creatures by studying tracks, dropped Items, or other leavings no older than twice your Scouting in Hours.`
	`Traces have a Duration of 24 Hours.`
	
Feat 2: **Scout Conditioning**
	*"High ground"*
	Limber limbs and sharp eyes are the key features of a good Scout.
	`You gain an Increase to your Climbing Speed, equal to twice your Scouting and, when above a Creature, can visually Spot and Gauge them from as far away as a Kilometer.`

Feat 3: **Found You**
	*"Right where I knew you would"*
	Knowing where someone is can be as good as a target on their head.
	`Traces can be Consumed to automatically Spot a Creature. When you do so, you gain a Bonus equal to your Hunting to Tests against them for the next Full-Turn.`

Feat 4: **Wirey**
	*"Gliding across the ground"*
	Long treks are part of the job description, an expectation you have more than enough experience with at this point.
	`You can ignore Difficult Terrain if its Tier is less than your Scouting. Likewise, when not on Difficult Terrain, you gain half as much as a Bonus to your Movement.`

Feat 5: **Caught Out**
	*"No escape"*
	When you're on the trail, nothing gets far.
	`When you Consume a Trace to Spot a Creature, your Attacks against them cannot miss, and Tests made against them that succeed by more than 3 critically succeed.`

Feat 6: **Omnipresence**
	*"I can smell you"*
	Scouts have sharp senses as a norm, but yours are simply uncanny.
	`You always know where a Traced Creature is, all your Senses including hearing and Smell are as good as Vision for the purpose of Targetting them. Likewise, all Tests against them are easier by 1.`
	*Re-Iteration* `When you would Consume a Trace, you can spend one more AP, or suffer 1 Strain to keep it instead.`
