****
An endless barrage of glinting missiles. Practitioners of Cascade are masters of the Knife, specifically of its use in throwing.

Lending itself to trickery, evasion, and even performance, Cascade is a popular Discipline for its lethality as well as flair.

**Passive Effects**
`When Attacking with a Knife, you can Blunt its Damage to count the Attack as a General Action.`

****
Feat 1: **Slingers Gate**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Follow the flash"*
	"Slingers" earn a reputation for fighting dirty, rapidly whipping spinning steel with each stroke of either hand.
	`For the duration, Throwing a Knife does not Provoke Attacks unless the Target is Engaged, you can add twice your Cascade to the Range of Knives when Thrown.`
	*Critical effect*
	`Critical hits with Knives deal double the normal Damage.`

Feat 2: **Endless Supply**
	*"Two?"*
	As if you only had two, Slinger's coats are lined with leather straps for a single reason. There's always another knife.
	`When you Throw a Knife, you can draw and Equip another for 0 AP. Further, you can make a free 1 AP Strike with a Knife, before or after Throwing one.`

Feat 3: **Pinpricks**
	*"Go for the heart, settle for the shoulder"*
	You might consider yourself a cartographer of a sort, leaving a trail of spikes stuck into your goals.
	`When a Creature is hit by a Knife you release, it Sticks whether or not it Injures them. Each Stuck Knife makes them 1 more Vulnerable, and takes a 1 AP Interaction to remove.`

Feat 4: **Sweet Release**
	*"Took you long enough"*
	Life's a story, one with many stages, steps, and halts. You are especially good at making each one hurt in the end.
	`When you Throw a Knife, you can Consume all Stuck Weapons (dropping them from the Target after this Attack is resolved) to double their Vulnerability from Stuck Knives.`

Feat 5: **Missed?**
	*"Never"*
	Happy accidents are just your speed, no time for second guesses.
	`Even when you miss a Target with a Thrown Knife, it still Sticks.`

Feat 6: **Blood Hail**
	*AP*: 0| *Qualities*: Strain_1|
	*"Arrowheads"*
	Deceptively simple, the greatest feat of Cascade is the Knife thrown between the blink of an eye.
	`You Throw a Knife.`
