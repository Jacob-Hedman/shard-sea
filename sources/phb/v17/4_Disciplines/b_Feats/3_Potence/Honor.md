****
A strict Discipline of martial prowess, Honor is the ancient test of will and body, coordinated in the smooth arc of time.

One of the best preserved Disciplines from the age before the Conquest, Honor is the dedication to the Saber. Noble, expressive, and demanding, Honor is a surprisingly powerful tool in the Shard Sea.

**Passive Effects**
`Feats of Honor require a Saber, unless otherwise stated. Your Attacks of Opportunity deal more Damage, up to your Honor.`
****

Feat 1: **Parry**
	*"Honor stands"*
	As danger looms, you do not fall back. Instead, the mettle of your Honor rises to meet it.
	`When subject to a Melee Attack, you can make a Potence Test with a Difficulty equal to their roll as a 1 AP Reaction. If you succeed, their Attack fails and they Provoke an Attack from you.`
	*Critical effect*
	`Your Provoked Attack is more Vicious, by half your Honor.`

Feat 2: **Fluidity**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Flexible as a river, lighter than air"*
	Adopt a loose grip with blade forward, a deceptively aggressive attitude that allows your sword to bite in a heartbeat.
	`For the Duration of this Stance, you gain a Buff of 1 to Potence Tests and can choose one of the following optional effects when you Parry:`
	- `Even if you fail, you can Blunt the Damage unless it is greater than your Honor.`
	- `The Potence Test is rolled with a Bane and the foe does not Provoke an Attack, but the AP Cost is reduced to 0.`
	- `The Potence Test is rolled with a Bane, but you can attempt to Swipe the Foe's Weapon as part of the same Action.`

Feat 3: **Precision**
	*"Know the design of your enemy, foreknow their demise"*
	Your sense of what could be is keener than any Saber.
	`Sabers are more Vicious for you by one.`

Feat 4: **Slipstream**
	*"Honor is a tree, it bows before the wind which slays the inflexible."*
	Passing under the edge, you can inflict your own punishment.
	`When you Maneuver, you can spend an additional AP while passing through a Creature's Engagement to force them into Provoking an Attack from you.`

Feat 5: **Deflection**
	*"Intention is not the Law"*
	Rather than let the force of failure go to waste, you can drive it where you will.
	`When a foe misses you with a Melee Attack, you can redirect it, making something else other than the foe within its Range the Target.`

Feat 6: **Sunrise**
	*AP*: 0| *Qualities*: Strain_3|
	*"Honor is a contest, a lethal comparison of skill"*
	Light gleams from your flourish, an arc of shining steel swiftly seeking a soft home.
	`Make an opposed Potence Test against a foe within Melee Range. If you succeed, they Provoke an Attack from you and become Stunned for 1 Round. Repeated use increases the Tier of this Condition up to 3.`
	*Critical effect*
	`The subsequence Attack is made with 3 Boons.`
