****
An Ancient Discipline, the art of taking life. Assassins are at home in the dark, ready to spring at a moment's notice.

Taking a life in this way is a useful skill for an Expeditioner, though one might be disturbed to learn that most Trained Assassins tend to find their work inside the Ark.

**Passive Effects**
`You can add your Assassin to Attacks against Creatures that do not Detect you.`
****

Feat 1: **Sneak Attack**
	*AP*: ~| *Qualities*: Primary|
	*"Now"*
	Simple approach, deadly consequence.
	`Move for 0 AP, up to your Movement and Strike, Throw, or Shoot for at least 1 AP. Both of these are combined to one Action for Sneaking.`

Feat 2: **Surprise**
	*"What's that sound"*
	Taken in the dark, your enemies are left reeling.
	`When you Damage a Creature that does not Detect you, they become Stunned for Rounds equal to your Assassin.`

Feat 3: **Tainted Strikes**
	*"Blood on the tongue"*
	Assassins and poison are friends far older than the Arks.
	`You can transmit any Poison via an Attack, regardless of its normal transmission modality.`

Feat 4: **Lethal Jolt**
	*"Falling in your sleep"*
	When you spring your trap, few things can keep their feet.
	`When you Strike a Creature that does not Detect you, they fall Prone.`

Feat 5: **Killers Intuition**
	*"To the heart"*
	Presented with a passive mark, you always know your way to the core.
	`Against Creatures that do not Detect you, Major Injuries are lethal.`

Feat 6: **Mortal Blow**
	*"Time cut off"*
	You can shorten the distance between life and death, the space between breath and not.
	`When you succesfully Attack a Creature, you can suffer 1 Persistent Strain to expand your Critical Success range. You can do this as many times as you wish for the same Attack, potentially turning into a critical success after rolling.`
