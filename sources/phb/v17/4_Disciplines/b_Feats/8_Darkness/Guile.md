****
Liars are never popular, at least not when you know them. Otherwise, they run the show. While hard to sympathize with and easy to scorn, the Liar is a useful ally when things go bad.

The Nobility tend to favor this Discipline to some degree, but almost everyone ends up having to distort the Truth somewhere, which is what Guile teaches one to do.

**Passive Effects**
`You can add your Guile to the roll when you Preempt.`
****
Feat 1: **Silver Tongue**
	*"Honeyed words"*
	Guile teaches one to stupify with words, to take control of another's will in a way that fools them into thinking they remain in control.
	`Creatures with Mind less than yours are Gullible for you. You have a Boon to Impress them, two if you're lying.`

Feat 2: **Liar's Sense**
	*"We'll see"*
	You have a pre-cognitive grasp of the limits to the Truth, how much it can be bent before breaking entire.
	`When speaking with a Gullible Creature, you can loosely infer whether or not a lie is plausible to them, learning their Attitude, asking the GM before you'd Deceive them.`

Feat 3: **Countercharm**
	*"Not too clever"*
	While not impervious to deception, your familiarity with the Discipline makes it difficult to pull the wool very far.
	`If a Creature is lying to you, you know, just not what about. You can also treat Scrutiny as a Darkness Activity to find that out.`

Feat 4: **Internal Rehearsal**
	*"Did I?"*
	You're always at least a sentence ahead, pantomiming yourself to yourself. It's not like you can un-say something, so much as choose to omit a flubbed line from the play.
	`You can Retract something you said within the last number of Seconds equal to twice your Guile. This retro-actively removes the sentence from the game entirely and replaces it with something harmless.`

Feat 5: **Sly**
	*"That sound right?"*
	The irony of the Liar is their intimate grasp of the Truth.
	`You can infer Attitude even if the Target isn't Gullible.`

Feat 6: **Subversion**
	*"Watch out!"*
	When you spot weakness, you can sieze it fully, bending weaker minds to your own ends.
	`A Gullible Creature you Communicate with must spend 1 AP doing whatever you wish, so long as it does not literally harm it, like a self-Attack. Walking into a hazard does not count as literal self-harm.`
