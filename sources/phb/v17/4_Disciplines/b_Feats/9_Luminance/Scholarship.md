****
Intellectuals and practical researchers alike practice the Discipline of Scholarship; not the study of a subject, but the investigation of knowledge itself.

Mages and Nobles both find Study useful, the latter especially. Understanding itself is a concern for people with the time, in the latter case, or the necessity due to the abstraction of their Discipline, in the case of Mages.

**Passive Effects**
`When you Study, a failure by a margin equal to or less than your Scholarship still results in you learning whether the answer exists in a given Source.`
****
Feat 1: **Academic Interest**
	*"Study by division"*
	All knowledge is a kind of dissection, taking a thing apart to understand its internal workings. Scholars simply formalize this, as they do most things.
	`All kinds of information can be grouped under a Subject, a species of knowledge. Relative to any Subject, you have a Grasp, which quantifies your past academic experience with a Subject.`
	- *Esoterica*: The Sacred, Eldritch, and other unnatural things.
	- *Culture*: People, places, customs, and the every-day.
	- *Antiquity*: Lore, events, and forgotten names.
	- *Nature*: Plants, Beasts, the People, and other living things.
	- *Law*: Contracts, statues, government, and the judicial.
	- *Arts*: Music, literature, paintings, and aesthetics.
	`Your Grasp of a Subject allows you to automatically answer certain Questions without needing to Study, recalling it from your mental library.`
	- *1*: Surface level reading, such as common terms or concepts.
	- *2*: A working inventory of names and ideas, able to confidently describe to a novice.
	- *3*: Core conceptual knowledge, though not enough to make novel predictions.
	- *4*: Working understanding of the Subject as a theory, capable contributing new ideas.
	- *5*: The current state of the Subject itself, whose believes what and why.
	- *6+*: Deep understanding, expected of a dedicated master of the Subject.
	`When you gain this Feat, you can choose to increase your Grasp of two Subjects by 1. Further, when Gauging a Creature whose Tier is equal to or less than your Understanding of a Subject that studies it, you automatically succeed.`
	*Increasing your Grasp*
	`Your Grasp can never exceed thrice your Tier, and, when you Study, if the Sources have a reasonably comprehensive depth, you can increase your Grasp of a Subject by spending 3 or more Opportunity.`

Feat 2: **Applied Study**
	*"I've read about this"*
	Your experience taking ideas apart can transfer to taking apart foes.
	`When Gauging a Creature, you gain an Opening for them, a natural defect which you can Exploit. Exploiting an Opening Consumes it and grants you a Bonus equal to your Scholarship to a Test made against them.`

Feat 3: **Sparks of Insight**
	*"Slipping through fingers"*
	Study is a reward in itself, both practice and tool.
	`Each time you Study a Subject, you gain a temporary Buff to your Grasp of it, equal to half the Opportunity spent. This Buff decays by 1 each Day.`

Feat 4: **Domain Knowledge**
	*"Predictable"*
	Academic understanding has a reputation for being ephemeral, overly abstract. Your expertise is an exception.
	`If your Grasp of a Subject which studies a Creature is greater than their Tier, you always have an Opening for them.`

Feat 5: **Instruction**
	*"No, there"*
	Now having learned, you can educate, making a gift of your Study.
	`By Communicating with an Ally as a 1 AP Reaction, you can give them one of your Openings. They only gain half the benefit of Exploiting it.`

Feat 6: **Return on Investment**
	*"It was always for this"*
	With enough research, your understanding can make any effort effortless.
	`When you Exploit an Opening for a Test against a Creature whose Tier is less than your Grasp of a Subject that studies it, you can change a success into a critical success.`
