****
The Discipline of collective action, Tacticians studied crowds and formations of soldiers in lost ages. Now, groups are more disorganized, all the more reason for Tacticians.

Nobles and Lumenaries often Train in Tactics, as the position in the lead is frequently safer, and fitting for authority.

**The Discipline of Tactics**
****
Exclusive Activity: **Make a Plan**
	*"This way and that"*
	`Spend at least one Watch. You record a specific Plan, a sequence of Actions taken in Initiative by Members (Yourself and Allies) up to your Tactics.`
	*Specificity*
	`Plans identify the exact Actions taken in Initiative, assuming a simultaneous Turn for all Members and their order. For example, 'John Grabs the Target I point to, then Suzette Shoots them.' If the sequence cannot take place, owing to a lack of AP, of specific Targets, or lack of a Member in a Plan, you cannot Execute it. If such an impossibility becomes the case during a Plan, it ends.`
	*Duration*
	`When Making a Plan, the total AP called for cannot exceed twice the Watches spent Making it. During its simultaneous Turn, Members of a Plan have 3 AP to spend for its sequence of events, even if they otherwise wouldn't (from the Stunned Condition, for example).`
	*Executing a Plan*
	`At any time, you may Execute your Plan as a Reaction, instantly bringing all of its Members into one simultaneous Turn at the present Initiative (as if you had Preempted the current Turn.) Then, the Plan either takes place exactly as specified, or ends when its sequence is broken/becomes impossible to carry out.`
	*Duration and Aftermath*
	`Foes can still React to a Plan, but this does not break it unless the effect of the Reaction does so as outlined above. More importantly, the simultaneous Turn of a Plan cannot be interrupted in any other way than Reactions while it remains in effect. Members in a Plan return to their previous places in the Turn Order after it has taken place, including yourself.`
	*Limits*
	`if you were a Member in your own Plan, Execution counts as a Primary Action for you. Further, you may only have a number of Plans up to your Tactics at any time. After Executing one, it is Consumed and must be Planned again.`
	*Collaboration*: Members of the Plan must Collaborate. If Trained, they can pool their total spent Opportunity towards the effective total.

****
Feat 1: **Coordination**
	*"You go left, I go right"*
	Tactics is the study of sequences and people, collecting a disorganized mob and transforming them into a machine.
	`When you Flank, you can Coin-Flip to make the Target Off_Balance for 1 Simple-Turn.`

Feat 2: **Prediction**
	*AP*: 1| *Qualities*: General|
	*"Called it"*
	Foreknowledge is divine. You might call yourself a secular authority on the subject.
	`You make a verbal (out loud) Prediction about an Enemy, such as "they will Attack (so and so)" or "you will Interact with (such and such)". If they follow your Prediction, they become Off_Balance for 1 Full-Turn.`

Feat 3: **Group Action**
	*"Shoulder to shoulder"*
	Plans have a flow, a tidal current that can bolster the, efforts of all involved.
	`Members of your Plan gain a Boon which they can spend on Test made for its Duration.`

Feat 4: **Call Out**
	*"You're about to say..."*
	Few things are as troubling as being read.
	`When your Prediction is successful, the Target Risks Minor Panic. If they already were the Target of a successful Prediction, this is Moderate instead.`

Feat 5: **Double Time**
	*"Rally!"*
	Under your instruction, the collective machinery of the group operates at maximal efficiency.
	`When you Execute a Plan, you can Coin Flip to keep it, instead of Consuming the Plan.`

Feat 6: **Demoralize**
	*"Too easy"*
	Effective jibes and clever words are often as deadly as any sword in a fight.
	`When your Prediction is successful, you can Taunt them as a 1 AP Reaction. Taunting an Enemy provokes Minor Panic Risk from them, regardless of how many times you have already Predicted them. Each Taunt after the first increases the Severity of this Risk.`
