****
Expertise in the use of the Shield, a Discipline of Paladins and protectors. This is often viewed as an essential exercise for any swordsman, and a vital element of Training as a Nobleman or Paladin.

This Discipline carries a heroic history for Verdun, as ancient as the Shield itself.

**Passive Effects**
`When you Block with a Shield, it suffers less Durability loss, equal to your Bulwark.`
****
Feat 1: **Adaptability**
	*"Ever-ready"*
	Opportunity is the defender's currency, response demands swift judgement.
	`You have an extra AP each Round, exclusively used to Block.`

Feat 2: **Iron Stance**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Here I stand"*
	Plant your feet and hold the line. This posture is an iconic symbol of the lost age, resembling a nobler time.
	`For the Duration, Damage against you is less Vicious by half your Bulwark, Shields gains the same Bonus to their Durability, and you cannot be Moved against your will except by blows that would instantly Break your Shield.`

Feat 3: **The Line of Duty**
	*AP*: 1| *Qualities*: Reaction|
	*"Everyone stands together, lest we fall divided"*
	You burst towards the threat, Shield at the ready.
	`When an Ally within Meters equal to your Bulwark is Attacked, you Leap towards them and Block the Attack if you enter Engagement.`
	`This Action counts as a Block.`

Feat 4: **Retribution**
	*"The whole of the Law"*
	As you pass the steeper slope towards mastery, the strength you built affords you ample response.
	`When you Block an Attack, the Attacker Provokes one from you if they fail to Break your Shield.`

Feat 5: **Reflection**
	*"Its all in the angle"*
	When they fail to exhaust your resilience, you can turn their own strength to your favor.
	`You can Block Ranged Attacks, provided the projectile is mostly physical, with any Shield. If they fail to Break your Shield, you can reflect the Attack to another Target within Meters equal to your Bulwark.`

Feat 6: **Perfect Resistance**
	*"Halt"*
	Something greater than defense, an opposition clearer than water, bright as the sun.
	`When you Block an Attack which fails to Break your Shield, the Attacker loses all remaining AP.`
