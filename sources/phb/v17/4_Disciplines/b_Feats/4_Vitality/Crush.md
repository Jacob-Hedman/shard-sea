****
Brutal, slow, effective. The Discipline of Bludgeons. Crush is simplistic, its only function to destroy.

The Discipline is a favorite for Expeditioners especially interested in hunter larger, more dangerous Targets.

**Passive Effects**
`All Bludgeons you Wield gain or increase their Powerful Quality, up to half your Crush. Likewise, these Weapons suffer no Durability Loss unless you Block with them.`
****
Feat 1: **Skull Cracker**
	*AP*: ~| *Qualities*: Primary|
	*"At the brink"*
	You heave your Weapon in a relentless arc, one that throws away caution and embraces the weight of your swing.
	`Strike with a Bludgeon. The Target you hit it is Stunned for 1 Round and, if they would collide with something, the Damage is more Vicious for each Meter moved.`
	*Sway*
	`You are Off_Balance and Vulnerable for one Turn after using this Action.`
	*Critical effect*
	`You negate the Conditions applied to you by this Action.`

Feat 2: **Moving Mountain**
	*AP*: !0| *Qualities*: Stance|
	*"Harder"*
	Momentum is fuel.
	`For the Duration, you gain a Bonus to Attacks with Bludgeons, equal to the number of Meters moved in the previous Action. Further, When you Block with a Bludgeon, Durability loss is reduced by half your Crush.`

Feat 3: **Skyward**
	*"I'll be waiting"*
	Gravity is both friend and foe for you, something to be struggled with at the same time as it gives your blows their real force.
	`If a Target is repelled up by an Attack from you which also Injures them, they are knocked Prone.`

Feat 4: **Predatory Instinct**
	*"Better get ready"*
	Your training began with inflicting weakness, now you can punish it.
	`Attacks made against Targets suffering from more than one Condition are made with a Boon.`

Feat 5: **Impending Doom**
	*"It's here"*
	From the sky to the earth, the relentless downward curve traces the path from life to certain death.
	`When you spend at least 3 AP Striking with a Bludgeon, your Critical Success range is expanded by half your Crush.`

Feat 6: **Unstoppable Force**
	*"More"*
	The weight behind your blows reaches an impossible apex.
	`When you hit a Target with your Skull Cracker, they are repelled an additional number of Meters equal to your Crush. (This has no effect on the Powerful value.)`
