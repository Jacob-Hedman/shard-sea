****
The Discipline of Mages, used to develop their facility with Arcane Runes. 

Progression down this path is irreversible, a way towards a colder light, brighter perhaps, but far less kind.

**The Discipline of Arcane**
The study of Magic as a serious Discipline is the method of the Mage, one involving experiments both in the world and within the self.

****
Feat 2: **Wyrd Hunger**
	*"The greatest drug"*
	Magic is addictive, an illness from which no true Mage would ever be cured.
	`When you Generate Aether, you gain more, up to half your Training in Arcane. This can only be done once per Turn.`
	`Further, in lieu of Generating, you can consume one Plug to gain one Aether (as a 1 AP Interaction). 10 Plugs per Day is enough to mimic the effects of food and water for you, for consecutive Days equal to your Arcane.`

Feat 4: **Focus**
	*"The Needle's Eye"*
	Deeper study and greater concentration. First the narrow way, then the broad.
	`Pick a single Class, Spells which you Cast with Runes from that Class always count as 1 Tier higher and are 1 step Easier to Cast.`

Feat 6: **Less and More**
	*"Never back"*
	Advancement in Magecraft is a path which leads away from the rest of Verdun-kind. More Magical Artifact than living Creature, you practically live and breath Aether.
	`Whenever you Generate Aether while already holding more than your Reserve, you shed as much Strain as AP spent. Likewise, you automatically detect the presence of Magical effects within your Influence.`
#### Tier 1 Runes
************
Rune 1: **Void**
	*Class*: Aether
	*"Purity"*
	Absence, an emptiness that pulls and consumes. Void lies between, being in the negative.
	`You automatically Generate Aether up to half the Tier of the Spell (minimum 1) at the start of each Turn. However, you must roll a Chaos Die for each, suffering Persistant Strain for each 6.`

Rune 1: **Weight**
	*Class*: Space
	*"The chains of Gaia"*
	Primordial force, the descent. Weight governs the stability of a thing, its resistance to motion.
	`When Cast, something you Touch becomes heavier or lighter by 2T. Something with negative Weight falls up instead of down.`
	*Addition*
	`Multiple applications of Weight add, combing into a single Magical effect with a Tier equal to the greatest Weight Effect.`

Rune 1: **Velocity**
	*Class*: Time 
	*"Sooner, Later"*
	Time as length. Velocity is the partner of Distance, its Temporal counterpart.
	`You create a Burst within your Influence with a Radius up to T. Creatures within it are subject to one of the following effects, chosen when you Cast the Spell:`
	- *Haste*: `At the start of your Turn, roll a Chaos Die. On face 6, you gain an AP. At Tiers 3 and 6, you can roll additional Dice.` 
	- *Slow*: `You are Stunned for a third of the Spell's Tier (min 1).`

Rune 1: **Presence**
	*Class*: Time
	*"Nowhere else"*
	Instancy. Presence situates events in the now, the limit of the future.
	`A Creature within your Influence gains or loses Reflex, up to T. These effects stack.`
	*Too Much*
	`When a Creature's Reflex is greater than it would naturally be by more than their Tier, they are Stunned. If the difference exceeds six times their Tier, they are Incapacitated.`

Rune 1: **Density**
	*Class*: Change
	*"Neither hard nor soft"*
	Resolute. Density governs mutability, the resistance or ease of flexion.
	`The Durability or Reserve of a Target within your Influence increases or decreases by T.`
	*Asymmetry*
	`Altering this attribute can have unexpected effects; when the total change is greater than the Target's Tier, the following occurs, depending on kind of change:`
	- Increase: `Creatures lose half their Reflex, and Objects become twice as Heavy. Exceeding twice the Target's Tier doubles this effect, and so on.`
	- Decrease: `Creatures gain the ability to pass through obstructions as if they were half their Size, and gain 3 Reflex. Objects become half as Heavy. Exceeding twice the Target's Tier doubles this effect, and so on.`

Rune 1: **Distortion**
	*Class*: Change
	*"Twist"*
	Flux pure. Distortion is at the heart of all changes.
	`Spaces up to 5T fill with random distributions of materials from a source in your Influence. These are not properly real, and vanish when the Spell ends, but they do obstruct Movement and Detection as normal.`

Rune 1: **Force**
	*Class*: Power!
	*"Push or Pull"*
	The primitive of motion. Force underlies all things that move.
	`Target something in your Influence and choose one of the following effects.`
	- *Lift*: `You move the Target up to 3T in total, in any direction/s, so long as it does not leave your Influence.`
	- *Push*: `You move the Target up to 5T, only in a straight line, but the destination can be outside of your Influence. If the Target collides with something over the course of this, both suffer Blunt Crushing Damage. Cast at Tier_2 this is no longer Blunt, and each greater Tier makes the Damage more Vicious by one.`
	*Limits*
	`To Lift an Object, it must weigh no more than 10T+(Your Influence.) If your Spell could not Lift a Target, Pushing it only moves it half as far. If it weighs more than twice this limit, you cannot Push it at all.`

Rune 1: **Growth**
	*Class*: Life
	*"Green and grow, burn to nothing"*
	Vital. Growth, like all the Runes of Life, is a fleeting glimpse of the majesty of creation. Alone, it often harms as much as helps.
	`Spells from this Rune only effect living things you Touch and in the following ways, depending what they are:`
	- *Creatures*: `The Target may shed T Strain but, when the Spell ends, must roll as many Chaos Dice, suffering Persistant Strain for each 6 rolled.`
	- *Plants*: `It surges to life and takes a form you choose (which must still fit the loose normal range for the plant). Among other uses, these rapid movements can be used to form tendrils which allow you to Interact up to T Meters away using the plant. When the Spell ends, the plant instantly dies and crumbles to dust. Likewise, this robs the plant of any wholesome nutritional value it had.`

Rune 1: **Mimesis**
	*Class*: Life
	*"I, I"*
	Replication. Mimesis is the partial form of reproduction, capable only of echoes.
	`You adopt the exact shape and form of a living Creature within your Influence as long as its Tier is less than T. This changes everything but your Mind and Training. You are considered Channeling for the Duration of this Spell, and imperceptibly so to all but means of detecting Magic, but cannot Cast anything with a Tier greater than that of the Creature.`
#### Tier 3 Runes:
****
Rune 3: **Negation**
	*Class*: Aether
	*"Nihil fit"*
	Antithetical to Being. Negation erases and prevents, to Mages, this makes a superior defense.
	`Target an Active Spell or other Tiered Magical effect within your Influence. If its Tier is less than your Spell's, it ends if it is a Spell or other ambient Magical effect. Magical Items lose their supernatural properties for the duration.`
	*Counter-Spell*
	`You can also Cast this Spell as a Reaction to being Targeted by a Spell to attempt to dispel it before it effects you.`

Rune 3: **Eye**
	*Class*: Aether
	*"Opening itself"*
	A Primordial Rune. The Eye is the principal of reception, the ground of Sense entire.
	`You can percieve anything within your Influence by a supernatural Sense. However, anything you Detect or Target in this way can feel your presence as a prickly, unnatural sensation.`

Rune 3: **Distance**
	*Class*: Space
	*"Gone but I'm there"*
	Separation. Distance determines locality, the thisness of thereness.
	`Spaces up to T within your Influence have their Terrain Difficulty increased or lowered by up to 3T. Negative Difficulty causes a Creature to gain Movement when they pass through them (but they can only benefit from such a Space once per Round).`

Rune 3: **Touch**
	*Class*: Space
	*"Intimacy"*
	Contact. Touch mediates interactions of any kind in many ways.
	`Two Spaces no more than 2T Meters away become Co-incident. This means that Creatures occupying one, occupy both for the purposes of Engagement and Interaction.`

Rune 3: **Else**
	*Class*: Time
	*"Thence and yonder"*
	Connection across moments. Else is the weave of seconds into minutes.
	`A Target within your Influence gains a weak ability to perceive the recent past and near future This confers a Bonus of T to Preemption as well as the ability to know what happened in a Space they Detect within the last T Minutes.`

Rune 3: **Echo**
	*Class*: Time
	*"Wishes and Dreams"*
	Could have been. Echo is an esoteric principle and makes a dangerous Rune which even reckless Mages consider well before Incorporating.
	`An event within your Influence such as a single Action repeats, as long as it was no more than T Simple-Turns in the past. All associated rolls and random event are re-rolled, and the local timeline is reset back to that point. This rolls back Initiative and erases all consequences of that event and all following it. The backlash of this Spell is very strong, and makes you Stunned_3 you for T Full-Turns after Casting it.`
	*Emergence*
	`You can also Cast this Spell as a Reaction, but doing so doubles the Duration of the Stun.`

Rune 3: **Size**
	*Class*: Change
	*"One makes you larger"*
	Comparison. Size orders the world from small to great and back again.
	`Something within your Influence changes in Size, by up to T Meters in total. This change does not distort the form of a thing and applies evenly. This effects the Weight as well, by 10 Bulk per Meter.`
	*Square-Cube Law*
	`Increasing a Creature's Size by 1 Meter increases their Body by 1, but reduces their Reflex by the same amount. Decreasing their Size has the inverse effect.`

Rune 3: **Cohesion**
	*Class*: Change!
	*"Be not"*
	Bind and break. Cohesion governs the consistency of substances.
	`Touch an Object, if its Hardness is less than T plus your Influence, it is destroyed and reduced to dust. You can effect up to 10T Bulk or T Spaces worth of a substance at once with this Spell.`
	*Fusion*
	`Alternatively, you can fuse two things together, provided neither exceed the limits to Hardness.`

Rune 3: **Heat**
	*Class*: Power
	*"Primal motion"*
	Chaos in the air. Heat is invisible, but its power can send a thing into a blaze or see it bound in pallid chains.
	`Choose a Target in your Influence, in a Radius of up to 2T around it, Extremity from Heat or Cold is increased or decreased by T.`
	*Combination*
	`When multiple Heat effects move the Space in the same direction, Hotter or Colder, only the highest Tier effect holds. When multiple effects pull in opposite directions, they interfere. So, three Heat effects, 1 Hotter, 3 Hotter, and 3 Colder, would result in a net change of 0.`

Rune 3: **Gravitation**
	*Class*: Power
	*"The grand expanse"*
	Weight's sire. Gravitation is what gives a thing a ground, and what moves the world.
	`You Touch a Target whose Weight is no greater than 10T, and choose a Point (an Object, Creature, or Space) within your Influence. The Target instantly falls towards that Point as if that were its down-direction. If the Target collides with anything on its way 'down', it both suffers and inflicts the Falling Damage to whatever it hits. Then, it either stops or the obstruction is broken/gives way, and the Target resumes its fall.`
	*Blunt Force*
	`When the Target hits something heavier than it, it inflicts Blunted Falling Damage, standard otherwise. Conversely, it inflicts Traumatic Damage if it is heavier by twice or more.`
	*Suspension*
	`Once the Target reaches the Point you choose, and the Spell persists, it becomes Linked to that Point.`

Rune 3: **Death**
	*Class*: Life!
	*"Agony"*
	Expiration. Death is the terminal phase of life, without a generative element, it produces only pain.
	`Anything living that you Touch suffers Chaos Damage, Blunted if Cast less than their Tier, more Vicious for each in excess of their Tier. The Undead shed T Strain instead. If you are not Undead, you suffer 1 Corruption.`

Rune 3: **Entropy**
	*Class*: Life!
	*"A slow fall"*
	Decay. Something inextricable from life, Entropy also directs it towards the end.
	`A living Creature within your Influence instantly Risks Minor Panic from pain. If their Tier is less than this Spell's by 1, they Risk Moderate Panic instead, Major for more.`
	*Rot*
	`If Cast Targeting a living non-Creature like a plant, it dies instantly, withering and decaying at an amazing rate. The latter also holds for corpses and other inert dead, which decay to nothing almost instantly.`
#### Tier 6 Runes
****
Rune 6: **Control**
	*Class*: Aether
	*"Regal"*
	Regulation. Control is what gives form to Magic, only surpassed by Manifestation in grandeur.
	`Target an active Spell or Tiered Magical Effect within your Influence. If this Spell's Tier exceeds that of the Target, you can exempt any currently effected Targets, and re-orient the effect to Target something else, provided that the new Target is valid for the effect.`

Rune 6: **Manifestation**
	*Class*: Aether
	*"With many Crowns"*
	The Crown Rune of Aether. Manifestation is the Crown of Crowns, in the sense that it can regulate and govern every other Class.
	`You instantly replicate the effects of a Ritual you know, provided the Tiers of the component Runes do not sum to a number greater than the Tier of this Spell and you provide any necessary materials and Reagents.`
	*Decree*
	`Alternatively, you can forbid all Spells of an Class from your Influence, automatically dispelling all unless their Tier exceeds this Spell's. This also Targets Rituals that use Runes from the pertinant Class.`

Rune 6: **Acceleration**
	*Class*: Space
	*"Hylomorphic"*
	Composite. Acceleration regulates the transformation of potential to actual.
	`As a Reaction, you can halt any Action as long as its AP cost does not exceed T/3. You can also halt a moving projectile, a falling Creature, or other moving object within your Influence, provided its Weight does not exceed 10T. Halting something in the air keeps it levitating there for the Duration. Levitating Creatures are Restrained until they touch the ground or the Spell ends.`
	*Inflammation*
	`You can also accelerate any of the above, granting a Bonus of 2T to a Creature's effective Movement, a Bonus to the same amount to the Damage of an Attack or projectile, or cause a Fall to resolve instantly, increasing the effective distance of the fall by T Meters for calculating any kind of fall Damage.`

Rune 6: **Locality**
	*Class*: Space
	*"Everywhere is here"*
	The Crown Rune of Space. Locality governs all other elements of motion and reveals the underlying connection of everything.
	`Choose up to T Spaces within your Influence. All of them become Co-incident, further, Creatures in one of these Spaces may exit from any other, as if teleporting.`

Rune 6: **Possibility**
	*Class*: Time!
	*"Nowhere is everywhere"*
	What could be. Possibility governs the distinction between what can be done and what cannot.
	`Choose a Spell with a Tier up to half the Tier of this Spell. Under a specified condition of your choice when Casting this Spell, the chosen Spell is Cast automatically. The Possibility Rune cannot refer to itself. Further, the conditional Spell you create is a Magical Effect with its own Tier, Linked to you, which can be Detected and Negated.`

Rune 6: **Relativity**
	*Class*: Time!
	*"All is now"*
	The Crown Rune of Time. Relativity is the limit at which Time and Space meet and almost seem to blur.
	`You are able to dynamically warp time within your Influence. Doing so allows you to take up to T AP from one or more Creatures and give it to another, provided their Tiers are less than this Spell's and you do no transfer more than T AP. AP given in this way can allow the beneficiary to exceed the normal limit of 3 to a Round.`

Rune 6: **Substance**
	*Class*: Change!
	*"Change!"*
	The Crown Rune of Change. Substance is an extremely abstract but equally vital element of Being.
	`You transmute up to T Bulk of something you Touch into a different compound. All such changes obey the following laws:`
	- *Purity*: `The resultant compound is always less pure than the original. Metal can never become a gem, silver cannot become gold, but any metal can become dirt, and a gem can become gold. This almost directly means that the transmutation depreciates the Value of the product.`
	- *Phase*: `The product must be of the same "state" as the ingredient under current conditions. Stone cannot become water, but ice can become some kinds of stone. Relative changes in Hardness should not exceed 2T.`
	- *Potential*: `The product is never more "reactive" than the ingredient. Black powder could be made into sand, and acid could become saltwater, but not the inverse. Likewise, the Substance Rune alone cannot make something edible or from something poisonous and the nutritive principle of the product is always less than the ingredient. In the acid example, brackish saltwater can be produced, but not freshwater. Conversely, milk could become water, but not vise versa.`

Rune 6: **Collision**
	*Class*: Change
	*"What touches"*
	The governing principle of interaction, Collision mediates almost everything.
	`Up to 1+T Objects or Creatures within your Influence become unable to interact with eachother at all, one phasing through one the other as if were not there. Expanses of a substance T Meters or smaller count as an Object for this purpose.`
	*Co-instance*
	`When two or more things would overlap at the end of this Spell, they are displaced to the nearest, most normal configuration of all involved. For example, a Creature underground is shunted above it, and so on.`

Rune 6: **Lightning**
	*Class*: Power!
	*"Voltaic energy, pure and holy"*
	Ruin, the penultimate expression of the Class of Power.
	`A Target within your Influence is struck by an arc of power, inflicting Voltaic Damage. When it hits, roll T Chaos Dice, if any comes up 6, the nearest Creature to the Target is struck, and you roll one less Chaos Die. This procedure repeats until it fails to produce a 6, or no Dice remain. The same Target cannot be struck twice by the same Spell.`

Rune 6: **Sun**
	*Class*: Power
	*"The first flame of the Glory, eternal fire"*
	Destruction. The Sun is the crown Rune of the Class of Power, and the most primeval of forces. 
	`At a Space in your Influence, a blisteringly bright light appears, accompanied by deadly furnace blasts of fire. Everything within a Radius of it up to 10+3T Meters is Blind and subject to 3+T Extreme Heat and those Engaged directly 6+T Extreme Heat (Incendiary Damage is suffered at 6, which is more Vicious for each greater step).`
	*Drain*
	`These Spells are extremely demanding, and need a constant source of energy. You must supply T Aether each Round, and are Stunned while maintaining a Sun.`

Rune 6: **Antilife**
	*Class*: Life
	*"Undeath is a form of life, one that feeds on the essence of death"*
	A Rune both crucial and perverse, the exact inverse of life itself.
	`You create a surge of necromantic energy that has one of the following effects of your choice:`
	- `An Undead Creature within your Influence fall under your verbal control if their Tier is less than this Spell's.`
	- `A Creature whose Tier is less than T and has died within 1 Minute returns to life as a Mindless Undead.`
	`Unless you are Undead, using this Spell also inflicts T/2 Corruption on you.`

Rune 6: **Regeneration**
	*Class*: Life!
	*"Heat and breath"*
	The crown Rune of Life. Regeneration is the highest expression of the Vital principle, even alone it has the power to truly mend.
	`Touch a Creature, they shed Injuries depending on the amount of Aether spent (in Casting or afterwards with persistent Touch). You may continue to spend Aether for T Rounds thereafter for this purpose.`
	- *Minor*: 6| *Moderate*: 10| *Major*: 30|
	`So, a T6 Regeneration Spell could instantly heal a Minor Injury, and would provide a subsequent 6 Rounds after Casting to supply more Aether for a greater, or additional Injuries. E.G., a Creature with a Minor and Moderate Injury could be fully healed with one T6 Spell followed by 10 more Aether spent.`
	*Consumption*
	`This Spell is extremely taxing, and you suffer Persistent Strain whenever you Cast it.`
