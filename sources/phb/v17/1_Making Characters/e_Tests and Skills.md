#### Rolling Dice
****
Kriegsmesser uses six sided Dice for practically everything. Some of these are special, in the sense that they have unique behaviors when rolled. However, these behaviors are procedural, not actual properties of the Dice. Hence, any six sided Die will do. The special varieties of Dice are: 
- **Chaos Dice**: which are six sided Dice that have unique contextual effects when rolling a six. Rolling a 6 is almost always bad.
- **Pools**: which are arbitrarily sized collections of Dice, rolled together. Pools have effects depending on how many Dice roll a certain face. For example, Pools of Chaos Dice usually have effects depending on how many roll a 6.
#### Skill Tests
****
These are used to adjudicate a task or course of action that you declare in-character that have a plausible chance of success or failure. Some mechanically codified tasks, like Actions, might call for a Test of some kind in their description, or the GM can require one of you. To make a Skill Test, roll 2d6 and add both your Tier and Ability Bonus.
*Unskilled Tests*
	You can still make a Test without a given Skill, but you can only add your Ability Bonus.

**Evaluation**
compare the result to a Difficulty or 'DC', a number given by the GM. If your roll is equal to or higher than the DC, you succeed.

**Adjusting the Difficulty of a Test**
Difficulty has a default value of 8, but is adjusted very frequently. When a Test is adjusted, the Difficulty will increase or decrease by an amount implied by the circumstance. 

A good guide to the "feel" of Difficulty as well as a rough statistical estimate can be derived from your Tier as follows:
****
**Minor**/*80%*: T+6| **Moderate**/*60%*: T+8| **Major**/*30%*: T+10| **Extreme**/*10%*: T+12|
****
- *If you lack the Skill, default T to 0 for this chart.*
- *Adding a Boon or Bane moves these odds by about 10% with diminishing returns for each additional Die.*

**Resistance**
When you make a Test against a Creature, they Resist, raising the DC by its Tier. So, making an Attack against a Creature of Tier 1 has a DC of 9 (8+1). Likewise, when making Tests against a Creature, and the Test is against a Skill, the Difficulty is raised by their Tier again, twice the increase. 
*Tests Against Skilled Targets*
	- Tier 1| *DC*: 10
	- Tier 2| *DC*: 12
	- Tier 3| *DC*: 14
*Tests Against Unskilled Targets*
	- Tier 1| *DC*: 9
	- Tier 2| *DC*: 10
	- Tier 3| *DC*: 11
*Unaware Targets*
	In the exception of the above, Creatures that cannot Detect you (against whom you have Full Cover) and fail to Target (in the case of Partial Cover) you cannot Resist. Against such a Target, all Tests have the default DC of 8. ([[d_Senses, Targeting, Cover, Spaces, and Environmental Hazards]])

**Critical Success and Failure**
When you make a Skill Test, there is a remote possibility that your result is critical.
*Critical Success*
	- `If you roll a natural 12 and pass the Test DC.`
	Critical successes are the "best case" outcome of a Test. Most Actions will have explicit consequences for a Critical Success, but if there no pre-written benefit, the GM should confer some additional benefit.
*Critical Failure*
	- `Following any roll of a natural 2, even if you would have otherwise succeeded.`
	Critically failing a Test is the inverse, you fail and drastically so. Critical failures provoke Moderate Panic Risk in addition to the negative outcome of the Test.

**Boons and Banes**
These are d6 that can be rolled along with any other d6, which curve the rolled result a bit in either direction, up for Boons, down for Banes. Rolling 2d6 with a Boon means you would roll 3d6 and take the highest two, or the lowest 2 for a Bane.

Banes and Boons subtract from one another, so a roll with both a Bane and a Boon is a roll with neither.

**Ability Bonuses**
At certain thresholds of Ability, you gain an Ability Bonus to Tests made with corresponding Skills. In general, you obtain an Ability Bonus to a Skill equal to 1/6th (or one for every six) of the Ability, rounded down (meaning that at 5 or less, the Bonus would be 0.)
*Ability Bonus Thresholds*
	6: `+1`| 12: `+2`| 18: `+3`|...
- *Body Skills*: Vitality, Traversal, Unity.
- *Mind Skills*: Immanence, Resolve, Creation, Luminance.
- *Reflex Skills*: Action,  Potence, Darkness.
*Ability Bonuses and Specialized Tests*
	In general, Ability Bonuses can be applied to a Specialized Test, provided the Skill and Discipline correspond match the Ability.
*Dynamic Bonuses*
	Strain can reduce your Abilities, and when they fall below one of the Thresholds, you lose the corresponding Bonus. For example, if your Mind was 12, your Ability Bonus would normally be +2. Yet, if you suffered Strain, it would reduce by 1 (because your Mind would fall below 12). If your Mind fell below 6, you would lose the Bonus entirely until you shed enough Strain.

**Skill Etiquette**
In general, it is a faux pas to simply declare a Skill Test, or to argue with the GM about which Skills are requested of you for Tests. Of course, you are the one who decides the Actions, Feats, and activities of your PC. Nonetheless, engaging in so-called "mother-may-I" behavior, wherein you negotiate with the GM at length, is usually a bad idea. This slows the game for other players and, unless handled with tact and maturity, can depreciate the experience for most others. Further, it doesn't even make sense to do this, as AMPs, DIMs, and Difficulty require input from the GM, but radically change the outcome of a roll. 

There is no rule, per-se, against doing this, and your judgement can help everyone learn, including your GM. Simply keep this as a rule of thumb: Skills are asked for by the GM when you take an Action, Feat, or do something in Character. Declaring "I roll Luminance!" is the wrong approach to finding traps.
#### Coin-Flip
****
Sometimes, a Skill Test is too small-scale or too predictable for truly random chance. In such cases, employ a Coin-Flip. The GM will briefly describe the two possible outcomes and ask Players to pick their preferred face, "calling" the Flip. The GM will then flip the coin and adjudicate results accordingly.AMPs and DIMs, should they apply to a Coin-Flip, are extra flips of the coin. A Flip with a DIM takes the worse result, while a Flip with aAMP does the opposite. 
### Skills in Specific
****
With all of the above in mind, we can discuss the 10 Skills. 
- **Amount**: Each Character has 5 Skills, and each is scaled by their Tier.
- **Principals and Limits**: The Skills of Action, Potency, Vitality, and Resolve are your Principal Skills. These are used to make Attacks and constitute your defenses against the Actions of others. *You cannot have more than 2 Principal Skills.*
- **Implications**: Skills can also have a psychological dimension, in addition to the practical. A Character with Darkness and Action would be quite a bit different from one with Immanence, Potency, and Vitality. This can give you a clue on how to roleplay them, should you need some guidance. 
****
**Immanence**
This Skill represents the continual pursuit of higher things. You can use Immanence to intuit abstractions, comprehend complex problems, and construct logical connections.
****
**Action**
This Skill represents the practiced, instant exercise of force. You can use Action to impose your strength, perform athletic feats, make snap judgements, and resolve problems under pressure. 
- *Action is a Principal Skill used to make and defend against Melee Attacks.* 
****
**Potence**
This Skill represents trained coordination through time. You can use Potence to balance yourself, to make and execute plans, construct inferences, hypothesize, and intuit danger. 
- *Potence is a Principal Skill used to make and defend against Ranged Attacks.* 
****
**Vitality**
This Skill represents cultivated physical robustness and health. You can use Vitality to act out raw strength and speed, assess and aid those who suffer harm, express the force of your passion, and endure physical trials.
- *Vitality is a Principal Skill used to make and defend against Melee Attacks.*
****
**Resolve**
This Skill represents honed intention and force of will. You can use Resolve to concentrate through immense physical tasks, impress with your presence and strong affect, force intellectual problems through sheer deliberation, and pry answers from even the most resistant situations and persons. 
- *Resolve is a Principal Skill used to make and defend against Ranged Attacks.* 
****
**Creation**
This Skill represents the channeled ability to create. You can use Creation to design complex devices, construct from raw materials, make art and artifice, employ alchemy, assess the creations of others and design armaments. Creation is also the basis of repairing and restoring things. You can use it to fix Ruined gear and perform Surgery.
****
**Traversal**
This Skill represents the trained faculty for navigation. You can use Traversal to negotiate precarious terrain, force your way through obstacles, decipher networks of relationship and trade, assess localities, and circumvent confusion. 
****
**Darkness**
This Skill represents the methodologies which distort and conceal. You can use Darkness to lay snares and play tricks, deceive, forge documents, break locks, assess marks, and provoke fear.
****
**Luminance**
This Skill represents the hunt for the truth. You can use Luminance to physically examine, sift out a liar, find holes in forgeries and illusions, sense dangers and hidden things, and wring the truth from a stone.
****
**Unity**
This Skill represents the cultivation of leadership and authority. You can use Unity to give a rallying shout, spur a charge, manage an organization, detect unrest, and decry chaos.
****
