## The Three Abilities 
****
The three dimensions of a Character in terms of Ability measure their basic capacities, their fundamental strengths without Skill.

**Thresholds of Ability**
Once your Abilities reach a multiple of six, 6, 12, and 18, you obtain certain benefits. Firstly, an Ability Bonus to Skills related to that Ability and, second, a Threshold Feat corresponding to the value of your Base Ability.
*Thresholds are Permanent and Intrinsic*
	When you gain a Threshold Feat, you don't lose it, though the effects of Strain may reduce its magnitude. Conversely, you only gain a Threshold Feat when your Base Ability reaches that value. Hence, only Ability Advancements from EXP or other, extraneous effects that specifically hit your Base can grant a Threshold Feat.
*Conditional Function*
	Whenever a Threshold Feat refers to your Ability Bonus, it refers to your current Bonus, which can diminish as you suffer ever more Strain. While you never lose these Feats, their benefits might be negated by this change. For example, if a Feat added your Ability Bonus to a Test while under enough Strain to reduce your Bonus to 0, the Feat would also add 0.
#### Body
****
Strength, power, stability. Body reflects your physical development in terms of active capacity to effect the world and resist Damage.

**Body Thresholds**
	6: `+1`| 12: `+2`| 18: `+3`|...
- *Body Skills*: Vitality, Traversal, Unity.

Threshold 6: **Power Attack**| Ability Bonus: `Begins at +1`
	Your strength allows you to amplify the destructive power of your Strikes.
	`When you make a Melee Attack to Strike with a Weapon (including Improvized but not Unarmed), you can suffer a penalty up to your Body Bonus to make the Damage more Vicious by an equal amount.`

Threshold 12: **Greater Resistance**| Ability Bonus: `Increases to +2`
	While unable to prevent harm, the refinement of Body can resist it.
	`You may add your Body Bonus to your Injury Risk. If this rolls outside the range of the Table (13 or more) you avoid all effects.`

Threshold 18: **Indomitable**| Ability Bonus: `Increases to +3`
	At the apex of physical achievement, only the greatest of peril can wear you down.
	`You gain Resistence to all Physical Damage, up to your Body Bonus.` 
### Mind
****
Apprehension, intuition, will. Mind reflects the force of your mental acuity. Not necessarily a matter of traditional "smarts", Mind is the power to overcome problems and see connections in the world around you.

**Mind Thresholds**
	6: `+1`| 12: `+2`| 18: `+3`|...
- *Mind Skills*: Immanence, Resolve, Creation, Luminance.

Threshold 6: **Wits**| Ability Bonus: `Begins at +1`
	Knowing yourself, you might heighten the magnitude of your victories.
	`The range of natural roll you need to Critically Succeed is increased by your Ability Bonus; this allows you to Critically Succeed on a natural 11, 10, and so on as your Bonus grows.`

Threshold 12: **Cunning**| Ability Bonus: `Increases to +2`
	Knowing the world, even certain failures can be reversed.
	`When you fail a Skill Test but not by more than your Ability Bonus, you can suffer 1 Strain and succeed instead. This can also be applied to Preemption rolls, as if they were Skill Tests.`

Threshold 18: **Prediction**| Ability Bonus: `Increases to +3`
	Knowing the Other, each exchange is riddle; its solution gives way to your uncontested superiority.
	`When you Preempt a Creature, you can Critically Succeed if your roll exceeds their Initiative and the natural roll is a 12(adjusted by your Wits). Critical Successes confer the following benefits:`
	- `If you Preempted the Target just after they declare an Action, you gain AP equal to the amount they intended to spend.` 
	- `The Target becomes more Vulnerable to you, equal to your Ability Bonus.`

#### Reflex
****
Speed, dynamism, reactivity. Reflex reflects your inner potential for motion, both to propel yourself and in swift response to sudden stimulus.

**Reflex Thresholds**
	6: `+1`| 12: `+2`| 18: `+3`|...
- *Reflex Skills*: Action, Potence, Darkness.

Threshold 6: **Unnatural Agility**| Ability Bonus: `Begins at +1`
	You are almost never off your guard, replete with Reflex, each step lighter than the last.
	`Each Turn, you gain Agility up to your Ability Bonus. Agility can be spent like AP, but only for Reactions. You also gain half as much when rolling Initiative, rounded down.`

Threshold 12: **Dodge**| Ability Bonus: `Increases to +2`
	The wind shifts, and you as well.
	`When you are the Target of an Attack, you can spend AP as a Reaction, up to your Ability Bonus, to inflict that many Banes on the Test.`

Threshold 18: **The Apex of Motion**| Ability Bonus: `Increases to +3`
	Few, if any, match your pace anymore.
	`You can spend two Agility to gain one AP at any time.`
