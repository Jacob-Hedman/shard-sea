#### Armor
****
Armor is a Worn Item, meaning that it Equipping grants a constant passive benefit. Likewise, Armor allows you to negate damage of a specific Rating in exchange for the Armor taking Notches. 

**Armor and Encumbrance**
Usually, Weight and Item Bulk are one and the same thing. Armor is an exceptional case, because Armor Bulk measures both its Weight and the ergonomic penalty of wearing a more or less hardened set of equipment.

Hence, Armor Bulk always counts against your Encumbrance, regardless of whether the Item would weigh nothing separately.

**Armor and Weight**
Being heavier can be a boon. For example, your Weight can defend against certain Spells (being too much mass to move) or Attacks, like those from Cleavers or Axes. 

Your Armor Bulk is added to your Weight.

**Unique Materials**
The Resources in the sea provide a great number of ways to increase the effect of your Armor.
*Pricing*
	When adding a Unique Material to Armor, the Item is more expensive. To calculate this, use the following formula:
	Price + Bulk × (Material Value Multiplier)
*Known Unique Materials*
- *Caeline*| *Multipier*: 8,000T| `The Armor is Holy, more Durable by T, and confers Resistance:3_Chaos/Incendiary.`
- *Akhlysite*| *Multipier*: 8,000T| `The Armor is Unholy and confers Resistance:3_Energy/6_Chaos, Blunted Chaos Damage sheds one Persistent Strain instead of Notching your Armor.`
- *Adamant*| *Multipier*: 4,000T| `The Armor is twice as Durable.`
- *Stygia*| *Multipier*: 3,000T| `The Armor grants Resistance:1_All.`
- *Ruark*| *Multipier*: 2,000T| `The Armor's Bulk value is reduced by half (even when Equipped), and has one less Movement Penalty, but is less Durable by 1.`

**Cloth Armor**
	*Tier*: 1| *Cost*: 1,000
	Well fashioned clothing, suited for most kinds of wear and able to provide slight protection.
	*Bulk*: (2 times Your Height)| *Resources*: Fiber
	*Durability*: 3| *Movement Penalty*: 0
	*Rating*: `Physical, Incendiary.`

**Leather Armor**
	*Tier*: 1| *Cost*: 6,000

	*Bulk*: (5 Times your Height)| *Resources*: Fiber
	*Durability*: 6| *Movement Penalty*: 1
	*Rating*: `Physical, Incendiary.`

**Chain and Gambeson**
	*Tier*: 2| *Cost*: 10,000

	*Rating*: `Physical, Incendiary`

**Dragon Armor**
	*Tier*: 3| *Cost*: 100,000

#### Shields
****
Held in one Hand, Shields can be used to Block.

**Buckler**
	*Tier*: 1| *Cost*: 600
	A small dome of hardened steel fit to the hand, used to deflect more than really obstruct an Attack. Its size allows it to be tempered to a degree unfit for larger Shields, but its size makes it equally unfit for some tasks.
	`The Hand used to Equip a Buckler is not considered full.`
	*Bulk*: 2| *Durability*: 3| *Resources*: Craft_Metal

**Heater**
	*Tier*: 2| *Cost*: 1,000
	Constructed to affix to cover the side and deflect most blows, Heaters are popular for their middle approach, neither too large nor small. 
	*Bulk*: 6| *Durability*: 6| *Resources*: Craft_Metal

**Dragon Shield**
	*Tier*: 3| *Cost*: 20,000
	Like a wall, the Dragon Shield is a bulwark against nearly anything, but obscenely cumbersome. Its more popular use is to set up emplacements for archers, but some combatants choose to carry them.
	`Everything in the Shield's Engagement benefits from T Cover, including the wearer. When this Cover causes something to miss, it always Damages the Shield.`
	*Bulk*: 10| *Durability*: 10| *Resources*: Craft_Metal/Timber
	*Massive*
	`Unless the wearer has at least 10 Body, the Dragon Shield must be used in 2 Hands, altough it can be Placed in a Space as a 1 AP Interaction.`
