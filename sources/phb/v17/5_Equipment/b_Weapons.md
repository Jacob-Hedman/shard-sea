Weapons are tools, implements, defenses, and even personal expressions. In the Shard Sea, Weapons are collected under either Melee or Ranged, indicating what Attacks may be executed with them. Likewise, Weapons come in Groups, categories that reflect their form. 

Each Weapon Group has an Effect that modifies how you can use them as compared to others.

**Damage**
Damage from an Attack depends on its Traits (or those of the Weapon used to make it). The Base Damage is set by a Type, Cutting Damage has a Base of 1 for example. Further, the Vicious Trait increases this amount. So, an Attack with a Vicious_2 Cutting Weapon deals 3 Damage.

#### Weaponry Traits
****

- **Range_x**: Weapons with this Trait are used for Ranged Attacks with the Skills of Potence or Resolve and can threaten Targets up to 2x Meters. 
	- *Attacks against Targets more than x Meters from you are made with an Bane and harder by 1 for every 2 Meters beyond x.*
- **Melee**: Weapons with this Trait are used to make Melee Attacks with the Skills of Action or Vitality.
- **x_Accuracy**: Positive or negative, x is added to all Attacks made with the Weapon.
- **Damage**: this quantifies the types of Damage you can deal with the Weapon (choose one when Attacking.)
- **Reach_x**: Melee Weapons with this Trait have a Range beyond Engagement, up to x Meters.
- **Vicious_x**: This increases (or decreases, if negative) the Damage dealt by a Weapon.
- **Blunt**: Creatures roll a natural 12 on Injury Risk provoked by these Weapons, and their Damage is reduced to one at most.
- **Powerful_x**: Weapons with this Trait can displace the location of their target. On a successful attack against a Creature with a weapon that has this Trait you can displace it x Meters in any direction.
- **x_Handed**: you need to use x hands to Equip these Weapons. 
- **Reload_x**: After making an Attack with these Weapons, they must be Reloaded as an Interaction for x AP. 
- **Bomb_x**: Weapons with this Trait Explode when they hit and deal their Damage in a Radius, up to x Meters.
	- **Delayed_x**: Bombs with this Trait deal Damage after hitting, within x Turns. (Delayed Bombs can be displaced during this Duration to change where they detonate.)
- **Finesse**: When used to make an Opportunity Attack, you gain a Boon.
- **Light**: You can Throw these Weapons twice as far.
## Melee Weapons
****
The principle focus of the Melee Attack and Strike. There are a wide array of options for this purpose and one should choose whichever fits their approach and Training. Pre-Conquest Verdun tended to view Melee as a testing ground for Honor, and much of their Weapon design and beliefs still reflect this. However, the Shard Sea is often where Honor is the first virtue to be sacrificed. 

**Unique Materials**
Expedition-ready arms are usually made from Steel. However, special substances can change the properties of a Weapon in a number of ways; namely, adjusting both Cost and Damage.
*Pricing*
	When adding a Unique Material to a Weapon, the Item is more expensive. To calculate this, use the following formula:
	Price + Bulk × (Material Value Multiplier)

## Ranged Weapons
****
Distance and reach are essential on the battlefield. Slaying or incapacitating an opponent before they can reach you is a sure path to victory. For this reason, and because offense at a distance is the more skillful way, Verdun heavily favored the Bow. On foot and especially mounted, Verdun bowmen decimated the Orks and other foes in the Great Conquest. 

After the Doom, this priority had to re-examined. Many found that the dynamic environment of the Shard Sea made trained archers a steep cost, perhaps impractically so. Still, many hold strong to the noble tradition, and others have found more modern ways to kill from afar. 
#### Bows
****
The Bow is the prince of Weapons, able to deliver death at long distances with almost no delay. Though, it requires strength, precision, and dedicated training to use well.

The Verdun prized their Bows for as long as history can report. The difficulty in using them leant the Weapon a noble affect, and the fact that the Orks seemed to disdain them was honor enough for skilled Bowmen. In the Shard Sea, they are no less effective, but the skill needed can often be seen as a tax by Expeditioners.

Item: **A Quiver of Arrows/Bolts**
	*Cost*: 600

*Special Ammunition is also Consumed when used and generally cannot be reclaimed.*
