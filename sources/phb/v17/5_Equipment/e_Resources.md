#### Resources
****
Everything is made of something. Resources quantify this, in a use-oriented fashion. There are six general Types of Resource:
- **Rare Metals**: Gold, Silver, Titanium... etc.
- **Craft Metals**: Iron, Copper, Bronze... etc.
- **Fiber**: Leather, Cotton, Paper... etc.
- **Minerals**: Salt, Lime, Quartz... etc.
- **Timber**: Pine, Oak, Ebony... etc.
- **Stone**: Granite, Limestone, Shale... etc.

**Resource Traits**
Most things, especially Items, interact with Resources, being made of them. When anything has a Resource Type as a Trait, this implies that it contains that Resource. E.g., a Weapon with the Craft Metal Trait requires such to be Crafted, while a Terrain Hex with the Timber Trait can be harvested for Timber.

**Bulk Value**
Resources have a Value per unit of Bulk. As Bulk is an abstraction of equal parts literal mass and size, this is uniform across the entire Type of normal Resources, as follows:
- **Rare Metals**: 600
- **Craft Metals**: 100
- **Fiber**: 60
- **Minerals**: 30
- **Timber**: 10
- **Stone**: 3

**Unique Resources**
While Resources are usually general, some highly specific substances require their own Traits. The best known of these within the Shard Sea are:
- **Residue**: Crystalline Aether, which comes in several Types of its own.
	- *Value*: Varies by Type.
- **Caeline**: Heavenly gold, found only in the bodies and works of Celestials. This substance is intrinsically Holy.
	- *Value*: 1,600
- **Akhlysite**: Infernal Bronze, forged by Demons and deadly poisonous to living Creatures. This substance is intrinsically Unholy.
	- *Value*: 1,600
- **Adamant**: Silvery white, Adamant is extremely hard and confers that property to nearly anything made from it.
	- *Value*: 800
- **Stygia**: Black, cold to the touch, and very similar to Iron. Weaponry forged from Stygia is often more lethal.
	- *Value*: 600
- **Ruark**: Flexible, literally weightless, almost translucent and nearly as hard as steel. Ruark is found in the Sea and is extremely useful for building for both its elasticity and lack of weight. 
	- *Value*: 400
