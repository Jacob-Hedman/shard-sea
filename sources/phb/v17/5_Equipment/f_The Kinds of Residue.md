Residue is not a homogenous substance, and can present in a number of different forms.

#### Artificial Residues
****
These are the creation of Mages, Artifice, or other interventions, usually for a purpose.

**Primed Residue**
A unique and highly active form created by secretive means for Mages. Primed Residue is often black or purple and glows faintly. When consumed, it can swell a Mage's internal Reserve. Otherwise, this is a fairly dangerous substance. Non-Mages in physical contact with it suffer 1 Corruption for each Minute of exposure.

**Pure Residue**
The common, wholesome, pale blue form recognizable in Plugs, Loops, and Spheres. This is simply Residue purged of impurities.

**Infused Residue**
Violet-red and usually less vibrant, Infused Residue is created in Rituals that imbue it with some kind of Magical effect. This substance is valued for the effects it can cause, but little else, as the Ritual usually renders Infused Residue useless for anything else.

#### Natural Residues
****
The states of the substance one might encounter beyond the Ark, occuring with no intentional adulteration or influence.

**Corrupt Residue**
	*Value*: Half that of normal Residue.
Blue-violet crystals that surge with a dark, internal energy. Corrupt Residue is a contaminated form saturated with Abyssal energy released in the Doom. 
`Creatures which are not Unholy suffer Minor Panic Risk from pain caused when touching this form of Residue.`

**Elemental Residue**
A wider category of Residues that exhibit unusual properties aligned with a distinct Magical Aspect. Mage-Scholars theorize that these come about in regions with concentrations of natural substances that align with the Aspect of the Residue.
	*Aether*| *Value*: `The same as Pure Residue.`
	A paler blue than normal and shaped like thin, bright spikes. Aetheric Residue forms near high concentrations of Magic.
	`Aetheric Residue held to the skin suffuses the region with light if the individual bears the Gift.`
	*Space*| *Value*: `Twice normal.`
	Black with no inherent glow at all, Spatial Residue is usually found in clouds of dust blown by the winds of the Shard Sea. This is often used by Artificers to assess Relics and Artifacts, or to warn of nearby Unholy foes.
	`Spatial Residue reacts to Magic and other supernatural phenomena nearby, glowing with a unique pattern and hue depending on the kind, Gold for Holy, Red for Unholy, Blue for Arcane. The intensity of this glow indicates the strength of the presence.`
	*Time*| *Value*: `Ten times normal.`
	Colorless and seemingly weightless crystals, Temporal Residue forms near ancient sites and has a strange relationship with memories and thought. Its most common use is as a kind of recording device.
	`When touching a Plug-worth of Temporal Residue, you can store a memory within or experience one already there. When found in the wild, it often contains captured, fractured memories from ancient times.`
	*Change*| *Value*: `100 times normal.`
	A white glowing liquid, Transient Residue seems to collect in or around regions of extreme Magical activity. This substance is intensely caustic, but has intrinsic properties that more than pay back the risk of collecting it.
	`Transient Residue functions as a perfect acid, able to dissolve anything and convert it into yet more Transient Residue, except for other Residue. Creatures that touch it do not suffer Damage, per say. Rather, the part that it touches is instantly destroyed.`
	`After dissolving something, Transient Residue soon excretes an amount of another Elemental Residue corresponding in some way to its substance, with a Bulk ratio of 10/1. For example, a Creature's arm would likely net a Plug or so of Organic Residue.`
	*Power*| *Value*: `Ten times normal.`
	Red-hued and glowing, Primal Residue forms near regions of extreme heat and exudes constant powerful warmth, too hot to touch for any but a Forge Priest.
	`Primal Residue emits heat and light, and deals Incendiary Damage to those that touch it unprotected (Blunted if less than a Sphere). When used to fuel Artifice, Primal Residue is ten times as potent as Pure Residue and, for effects that deal Damage, it is more Vicious by one.`
	*Life*| *Value*: `Twice normal.`
	Green and bright, growing in lichen-like formations near regions of especially vibrant concentrations of living things. Organic Residue has very potent medicinal uses. Honey-sweet, this is the only known form of Residue which is edible for non-Mages.
	`A Creature can eat a Plug's worth of Organic Residue as a substitute for an entire day of food and drink. Undead Creatures suffer Major Panic when in contact with it, and Mindless Undead are unable to voluntarily move towards it.`
