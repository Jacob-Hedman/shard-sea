Siege defense might sound somewhat daunting, but this can be mich simpler than we might think. Cities are made of Zones and Structures, some of which have Linkages, Containment, etc. This is exactly the same outline as a Battlefield. So, any City can be a Battlefield. Your own Outpost is a City, so it can also be a Battlefield. One of the only things cosider further is how you get Armies for defense. Simple enough, just like a City does. 

#### Raising Armies
****
When Players Raise an Army to defend their Outpost, its Might is reduced, temporarily, by 3. This temporary penalty is replaced, upon the Return of the Army.
**Lasting Change in Might**

When an Army Returns, roll a Pool of Chaos Dice and, for each 6, the City loses one Might. The size of this Pool depends on the fraction of the Army that Returns.
- All Units Destroyed: `10 Chaos Dice`
- 25% of Units Return: `8 Chaos Dice`
- 50% of Units Return: `6 Chaos Dice`
- 75% of all Units Return: `3 Chaos Dice`
- All Units Return: `1 Chaos Die`
#### A Consideration About Units
****
Units in Battle that a City can raise are sort of generalized abstractions, and more things are possible. For example, the sample Units described with Battles have Commanders and a few other unique Units. These come in when the narrative demands. For example, when a notable Ally is a Friend of Death, include them (assuming that NPC agrees to help.)
