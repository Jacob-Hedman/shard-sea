The missionary effort to reclaim a Shard is monumental. Because the Sea always shifts, because the Arks struggle to chart their courses, and because the Shards are never stable, the Outpost must sustain itself, a mirror in miniature reproducing the form of Ark-dwelling Verdun.
#### The Population of an Outpost
****
	*"Three of the Allmother's Chosen, each with their spouse, twelve from the venerable houses, one of the anointed for each of the Seven, five of the Gifted, sixty of the Makers, and ten from those who traverse the Sea of Shards. Let them be given the Book of Wheels and its Branches, the Radius, and Heart."*

**Character Creation in the Outpost, and Character Established Outposts**
The goal of the Commission is to reclaim a Shard, meaning that the game begins at or near the point of establishing the Outpost. When doing so, count PCs against the total numbers, meaning that Mages will be one of the five, and Expeditioners one of the ten. Priests are uniquely impacted by this, as they will also be the only member of their Priesthood within the Outpost.
#### What this System Means
****
Establishing an Outpost means running one's own City. Building and running a City is handled by the Wheel of Civilization with a caveat, that one can control, spend, and increase the Stats of a City.
#### What an Outpost Needs
****
To build an Outpost, you must find a location with an Aether Node of at least Tier_3.
#### Outpost Characteristics
****
Outposts are Cities, and so they have the three Stats:
- **Size**: the population and expanse off a City.
- **Might**: its martial power.
- **Wealth**: financial might.
Unlike other Cities, represented from the outside, your Outpost is a growing thing under your direction. Consequently, there are three additional Stats to track when managing an Outpost. 

**Outpost Resources**
The Stats used to grow and develop an Outpost are Resources, which can be Taxed, spent, and increased. These are:
- *Growth*: the measure of working citizens as a function of Size.
- *Supply*: food and means of supporting the Outpost.
- *Fuel*: Residual means of feeding the Outpost's machine-works and Artifice.

#### Initial Values and using Resources
****

**The Initial Stats**
Outposts are not large things in their early phase, but the Great Commission provides them with more than a mere village.
****
*Initial Size, Might, and Wealth*: 3
****


**Growth**
	*Initial Value*: 9, thrice Size.
Population and labor, most Cards have a Cost in terms of Growth, representing the manpower needed to obtain and keep them.
*Gaining Numbers*
	Growth is a Resource obtained over time, as the population of the Outpost increases. *For every three Growth gained or lost, the Outpost's Size is increased or decreased by one.*

**Supply**
	*Initial Value*: 10
Food, drink, and housing, Supply represents the Outpost's ability to support its inhabitants and limits its Size, as well as providing one of the only means of increasing Growth.
*Scarcity*
	Supply has the following interactions with Growth:
	- Growth can never exceed Supply.
	- If Supply falls below Growth, Growth will also decrease to match if a Month of time is allowed to pass.
*Abundance*
	Supply Tokens can be spent (as opposed to Taxed), converting them into a Growth Token at the rate of three-to-one. *Spent Supply Tokens are a permanent deduction from the amount the Outpost would otherwise produce.*

**Fuel**
	*Initial Value*: 9
Power, Residue is needed for the elementary functions of an Outpost, as well as a number of its more advanced structures. Fuel is a Resource, and Cards can have a Cost in terms of Fuel, for example, the Outpost's Alembic Heart.
*Sources of Power*
	Fuel is power, literal and financial. For every three Fuel gained or lost, the Outpost's Wealth is increased or decreased by one. Likewise, Fuel can be permanently Spent, exchanging three Fuel to increase its Might by one.
*Financial Gain*
	As Residue is also a currency, meaning that Fuel can be Taxed for payment's sake. This yields 30,000 Plugs per Fuel Taxed.
*Non-Standard Residue*
	The above assumes the Mine is producing only Pure Residue. When a Mine is built on a Node with a distinct Residue Type or Types, Taxing the Mine can result in Residue of these kinds, so long as the total amount of Plugs gained per Fuel is equal to or less than 30k in Pure Residue.

**Managing Resources**
Resources can be played with and modified in a few ways over the course of play. As the Outpost weathers time, its shape and capacities can change.
- *Depletion*: When a resource Token is Depleted, it is removed from play until being Restored, but the total amount is not changed. *All Depleted Tokens are Restored within a Month.*
- *Loss*: A Resource Token can be permanently removed from play, such as when a Structure that produced the Resource is destroyed.
- *Taxation*: This is a means of using a Resource, allowing the Outpost to voluntarily Deplete a number of Tokens for a benefit.
- *Spending*: When Spent, a Token is lost permanently in exchange for some gain. (All spending must be recorded, as it alters the total production of an Outpost.)

**Dedicating Resources Tokens**
Structure Cards that can be built have Costs in terms of Growth, Supply, and Fuel. After creating a Structure, Tokens of the corresponding kinds must be Dedicated to it, meaning they cannot be Taxed, spent, or Dedicated to anything else so long as the Structure remains. 

Consequently, one might need to demolish a Structure to regain its Resources. Likewise, when Resources fall short of the sum Costs of all existing Structures, some must be sacrificed.

**Transit Routes**
Finally, as the outpost grows, it will spread and its network of influence must grow with it. Transit Routes trace back to the Heart of the Outpost itself, and quantify access. 
*Transit in General*
	In general, for the Outpost to gain something from a Structure or anything else in a Hex, a Transit Route must extend there, typically from a Wyvern Roost.
*Continuity*
	Given the methods of transport used by Verdun, Ship, Wyvern, and, eventually, Gateway, Transit Routes do not need to be continuous to function. They do not represent roads, as Verdun are not and were never great builders of roads.

#### Universal Card Play
****
Past this point, the entities that compose an Outpost consist of Cards. The following are concepts and rules that apply to practically all Cards.

**Tier**
The relative strength of a Card in its role.

**Durability**
When Cards are damaged or harmed in some way, they suffer a Notch. When a Card's Notches exceed its Durability, it is destroyed. 
*Shedding Notches*
	Notches can be removed, at the rate of 1/10th the Card's Cost per Notch.

**Cost**
The number of Plugs needed to obtain it. This will also give a scaling price for upgrading that Card, if it can be.

**Resources**
How many Tokens of a given Resource must be dedicated to that Card, usually placed within a Structure.

## Beginning the Outpost
****
After picking a Hex within a Shard with an active Aether Node, you can begin the establishment of an Outpost.

**Layers**
All Outposts have the same nested Regions as any City:
- *Interior*: the innermost and best defended.
- *Corpus*: the body of a City proper.
- *Boundary*: where the furthest line is drawn.
*Defaults*
	When an Outpost is established, a process taking at least one Month, it gains all the elements of a City with the same Stats.
*Locating and Building Structures*
	Structures are bound to the Regions of the Outpost, either dictated by the Structure or chosen in construction.
*Primary Structures*
	Some Structures are exclusive with others. Primary Structures define the Region placed within. Consequently, two Primary Structures cannot occupy the same Region.

**Time Constraints**
Building a Structure takes time, equal to its Tier, Durability, and Resource Costs added up in Weeks. So, a Mine would take four Weeks / a Month to obtain. *You can Deplete a Growth Token to remove one Week from the built time of any Structure, but all Structures take at least one Week to build.*

Expanding the Ward is accomplished by the installation of Aether Pylons, a Magical device powered remotely from the Outpost's Alembic.

**Difference in Outpost and normal City Growth**
Cities normally gain new Zones or have major changes when their Stats do. This is not necessarily the case for an Outpost that Players control. First, all new Zones are at Player discretion, if they happen, and require an in-game Month to take effect in their City.
