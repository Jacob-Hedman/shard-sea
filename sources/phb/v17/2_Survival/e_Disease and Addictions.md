#### Disease
****
Diseases represent corruption of the body by microbial, fungal, or viral life and other invasive forces. Each Disease is unique as are the ways of contracting and curing them. 

In general, a Disease has the following elements:
- **Risk**: which gives an initial roll or procedure for contracting it. Contracting a Disease makes you Infected. 
- **Progression**: when Infected, this quantifies how the Disease worsens.
- **Stages**: a Tiered system (beginning at 0, Infection), that gives symptoms and effects as the Disease progresses.
- **Resolution**: The manner by which the Disease can be Recovered from, if at all.
#### Addictions
****
An Addiction is a dependency on a single substance which is always an Item with the Addictive Quality, such that you cannot adequately function without it. When you fail to consume the Item within its Secondary Duration, you enter Withdrawal and suffer effects depending on the substance. Addictions can be recovered from by suffering withdrawal for an amount of time equal to twice its Tertiary Duration. Each use of the substance while in withdrawal resets the remaining time, not exceeding the total. Finally, you can choose to ingest lower-than-active doses of a substance (which counts as half of a use of the Item,) to avoid the negative effects of withdrawal while contributing half that time to recovery.
