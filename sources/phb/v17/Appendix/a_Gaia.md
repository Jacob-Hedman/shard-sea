Gaia was a beautiful world. Lush plains covered most of the land, punctuated with mountains and hills etched with flowing rivers and streams that fed into the World Sea. As the Verdun, its people, had it, it would have been a perfect place, were it not for the Impure; monstrous beings that came from the Depths to prey on the life above. The nations of Gaia waged battles against them that grew into wars. Led by the greatest representatives of the Verdun, they would eventually uncover the source of these unclean creatures.

A lurking evil, the source of monsters, Abbadon lay in the depths of Gaia itself, spewing forth corruption and malice that eventually emerged as the Impure. Upon their discovery, the Verdun resolved to slay it, to put Abbadon to rest for good and all. The Gifted wrought magic into great weapons of war, the Clerics called down instruments of celestial punishment, and the kings and emperors of Gaia sent both warriors and scholars so-armed into the Depth to do this great work.

Many bands and companies of heroes fought with Abbadon, but it was a few that eventually subdued it. The Company of the Prince eventually emerged from the Depths, hailed with thunderous applause and showered with golden gifts of praise for their deed. All the nations of Gaia were full of wine and exuberance, feasts and indulgence that quickly turned to ash on their tongues.

The scholars soon met with their lords in secret, their studies of the stars had of recent days gone awry. The Gifted and other students of the Aether whispered amongst themselves; the invisible breath from which they drew their strength was growing ever fouler. The Clerics witnessed terrible omens, Celestial emissaries growing cold and cruel in their conduct and speech. No one knew who first spoke of the Doom, but all agreed it would shortly arrive. The slaying of Abbadon had undone something vital within Gaia itself. For it was not only the source of Monsters. Whatever had made Gaia had intended Abbadon as a sort of loadstone, a balance to the radiant, subtle field of Arcane potential that suffused its air. Without this, the whole system would collapse, and soon.

With the time they had, the nations ordered their greatest works ever conceived to be built, artisans and machines to build Arks in the guise of greater cities and temples.  Soon, the first fissure lanced up from the World Sea, boiling with molten rock and transmitting a malignance through the Aether worse than even the mightiest of the Impure's vile sorceries.

More sprouted after, crawling across the whole of Gaia, spurred by the collapse of the Aether itself which laid the Gifted low. Soon the air too grew foul and only the Arks' defenses provided breath. The progression of the fissures, having laid all but the Arks in darkness and chaos, wrapped around Gaia entire, shattering and completing the Doom. The Celestials finally closed it from the Outside, wrapped in a crystal sphere, a world of floating Shards pivoting around Black Iron core of Abbadon's prison, now cold and dead. 

The Verdun remain in their Arks, scavenging the dead Shards that drift about them. Sending Shard Ship Expeditions with the brave few to scour these for remnants of the Aether, now crystalized Residue, Artifacts of the old world, and anything that could preserve their city until the next dawn. Cursing the Company of the Prince, remembered only as the Company of Fools, the Verdun continue to fight. A churning ever-war against the conditions that they themselves created, no matter how innocently; their greatest foe is the legacy of their own decimation.

Our story begins with a newly formed Expedition bound from the Ark Shard, Vinia. What they find, who does so, and their own survival is up to you.
