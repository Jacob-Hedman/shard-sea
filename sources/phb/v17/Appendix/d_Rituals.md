**Mage Ward**
	*Runes*: Shell, Distortion, Force
	*Reagents*: A Loop of Residue, Infused in the Ritual.
	*Time*: 1 Minute.
	Like the Wards that preserve the Arks on a smaller scale. A Mage's Ward offers simple physical insulation from potential harm by erecting a wall of thickened air. Few venture far without the Infused Loop somewhere on their person.
	`After completing the incantation, a sphere of translucent energy encases any holding the Infused Loop. Your Ward functions like a Shield, always active, but lacking any Hardness. It has Durability equal to the Ritual Tier. If your Ward is Broken, the Loop is destroyed.`

**Terra Magna Magia**
	*Runes*: Shell, Void, Star
	*Reagents*: As a Magic Circle.
	*Time*: 10 Minutes.
	A more ostentatious Magical work than a Circle, this enclosure of space designates a single Soul, drawing power from other Mages to feed their own.
	`This is created in the same manner as a Magic Circle, but requires multiple Mages. While the Circle remains unbroken, none of its makers but one may Channel. The beneficiary of this Ritual receives a Bonus to their Core Tier, Degree of Arcane, and to their Intelligence equal to the number of other Mages. In exchange, they also suffer 1 point of Permenant Corruption for each which cannot be removed until the Circle is broken, and are unable to leave the Circle or break it themselves.`

**Crystal Sphere**
	*Runes*: Shell, Distortion, Weight, Density, Star.
	*Reagents*: As a Magic Circle.
	*Time*: 1 Hour.
	Protection for longer periods of time for a larger group than a traditional Ward. Crystal Spheres are common tools for Mage Expeditioners spending a night on a Shard.
	`At the conclusion of the Ritual, an opaque mirrored Sphere of force appears around you with a Radius up to 10 Meters. This will mold to obstructions when formed, and close when they are removed. Otherwise, this is a solid wall with a Hardness up to your Influence, and Durability equal to ten times that amount. This Ritual lasts for 24 Hours, or until you Banish the Sphere.`

**The Door in the Eye**
	*Runes*: Eye, Relativity, Presence
	*Reagents*: A body-length mirror with a Price of at least 300 and Sphere of Temporal Residue, consumed in the Ritual.
	*Time*: 1 Hour.
	The past is inaccessible, time a wall over which none can climb. The Door in the Eye is a way through it.
	`At the conclusion of the Ritual, you step through the mirror, into a shadowy reflection of the space you were, but at any point in the past you wish. This is a quasi real space and can be interacted with, but you cannot move further than your Influence from the mirror. No people appear there, and the chosen moment is inert unless you act (though some physical traces like footporints can be found.) When you step back through the mirror, you return to the instant you left and the mirror shatters.`
	`No Mages has ever been able to venture within 100 Years of the events of the Doom with this or any other method of Temporal Magic.`

**Missile Array**
	*Runes*: Force, Spark
	*Reagents*: A Plug of Residue into which the Ritual is Infused.
	*Time*: 1 Hour.
	Intangible machinery able to focus Aether into bolts of pure energy. Arrays like these are a mainstay of practical Mages. While limited in scope, their strength comes from their reliability.
	`After completing this Ritual, you gain the ability to project held Aether into offensive Missiles as a 1 AP Primary Action. Each Missile requires 1 Aether and automatically hits a Target within your Influence. You may create as many Missiles as you like and choose as many Targets.`
	`Each Target hit with a single Missile suffers 3d6 Fire and Piercing Damage. If the same Target is hit with multiple, each Missile after the first increases this single instance of Damage by 1d6.`
